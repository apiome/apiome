Smithy export is complete: the emitter, its fidelity pack, validation with `smithy validate`, and
the **Smithy** target card, CLI and fixtures.
[Smithy from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#smithy-from-any-source) now opens with an overview of
all of it.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to export to Smithy:** to move a REST, RPC, GraphQL, JSON Schema or WSDL item to a Smithy
  2.0 model with its HTTP bindings, or to round-trip a Smithy item losslessly.
- **At a glance:** one table from the **Smithy** card and its options, through how the item is
  written and what the fidelity report lists, to the `smithy validate` check before every download
  and the CLI.
