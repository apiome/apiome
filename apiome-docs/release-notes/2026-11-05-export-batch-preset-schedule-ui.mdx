Multi-target export, presets and auto-export on publish are now one click away from where you already
export — the **Export** dialog and a version's **Export** panel. See
[The Export dialog](/ship/export-dialog#several-targets-and-presets).

{/* truncate */}

- **Several targets in the dialog:** tick two or more formats, each with this source's fidelity badge,
  and one job builds an archive with a folder per format.
- **Presets in the dialog:** **Apply a preset** selects its formats and options; **Save as preset** keeps
  what you picked.
- **Export all versions…:** on a version's **Export** panel, export every version of the project to one
  or more formats in one job.
- **Auto-export on publish beside your recent exports:** the version's **Export** panel lists the presets
  every publish of the project runs, with their halted and delivered runs, and adds or releases them.
