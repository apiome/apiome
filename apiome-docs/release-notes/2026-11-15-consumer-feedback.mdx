The people who use your APIs can now tell you when a response shape is wrong or a description is
unclear, without an email to support.

{/* truncate */}

- **Send feedback from Browse.** A signed-in reader opens a published version, **clicks the
  "Feedback" tab**, picks **Whole API** or one operation, and **clicks "Send feedback"**.
- **Answer it where you already discuss.** The thread appears in the project's **Discussion** tab with
  a **Consumer** tag. **Consumer feedback** in the new **Origin** chips lists only those threads.
  Reply and resolve as you do with any thread.
- **The reader sees your answer.** Replies show on the reader's **Feedback** tab marked **Team**, and
  either side can **Resolve** or **Reopen**.
- **Private by design.** A reader sees only their own threads, never your internal discussion, and
  their `@name` text pings nobody. Signed-out visitors can't send feedback.
- **For integrators.** The new `portal-feedback` endpoints are in the
  [REST reference](/reference/rest/portal-feedback), and `?origin=consumer` filters the thread list.

See [Send feedback on an API](/ship/send-feedback-on-an-api) and
[Consumer feedback](/build/versions#consumer-feedback).
