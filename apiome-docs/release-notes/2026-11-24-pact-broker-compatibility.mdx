"Change the broker base URL and token, and your Pact tooling works" is now something CI checks.
A conformance suite runs the official `pact-broker` CLI, pact-js and pact-jvm against Apiome, and
a new page lists every Pact Broker endpoint and whether Apiome serves it.

{/* truncate */}

**What the suite runs**, on a fresh Apiome, for every pull request that touches the Pact Broker:

- **The `pact-broker` CLI**: `publish`, the Ruby verifier publishing results, `create-environment`,
  `record-deployment`, `can-i-deploy` (yes, then no for an unverified version), tags, releases and
  undeployment.
- **pact-js**: a consumer test, then the `Verifier` fetching pacts with selectors and publishing
  its result.
- **pact-jvm**: a consumer test, the Gradle plugin's `pactPublish`, then a JUnit 5 provider test.

The HAL documents the clients read are compared with checked-in golden transcripts, so a change
to what a Pact client sees shows up in review.

**Fixed along the way.** The suite found three places where Apiome behaved differently from the
Pact Broker, and they now match:

- **Main branch detection.** The first version published on `develop`, `main` or `master` sets the
  pacticipant's main branch, as on the Pact Broker. `{"mainBranch": true}` selectors now work
  without setting it by hand.
- **The Ruby verifier's results.** Results from `pact-provider-verifier` (the `pact-cli` image) were
  refused because they send `testResults` as an object. They are now accepted.
- **`GET …/for-verification`.** The Rust verifier inside pact-js, pact-python and pact-go reads this
  resource with `GET` before it posts its selectors. Apiome answered `405`. It now answers both forms.

**Endpoints Apiome does not serve** answer `501 Not Implemented`, never a bare `404`. The answer
names the endpoint, its status, the methods that do work, the tracking issue and the Apiome route
to use instead, and links the [compatibility page](/ship/pact-broker/compatibility).

**For operators.** No migration. Run the suite yourself with `scripts/pact_conformance/run.sh`
(Docker and `uv`).

Read [Pact Broker compatibility](/ship/pact-broker/compatibility) for the full endpoint table.
