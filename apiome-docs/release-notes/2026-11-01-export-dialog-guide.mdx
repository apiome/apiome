A new page, [The Export dialog](/ship/export-dialog), brings together everything the in-place
**Export** dialog does: where to open it, its four steps and what each one asks of you, and how it
hands over to the Export studio.

{/* truncate */}

- **Where to start.** A version's **Actions** menu (**Export to another format…**), a catalog item's
  **Export** button or menu row, and a version's **View spec**. Export is always an action on the
  version or item you are looking at, never a side-navigation entry.
- **Four steps.** **Source → Target → Fidelity → Export**: the fidelity badge on every card, the
  report and acknowledgement for a lossy conversion, and the preview and download.
- **The version's Export panel.** Under **View spec**, **Best-fidelity targets** and **Lossy
  targets** chips open the studio on that format, and **Recent exports** re-runs an earlier export
  with its options. Its screenshot is on the page.
- **Round trip.** Where to see what reading the exported file back would change.
