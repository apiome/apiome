**Export studio** is now released: it sits in the rail's **Ship** section for every member, and the
places that hand an export to it show their studio actions — a catalog item's **⋯** → **Export to
another format…**, the Catalog's bulk **Export…**, the Export dialog's **Open in Export Studio**, a
version's export cards and the project facts card. See [Export studio](/ship/export-studio).
