**Contracts** → **Settings** gains its last four tabs: **Secrets**, **CI and commit statuses**,
**Verification schedule** and **Agents**. Bi-directional verdicts can now set commit statuses and fire
webhooks, migrated webhooks wait for their secrets instead of losing them, and a workspace decides what
an AI agent may do with its contracts. See [Manage secrets in the console](/ship/pact-broker/secrets#manage-secrets-in-the-console),
[Choose which statuses are written](/ship/pact-broker/commit-statuses#choose-which-statuses-are-written)
and [Choose what agents may do](/ship/pact-broker/cli#choose-what-agents-may-do).

{/* truncate */}

- **Secrets.** Every workspace secret with what uses it — write-only, a value is never shown again —
  with **New secret**, **Replace value** and **Delete** (blocked while anything uses it, with the list
  of what does).
- **Secrets waiting for a value.** `apiome pact migrate` keeps a credential the source redacted as a
  secret marked **No value** and imports its webhook turned off; **Set value** turns the webhook on.
- **Commit statuses.** Switch statuses on the consumer's and on the provider's commit; **For
  bi-directional verdicts too** gives spec comparisons statuses and `provider_verification_*`
  webhooks, as a verification has. A status a switch keeps off is recorded with the reason.
- **Pipeline recipes and the broker address.** Working pipelines for GitHub Actions, GitLab CI,
  Jenkins and Azure Pipelines — the tested files from the CI guide — and your `PACT_BROKER_BASE_URL`.
- **Verification schedule.** What Apiome verifies on its own, with the verification settings below it.
- **Agents.** Reading pacts, verdicts and can-i-deploy is on; recording deployments is **off until you
  allow it**, and never to production. A refused agent action says why and is audited.
- **REST:** `GET /v1/tenants/{t}/pact-contract-settings`, `PUT …/commit-statuses` and `PUT …/agents`;
  secrets carry `has_value` and `origin` (OpenAPI 1.707.0).
