Teams that live in Jira and Confluence can now sign in to Apiome with their Atlassian account, and
link it beside GitHub, GitLab or any other provider on
[Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Atlassian** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs a client ID and a client secret from an OAuth 2.0 integration in the Atlassian developer
  console — no issuer. [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side)
  lists what to set on Atlassian's side. The environment variables are `ATLASSIAN_CLIENT_ID` and
  `ATLASSIAN_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/atlassian`.
- **Email addresses.** Apiome joins an Atlassian sign-in to an existing account only when Atlassian
  reports the address as verified. Otherwise, sign in another way first and link Atlassian from
  **Linked accounts**.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `atlassian` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/atlassian` stores its credentials.
