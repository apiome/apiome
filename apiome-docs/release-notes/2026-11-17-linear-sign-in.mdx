People can now link their Linear account on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) and sign
in to Apiome with it from then on.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Linear** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the **Client ID** and **Client secret** of a Linear OAuth application — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Linear's side. The environment variables are `LINEAR_CLIENT_ID` and `LINEAR_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/linear`, added under the
  application's **Callback URLs**.
- **Scope.** Apiome asks for `read`, the narrowest scope Linear offers, and uses it only to read
  the name, email address and picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no issues, projects
  or teams.
- **Link first.** Linear does not say whether an email address is verified, so Apiome never uses
  a Linear address to find or create an account. People sign in another way, link Linear from
  **Linked accounts**, and can sign in with Linear after that. Signing up with Linear, or signing
  in with it before linking, ends with *Your sign-in provider could not confirm that your email
  address is verified…* — even when the address matches an existing account.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `linear` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/linear` stores its credentials.
