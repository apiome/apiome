ASN.1 export is complete: the module emitter, its fidelity pack, validation with `asn1tools`, and
the **ASN.1** target card, CLI and fixtures.
[ASN.1 from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#asn1-from-any-source) now opens with an overview of
all of it.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to export to ASN.1:** to hand an item's data types to a telecom, LDAP, PKI or aviation tool
  chain as one X.680 module, with lengths, bounds and simple patterns kept as subtype constraints, or
  to round-trip an ASN.1 item losslessly.
- **At a glance:** one table from the **ASN.1** card and its options, through how the item and its
  constraints are written and what the fidelity report lists, to the `asn1tools` check before every
  download and the CLI.
