Comparing two versions of a FlatBuffers or Cap'n Proto catalog item now follows the format's own
field numbers, and both formats get a lint pack.
[How versions are compared](/bring-in/flatbuffers-capnproto-import#breaking-changes-between-versions).

{/* truncate */}

- **FlatBuffers.** A table field is identified by its id. A renamed field is dangerous rather than a
  removal, a `(deprecated)` field is safe, and a field inserted before others, a reused id, a changed
  default or any change to a `struct` is reported for what it does to buffers already written.
- **Cap'n Proto.** Members are identified by their `@N` ordinal: renaming a field or method is safe,
  while removing or renumbering one is breaking.
- **Lint.** The `flatbuffers` and `capnproto` packs catch what `flatc` and the Cap'n Proto compiler
  refuse: duplicate ids or ordinals, mixed explicit ids, gaps in ordinals and a missing file id.
- **A parser fix.** A Cap'n Proto method returning a type such as `List(Person)` is now read; it was
  dropped before.
