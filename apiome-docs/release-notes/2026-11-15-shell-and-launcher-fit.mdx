The sidebar now fits a 1440 × 900 window without scrolling. The launcher shows only what you can
open. One new page maps where everything lives: [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around). (#5595)

{/* truncate */}

- **Folded groups with counts.** **Workspace** starts folded. A folded heading shows how many pages
  it holds, and the sidebar remembers your choice. (#5608)
- **Three pages moved off the sidebar.** **Tenants** is **“Manage workspaces…”** in the workspace
  switcher. **Data browser** and **Migrations** are in the user menu. **SDK settings** is the
  **SDK & branding** tab of **Export studio**. The command palette still finds all of them. (#5609)
- **Notifications leads the footer** and counts what is unread. In the icon rail the count becomes
  a dot. (#5610)
- **Preview is a dot, not a pill.** **Lint posture** shows a small honey dot. Hover it to read
  *Preview*. (#5611)
- **The launcher shows only cards that open.** Products that are not ready yet are listed on one
  **Coming next** line. (#5612)
- **Recently shipped** on the launcher shows the three newest What's new entries. The build string
  moved to the footer. (#5613)
