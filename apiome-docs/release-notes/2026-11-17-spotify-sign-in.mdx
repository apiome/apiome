People can now link their Spotify account on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) and sign
in to Apiome with it from then on.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Spotify** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It needs
  the Client ID and Client secret of an app created in the Spotify Developer Dashboard with the
  **Web API** — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Spotify's side. The environment variables are `SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID` and `SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/spotify`, added under the app's
  **Redirect URIs**.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **user-read-email** only and uses it to read the name, email address
  and picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no playlists, library, listening history or
  playback and keeps no Spotify token.
- **Link first.** Spotify does not verify the email address it shares, so Apiome never uses it to
  find or create an account. People sign in another way, link Spotify from **Linked accounts**, and
  can sign in with Spotify after that. Before linking, a Spotify sign-in ends with *Your sign-in
  provider could not confirm that your email address is verified…*.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `spotify` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/spotify` stores its credentials.
