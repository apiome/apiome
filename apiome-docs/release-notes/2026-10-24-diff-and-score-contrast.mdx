The side-by-side diff on a catalog item's **Versions** tab is easier to read, with a screen reader
and in the dark theme. Scores in the **Excellent** band also meet contrast.

{/* truncate */}

- **Screen readers** announce the two sides of a version diff as **Before** and **After**. Until now,
  neither side had a name.
- **In the dark theme,** added and removed lines get a fainter highlight, so the code on them keeps
  enough contrast to read.
- **Excellent scores** (90–100) use the theme's success colour instead of a fixed green. The fixed
  green fell below the contrast minimum on the light background.
