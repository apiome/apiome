A Smithy catalog item now keeps what its traits say, not only their names: each operation's HTTP
route and parameters, its errors, the service's resources and every member's documentation and
constraints. [What the catalog item holds](/bring-in/smithy-import#what-the-catalog-item-holds).

{/* truncate */}

- **HTTP bindings.** `@http` gives each operation its method and path, and `@httpLabel`,
  `@httpQuery` and `@httpHeader` members become its parameters. A model read from its JSON AST now
  converts to OpenAPI with its own routes.
- **Errors.** An operation's errors, and its service's, are error messages with their `@httpError`
  status.
- **Resources.** A resource keeps its identifiers, properties, lifecycle and children, and each
  operation it binds is tagged with its name.
- **Traits.** Member documentation, defaults and `@length` / `@range` / `@pattern` constraints are
  kept, a member inherits the constraints of the shape it targets, and enums keep their
  `@enumValue`s.
- **Per-shape hashes.** Each shape is hashed on its own, so you can see which shapes changed.
