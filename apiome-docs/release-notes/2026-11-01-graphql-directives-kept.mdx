title: GraphQL schemas keep their directives when imported as a project
description: Every applied directive now comes through as x-graphql-directive on the class.
authors: [apiome]
---

Importing a GraphQL schema as a project now keeps every directive the schema applies.
[See how GraphQL schemas import](/bring-in/import-wizard#graphql-schemas).

{/* truncate */}

- **Types, fields and enum values.** `@deprecated`, `@key(fields: "id")` and your own directives are
  kept with their arguments — on the class and on each property as `x-graphql-directive`, and per enum
  value under `x-graphql-enum-value-directives`.
- **Unions with a directive.** A union such as `union Result @key(fields: "id") = A | B` now keeps its
  member types too.
