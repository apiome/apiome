The EDI exports are complete: the X12 implementation guide, the UN/EDIFACT message guide on the same plan, their
fidelity report and checks, the target cards, CLI and fixtures, and the UBL / Peppol BIS profiles of the XSD
export. [EDI and e-invoicing guides](/ship/export-a-spec#edi-and-e-invoicing-guides) brings them together.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to use which:** X12 for North American partners, UN/EDIFACT for international ones, UBL and Peppol for
  e-invoicing and XML business documents.
- **At a glance:** the three side by side — the card, the files you get, how you pick the document, the units
  each is written in, what checks it, and the CLI command.
