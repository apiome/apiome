People can now link their Gumroad account on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) and sign
in to Apiome with it from then on.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Gumroad** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It needs
  the **Application ID** and **Application Secret** of an application created under **Settings →
  Advanced → Applications** on Gumroad — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Gumroad's side. The environment variables are `GUMROAD_CLIENT_ID` and `GUMROAD_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/gumroad`, set as the application's
  **Redirect URI**.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **view_profile** only and uses it to read the name, email address and
  profile picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no products, sales, subscribers or payouts
  and keeps no Gumroad token.
- **Link first.** Gumroad does not say whether the email address it shares is verified, so Apiome
  never uses it to find or create an account. People sign in another way, link Gumroad from
  **Linked accounts**, and can sign in with Gumroad after that. Before linking, a Gumroad sign-in
  ends with *Your sign-in provider could not confirm that your email address is verified…*.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `gumroad` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/gumroad` stores its credentials.
