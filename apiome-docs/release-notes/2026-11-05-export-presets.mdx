Exports you run again and again — a nightly sync, a release export — can now be saved as a **preset**:
the targets, each target's options and a delivery profile, applied by name in Export studio, the CLI and
the API. See [Save an export as a preset](/ship/export-presets).

{/* truncate */}

- **Save as preset** on an export's **Review** step keeps its targets, the options you changed and the
  ticked delivery profile.
- **Apply a preset** on the **Target** step selects its targets and options, and ticks its delivery.
- **The Presets tab** of Export studio lists, edits and deletes presets.
- **From the CLI:** `apiome export presets create|list|show|update|delete`, and
  `apiome export --preset nightly-sync ARTIFACT --out PATH`.
- **From the API:** `/v1/export/{tenant}/presets`, and `"preset"` on `POST /v1/export/{tenant}/jobs`.
- **Checked every time:** each target's options are validated against its emitter when the preset is saved
  and again when it is applied.
