**Email me about drift too** in **Drift alerts** now sends email: drift a scheduled live refresh
finds reaches the item's owner team by email as well as by webhook, inbox and chat.
[How it works](/bring-in/catalog-refresh#drift-email).

{/* truncate */}

- **Who is emailed.** Each member of the item's confirmed owner team, at the address on their
  account — the people who get the inbox notification. Nobody is emailed about an item with no
  owner, or about a version added with **Check now**.
- **When.** With **Right away**, one email per drift, with the grade, the two versions, the top
  findings and a **See what changed** link. With **Daily digest**, one email per member per digest,
  listing only their team's items.
- **For operators.** The REST API sends with SendGrid, like email sign-up: set `SENDGRID_API_KEY`
  and `EMAIL_FROM` for apiome-rest ([Email for notifications](/admin/operating-apiome#email-for-notifications)).
  Without them nothing is emailed and nothing fails.
