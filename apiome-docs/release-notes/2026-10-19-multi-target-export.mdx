Publishing a version usually means shipping it in more than one format. An export job can now take
a list of formats, so one job does what used to take a job per format.

{/* truncate */}

- **One job, one zip.** Send `targets` instead of `target` to `POST /v1/export/{tenant}/jobs`. The
  download is one zip with a folder per format and a root `manifest.json` that lists them all.
- **Fidelity per format, plus a rollup.** The job result carries each format's full fidelity
  report, guard, validation and delivery decision, and a rollup with the worst tier and the lowest
  and average preserved share.
- **All or nothing.** A format that needs confirmation stops the job before anything is generated.
  A format that fails stops the job and is named in the error, and no partial zip is kept.

See [Export to several formats in one job](/ship/export-a-spec#export-to-several-formats-in-one-job).
