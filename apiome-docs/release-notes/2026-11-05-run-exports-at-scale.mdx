Export studio now covers the operations side of exporting, end to end. The new
[Run exports at scale](/ship/run-exports-at-scale) page shows where each part lives.

{/* truncate */}

- **Job progress and recovery:** follow each stage, cancel, retry, or fix a failed job in Verify.
- **History and audit:** the **History** tab lists every export in the workspace for 90 days, with who
  ran it, how much it lost and where it was delivered; **Re-run in Studio** repeats one.
- **Delivery:** the **Deliver** section sends the result to git, a webhook, a bucket or a schema registry,
  and shows what each delivery left behind.
- **Presets and auto-export:** a preset keeps every delivery profile, and **Auto-export on publish** runs
  it on each publish, from Review.
- **States and telemetry:** every step says what's happening when there's nothing to show or something
  failed, and the studio records a few anonymous usage events.
