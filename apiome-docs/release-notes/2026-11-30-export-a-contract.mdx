A new **Export** page takes a contract out of Apiome — the screen for
`apiome pact export-consumer-contract` and `apiome pact export-postman`, and a way to download any
published pact. See [Export a contract](/ship/pact-broker/export-contracts).

{/* truncate */}

- **A pact from a project.** Derive a consumer's declared surface as a Pact v4 file. The operation
  checklist says where each operation's examples came from — the spec's own, or *Generated from the
  schema — review* — and why an operation was *Left out*; every finding is listed with its reason.
  Untick operations before **Download pact**, or **Review and publish** on the Publish page.
- **A Postman collection.** Replay a stored pact (the latest, on a branch, with a tag, a consumer
  version or a pact version SHA), **every consumer** of a provider at once (one folder each) or an
  uploaded pact file, with a `{{baseUrl}}` variable and **provider-state setup as pre-request
  scripts**: each request first POSTs its states to `{{stateChangeUrl}}`, as the Pact verifier does.
  A bar shows how many interactions the collection keeps; what it leaves out is listed with the
  reason.
- **A pact file.** Download any published pact exactly as stored, or every pact for a provider as a
  zip with a README naming any consumer left out.
- **Linked where you need it.** **Export** on a pact's page and a pacticipant's page, and **Export a
  contract** in Export studio. Large outputs download without an inline preview.
- **CLI:** `apiome pact export-postman --all-consumers`, `--state-setup` and `--state-change-url`.
  With this page every `apiome pact` command has a screen.
- **REST:** the derived pact answers `operations` (each operation's interactions and finding
  counts); `POST /v1/tenants/{t}/pact-postman-collections` takes `allConsumers`, `stateSetup` and
  `stateChangeUrl` (OpenAPI 1.710.0).
