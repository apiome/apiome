**Import to catalog** recognized an OpenAPI or Swagger source and routed it to **Projects**, but
then left **Continue** disabled. You had to open Projects and paste or upload the same source again.

{/* truncate */}

## What changed

- **One click to Projects.** For a source you pasted, uploaded or fetched from a URL, the **Detect &
  route** step's button now reads **“Continue in Projects”**. Click it and the Projects importer
  opens on **Analyze** with the source already loaded.
- **Finish where you are.** **Continue** to **Preview**, then **click “Import →”** to create the
  project. You stay on the Catalog page the whole time.
- **Back keeps the source.** **Back** from **Analyze** opens the **Clipboard**, **File Upload** or
  **URL Import** intake you started from, with the source still loaded. A URL source keeps its
  auto-refresh.

A **Git Repository** selection or an archive still links to **Open Projects**, because there is
no single document to hand over.

See [An OpenAPI source in Import to catalog](/bring-in/import-wizard#an-openapi-source-in-import-to-catalog).
