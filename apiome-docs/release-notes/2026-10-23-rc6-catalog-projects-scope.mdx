RC6 is scoped to the [Catalog](/bring-in/catalog), [Projects](/build/projects) and signing up and in.
Everything else waits for the next release, so a few more screens are held back:
**Connectors**, **Shadow APIs**, the **Dependency graph**, **Teams** and ownership, the **Schema
registry**, the agent, model and component kinds in the Catalog with their Backstage and Compass
import reports, and a project's **Consumers**. They no longer appear in the navigation, and opening
one of their addresses shows a **Not available yet** page.

{/* truncate */}

- **Catalog and Projects lose the panels that belong to them.** The **Owner** column, filter and
  **Assign owner** leave Projects, Catalog and Published; the **Dependencies** tab leaves Versions
  and catalog items; the **Subjects** tab and **Registry** chip leave the Catalog; **Recent runs**
  leaves Repositories; **Compass export** leaves the import wizard; the **Facts** checklist leaves
  a catalog item's Overview; and the **Ownership** section leaves the tenant manage drawer.
- **Their guide pages are withdrawn** until each feature ships, and come back unchanged with it.
  Pages that linked to them now link to the REST reference.
- **The APIs are unchanged.** The REST, CLI and MCP references still document connectors, teams,
  owners, the graph, the schema registry and consumer contracts.
