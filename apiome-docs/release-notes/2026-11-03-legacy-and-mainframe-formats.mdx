The legacy, mainframe and middleware formats are complete: COBOL copybooks, the z/OS Connect
binding, WADL, CORBA IDL, ASN.1, XML-RPC and ONC RPC. A new guide,
[Legacy, mainframe and middleware formats](/bring-in/legacy-and-mainframe-formats), brings them
together.

{/* truncate */}

- **What you can import:** one table of every format, what it is, its importer and its own page.
- **How it works:** importing, what lands in the catalog, comparing versions the way each wire sees
  them (by byte position, by tag, by name and position), and each format's lint rules.
- **Link and move:** linking a z/OS Connect API to its copybooks, and the way forward from each
  format to OpenAPI, OpenRPC, gRPC or JSON Schema.
