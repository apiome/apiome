**Primitives & types** is released: it is back in the rail's **Build** section, in the command
palette and the `G` shortcuts, and its pages — the registry, each type's page, **Namespaces &
scopes**, the **Resolver** and **Settings** — open for every member instead of showing **Not
available yet**. See [Primitives and types](/build/primitives-and-types).

{/* truncate */}

- **Its guide is back.** [Primitives and types](/build/primitives-and-types) returns with its
  twelve screenshots, and [Build](/build) links to it again.
- **New since it was withdrawn.** [Import types](/build/primitives-and-types#import-types) from
  JSON Schema, type-definition bundles and OpenAPI components;
  [see what uses a type](/build/primitives-and-types#see-what-uses-a-type);
  [deprecate a type](/build/primitives-and-types#deprecate-a-type) with a sunset date; and mark a
  namespace's [version root](/build/primitives-and-types#version-roots-stable-and-draft) stable or draft.
- **The screenshots show it.** The shell, the menus, Preferences, the shortcut sheet and the command
  palette are recaptured: their sidebar now lists **Primitives & types** under **Build**.
- **No flag is needed.** The screen is no longer behind the preview setting, and the optional
  `primitives-registry` entitlement gate stays off unless an operator turns it on.
