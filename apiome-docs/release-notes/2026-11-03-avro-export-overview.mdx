Avro export is complete: the emitter, its fidelity pack, Schema Registry subjects and evolution
defaults, validation and the push to a registry, and the **Apache Avro** target card, CLI and
fixtures. A new section,
[Apache Avro from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#apache-avro-from-any-source), brings it together.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to export to Avro:** to give Kafka producers and consumers the schemas of an API's data, one
  `.avsc` per type, and to register them on a Confluent-compatible schema registry.
- **How it is written:** records, enums, unions and maps; nullable fields as `["null", T]`; dates,
  timestamps, UUIDs and decimals as logical types; a folder per namespace.
- **Subjects:** `record_name`, `topic_name` or `topic_record_name`, with a `-value` or `-key` suffix.
- **Fidelity:** operations and validation rules are dropped, and a `null` default is synthesized for
  each nullable field, because schema evolution needs one.
