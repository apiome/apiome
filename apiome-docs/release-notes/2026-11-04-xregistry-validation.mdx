xRegistry exports now say plainly what an event registry cannot hold, and every document is checked
against the official xRegistry schema before you can download it. See
[xRegistry from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#what-the-xregistry-fidelity-report-lists).

{/* truncate */}

- **A critical warning for operations:** exporting a REST, RPC or GraphQL item to **xRegistry** starts
  the fidelity report with a critical warning — only its types and events are exported — and asks you
  to confirm. Export to OpenAPI or AsyncAPI as well to keep the operations.
- **No confirmation for event APIs:** an AsyncAPI, CloudEvents or xRegistry item exported to
  **xRegistry** or **CloudEvents** no longer asks you to confirm a "severe" conversion.
- **Checked against the specification:** every document validates against the official xRegistry
  CloudEvents registry schema and must import back with all its schemas and messages; one that fails
  is never delivered.
