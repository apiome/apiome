The navigation rail no longer scrolls on a 1440 × 900 laptop screen. The **Workspace** group starts
folded, and its heading shows how many pages it holds.
[The sidebar](/getting-started/launcher-and-home#home).

{/* truncate */}

- **One click away.** **Click “Workspace”** to reach **Members**, **Roles**, **Teams**, **API keys**
  and **Schema registry**. **Build**, **Bring in**, **Ship** and **Govern** stay open.
- **Every folded group counts its pages.** Fold any group and its heading shows the number of pages
  inside it. The number hides when the group opens, and in the icon-only rail.
- **Your choice wins.** The rail remembers what you fold and open on this device. If you had already
  folded or opened a group before this release, your rail stays as you left it.
