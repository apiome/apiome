**FlatBuffers** and **Cap'n Proto** exports now say exactly what they change.
[FlatBuffers](/ship/export-a-spec#flatbuffers-from-any-source) ·
[Cap'n Proto](/ship/export-a-spec#capn-proto-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **Constraints are kept as comments.** A field's constraints (`>= 1`, `max length 12`, a pattern)
  are written as a comment above it. The report says nothing checks them.
- **Every change is in the report**, on the field, type or operation it concerns:
  - maps written as key/value entries;
  - FlatBuffers field ids, and Cap'n Proto ordinals and file ids, that Apiome assigned;
  - Cap'n Proto interface methods that lose their HTTP method and path;
  - operations FlatBuffers leaves out.
- **Compile errors point at the line.** An export the compiler rejects fails with each error's file,
  line and column.
