The people who read your APIs can now sign in to your Browse portal without becoming members of
your workspace or getting an API key.

{/* truncate */}

- **Sign in with an email link.** On an organization's Browse page, **click “Sign in”**, enter an
  email address and open the link that arrives. It works once and expires in 15 minutes.
- **Nothing to manage.** A reader is a person with no workspace access. Their first sign-in records
  them as a reader of that organization, and a later sign-in reuses the same account.
- **Safe by default.** Opening the link only asks you to **click “Continue”**, so a mail scanner that
  pre-opens links cannot use it up. An expired or used link says so and offers a new one. Requests
  are rate limited, and the page never reveals whether an address has an account.
- **Audited.** Enrolment, sign-in and sign-out are recorded in the [access audit](/govern/access-audit).
- **For integrators.** The new `portal-auth` endpoints are in the
  [REST reference](/reference/rest/portal-auth). A reader session is accepted only by the portal and
  Browse endpoints.

Signed-in readers still see public versions only. Private versions for readers come next (PCX-1.3).
See [Portal readers](/ship/portal-readers).
