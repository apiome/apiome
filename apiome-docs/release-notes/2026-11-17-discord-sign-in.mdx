People can now sign in to Apiome with their Discord account, and link Discord beside any other
provider on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Discord** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** of a Discord application — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Discord's side. The environment variables are `DISCORD_CLIENT_ID` and `DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/discord`, added under
  **Redirects** on the application's **OAuth2** page.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **`identify`** and **`email`** at sign-in. Nothing needs selecting
  in the Developer Portal, and no bot is needed.
- **Email addresses.** Discord is a **Trusted** provider: a sign-in joins the account with the
  same address, or starts a new one, only when the Discord account's email address is verified.
  An unverified address is refused. To use such an account, verify the address in Discord, or
  sign in another way first and link Discord from **Linked accounts**.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `discord` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/discord` stores its credentials.
