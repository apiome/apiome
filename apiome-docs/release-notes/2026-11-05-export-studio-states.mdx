Every step of Export studio now explains itself when there's nothing to show yet or something didn't
load, and points to the way forward. See
[When something is missing or fails](/ship/export-studio#when-something-is-missing-or-fails).

{/* truncate */}

- **Target:** *Loading export targets…*, *No export targets for this source*, *Choose a target to
  continue*, and **Retry** when the targets couldn't be loaded.
- **When export targets can't be reached:** the studio and the Export dialog keep the list this source
  loaded earlier, say how old it is and that its fidelity may be out of date, and offer **Retry**.
- **Verify, Review, Test drive and pictures:** no more blank panels. A file that couldn't be opened gets
  **Try again**; a test-drive tab or a picture with nothing to show says so.
- **History:** an opened run whose reports couldn't load has its own **Try again**.
- **Usage telemetry:** Export studio counts a few anonymous events — the target chosen, verify verdicts,
  the lossy acknowledgement, test drive and pictures — never names, sources, files or option values.
  See [What Export studio records](/ship/export-studio#what-export-studio-records).
