The public catalog had a look of its own: cooler greys, a different blue, its own dark palette and a
dozen text sizes. It now draws from the same design tokens as the Control Panel, so moving from the
catalog into the app no longer feels like changing products.
[Browse published specs](/ship/browse-published-specs).

{/* truncate */}

- **One palette.** Warm paper surfaces in light, navy-black in dark, and the same blue for links,
  buttons and the current tab. Dark still follows your system setting or the theme switch.
- **One set of text sizes.** Every label, caption and heading sits on the app's type scale; nothing
  is smaller than 11 px.
- **One field style.** Search boxes, filters, sort menus and the **Try it** inputs are the same
  field: 36 px tall, or 30 px in toolbars and dense panels, with the same focus ring as the app.
- **Status colours mean the same thing.** Green for success and lossless, amber for warnings, red
  for breaking changes and errors, in the catalog as in the app.

HTTP method chips and the code-viewer colour themes keep their own colours.
