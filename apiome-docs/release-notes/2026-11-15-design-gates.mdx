The quiet pass made `apiome-ui` calmer. Five tests now keep it that way, and one page maps them:
[Design gates](/reference/design-gates). (#5596)

{/* truncate */}

- **Honey only in brand moments.** A honey token or utility outside the DESIGN.md §2 brand roles
  fails `tests/hive-design-tokens.test.ts`. Saved searches on **MCP servers** are a neutral chip,
  and feature-flag markers are outline badges. (#5614)
- **Header action cap, one banner, no dead controls.** A `PageHeader` holds one primary, two
  secondaries and a ⋯ menu; a page shows one banner under its header; "Coming soon" is never a
  button. `tests/page-header-gate.test.tsx` checks every rendered page.
  [More](/reference/content-voice#what-enforces-it) (#5615)
- **First-row budget.** The visual-parity harness measures where the first row of data starts at
  1440 × 900 and fails a page that is over its budget.
  [More](/reference/visual-parity#the-first-row-budget) (#5616)
- **Design docs as shipped.** DESIGN.md and the page mockups now describe the quiet pass as adopted.
  [More](/release-notes/quiet-pass-design-docs) (#5617)
