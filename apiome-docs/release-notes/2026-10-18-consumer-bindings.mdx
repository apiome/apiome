The [usage index](/govern/runtime-usage#attribute-traffic-to-consumers) can now attribute live
traffic to the consumers in your [registry](/reference/rest/consumer-contracts), without ever storing the
headers, keys or claims it recognises them by.

{/* truncate */}

- **Bindings.** On a project's **Consumers**, open **Bindings and traffic** and bind a header, an
  API key, a JWT claim or a user-agent pattern to a consumer. A value is hashed before it is stored
  and is never shown again, not even as a hash.
- **Nothing is dropped.** A call no binding recognises is counted under the reserved consumer
  **anonymous**. Deleting a binding stops it matching from the next batch, and what it already
  attributed stays attributed.
- **Declared vs observed.** For each consumer, see the operations it declares and calls, the ones it
  declares but never calls, and the ones it calls without declaring. **“Add to contract”** stores
  the next contract revision with that operation added.
- **REST.** `GET|POST …/consumers/{c}/bindings`, `DELETE …/bindings/{id}` and
  `GET …/consumers/{c}/runtime`, under the existing `consumer_contracts` permissions. Each consumer
  list row carries its counts in `runtime`.
