People can now sign in to Apiome with their Vercel account, and link Vercel beside any other
provider on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Vercel** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the **Client ID** and client secret of an **App** created under your Vercel team's
  **Settings → Apps** — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Vercel's side. The environment variables are `VERCEL_CLIENT_ID` and `VERCEL_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/vercel`, added under
  **Authorization Callback URLs** on the app's **Manage** page.
- **Scopes.** The app needs **openid**, **email** and **profile** only, enabled on its
  **Permissions** page. Apiome uses them to read the name, email address and picture of the person
  signing in. Apiome reads no projects, deployments or teams and keeps no Vercel token.
- **Who can sign in.** Anyone with a Vercel account, unless you set the app's **Sign-In Access** to
  **Members of the team**.
- **Email addresses.** Vercel is a **Trusted** provider: a sign-in joins the account with the same
  address, or starts a new one, only when Vercel reports the email address as verified. An
  unverified address is refused. To use such an account, verify the address in Vercel, or sign in
  another way first and link Vercel from **Linked accounts**.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `vercel` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/vercel` stores its credentials.
