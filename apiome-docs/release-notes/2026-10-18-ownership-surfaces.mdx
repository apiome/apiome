Ownership now shows up where you look at your APIs: every list says which [team](/reference/rest/teams)
owns each entity, and **Workspace → Teams** gives each team a page of its own.

{/* truncate */}

- **An Owner column** on Catalog, Projects, MCP servers, Published and Consumers. A *suggested*
  owner says where it came from — a gateway tag, CODEOWNERS, the spec — and **Confirm** makes it the
  owner in one click.
- **Filter by team** on every list; the filter is part of the address, so a team's view can be
  shared.
- **Assign owner** in the Catalog and Projects bulk bars gives many items the same owner at once.
- **Team pages** list the members, what the team owns by kind, your open findings for it and the
  last 30 days of findings it received.
- **`apiome teams`** lists, shows, creates and staffs teams and assigns owners from the command line.
