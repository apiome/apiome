The [dependency graph](/reference/rest/graph) now has screens.

{/* truncate */}

- **A Dependencies tab** on every project, catalog item and MCP server: what it depends on and what
  depends on it, grouped by kind, with provenance and owner teams. Stale edges are dimmed and say
  when they were last seen. A drawing follows; press **+** on a node to draw its own dependencies.
- **Govern → Dependency graph**: search every connected entity and filter by kind, owner team and
  provenance.
- **A Blast radius drawer**: pick an operation or a schema and see who is affected, directly and
  transitively, and every piece of evidence the change invalidates, with links.
