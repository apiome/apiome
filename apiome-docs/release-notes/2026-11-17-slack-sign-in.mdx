Teams whose shared identity is their Slack workspace can now sign in to Apiome with **Sign in with
Slack**, and link Slack beside any other provider on
[Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Slack** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It needs
  the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** of a Slack app — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Slack's side. The environment variables are `SLACK_CLIENT_ID` and `SLACK_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/slack`. Slack accepts only
  `https://` redirect URLs.
- **Scopes.** The Slack app needs the **`openid`**, **`profile`** and **`email`** user token
  scopes.
- **Other workspaces.** A new Slack app signs in only members of the workspace that owns it. Turn
  on **Manage Distribution → Activate Public Distribution** to let people from other Slack
  workspaces sign in.
- **Email addresses.** Slack is a **Trusted** provider: a sign-in joins the account with the same
  address, or starts a new one, only when Slack reports the address as verified. Otherwise, sign
  in another way first and link Slack from **Linked accounts**.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `slack` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/slack` stores its credentials.
