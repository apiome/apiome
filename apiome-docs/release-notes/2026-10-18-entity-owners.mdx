title: Every API can have an owner team
description: Owner teams on catalog entities, suggested from tags, CODEOWNERS and the spec itself.
authors: [apiome]
---

Projects, catalog items, MCP endpoints, consumers and connectors can now have an
[owner team](/workspace/teams#ownership) — the team findings about them will be routed to.

{/* truncate */}

- **Suggested, never assumed.** Gateway tags (`owner:payments`), CODEOWNERS rules for a spec's path
  and an `x-apiome-owner` extension in the document suggest owners, each shown with its source.
  Nothing is sent to a suggestion until someone confirms it.
- **Confirm, assign or bulk assign** over the API; every change is in the access audit, including
  who confirmed a suggestion.
- **Unknown teams are called out.** A gateway tag naming a team that does not exist is a connector
  finding — *unknown team `x` in tag* — instead of a silent miss.
- **Owners in every list.** The catalog, projects, MCP endpoint and consumer lists carry each
  entity's `owner`, with no extra request.
