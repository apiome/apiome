Subscribe an export preset to a project and every publish runs the preset's export on the new version and
delivers it — to a repository, a webhook or a bucket. See
[Export automatically on every publish](/ship/auto-export-on-publish).

{/* truncate */}

- **Kept current:** downstream copies follow every publish, with no one re-running the export.
- **Guarded:** delivery halts when the export's loss reaches critical (or, if you choose, any loss) — the
  fidelity advisory is now a gate. The publisher and the subscriber are notified; a pre-approved loss is
  delivered anyway and recorded.
- **Released by a person:** a halted export can be delivered with **Release**, or fixed and republished.
- **Audited:** every run lists its export, its loss and why, and each delivery's outcome.
- **Everywhere:** **Auto-export** on Export studio's **Presets** tab, `apiome export auto`, and
  `/v1/export/{tenant}/auto-exports`.
