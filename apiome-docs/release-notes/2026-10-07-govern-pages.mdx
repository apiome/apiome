The [Govern](/govern) group now has a page for every screen in it — [Style guides](/govern/style-guides)
with the rule catalog, custom rules, assignment and policies, [Lint posture](/govern/lint-posture), the
[Access audit](/govern/access-audit) and [Reviews and the approval gate](/govern/reviews).
(#5625)

{/* truncate */}

- **Waivers, step by step** — request a waiver on Lint posture, then approve it with an expiry.
- **Approvals, step by step** — turn on the approval gate in a style guide's Policy, review a version,
  and what publishing says when the gate is not met.
- **Every page links its rule-level guides**, its REST endpoints and, where there is one, its CLI
  command.

_The Style guides and Access audit pages were withdrawn while those features were held back — see
[Catalog and Projects first](/release-notes/mvp-focus) — and are back with RC8: see
