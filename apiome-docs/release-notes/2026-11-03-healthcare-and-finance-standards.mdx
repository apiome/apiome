The vertical-standard formats are complete: FHIR R4, R5 and R6, HL7 v2, ISO 20022, FIX and FIX
Orchestra, EDI X12 and UN/EDIFACT with UBL and Peppol, and ISO 8583. A new guide,
[Healthcare and finance standards](/bring-in/healthcare-and-finance-standards), brings them together.

{/* truncate */}

- **What you can import:** one table per industry (healthcare, finance, supply chain and
  e-invoicing), with each format, its importer and its own page.
- **How it works:** importing a message, definition or interchange, what lands in the catalog, each
  format's lint rules, and comparing versions.
- **Beyond the import:** FHIR live refresh, the HL7 v2 to FHIR map, linking FIX messages to their
  Orchestra repository, and export.
