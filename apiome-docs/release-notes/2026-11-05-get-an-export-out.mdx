What an export gives you, and every way to take it out of Apiome, is now summed up on
[Get an export out](/ship/get-an-export-out).

{/* truncate */}

- **One file, or one `.zip`:** a single document comes as itself; anything with several files comes as
  one `.zip` whose `manifest.json` names every file.
- **Streamed, and kept for 24 hours:** large downloads stream with their size known up front; a download
  is deleted after 24 hours, while the job and its history remain.
- **Sent on:** deliver the result to git, a webhook or a bucket, or register its schemas on a
  Confluent-compatible schema registry.
