**Members** now opens with the roster near the top. The seat-meter card above the table becomes one
line, and the two identity-provider cards at the foot become a single **Coming next** line.
[Members & seats](/workspace/members).

{/* truncate */}

- **One summary line.** Members, active, pending, suspended and seats used — *9 of 10 seats used* —
  then the plan, with the same numbers the meter showed.
- **A seat bar only when it matters.** A thin bar appears under the line once 80 % of the seats are
  taken.
- **No dead buttons.** The **Configure SSO** and **Enable SCIM** buttons, which did nothing, are
  gone: *Coming next: SSO (OIDC/SAML) · SCIM 2.0 provisioning*, with **Follow in What's new**. With
  every seat taken, **Invite member** is not shown and the page says why, instead of offering a
  turned-off button.
