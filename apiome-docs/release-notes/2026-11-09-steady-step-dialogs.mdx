title: Dialogs with steps hold still
description: "Multi-step dialogs keep a fixed top edge and a minimum height, so they no longer jump between steps."

---

A dialog that walks you through steps used to be exactly as tall as the step it was showing, and
centred. Moving to a longer or shorter step moved both its edges, and the buttons with them.
Those dialogs now stay put. [The Import wizard](/bring-in/import-wizard).

{/* truncate */}

- **A fixed top edge.** **Import specification**, **Add an MCP server**, **Enable two-factor
  authentication** and **Regenerate backup codes** open a little below the top of the window. The
  title and the step markers do not move when the step changes.
- **A minimum height.** Short steps share one height, with the buttons at the foot of the dialog.
  A step that needs more room grows downward, up to the height of the window, and then scrolls.
- **First-tenant setup.** The setup card keeps one height across its short steps, so **Back** and
  **Continue** stay in the same place.
