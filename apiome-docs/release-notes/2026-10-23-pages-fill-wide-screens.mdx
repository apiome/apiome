title: Pages fill wide screens
description: Content now spans the space beside the sidebar instead of a centred column.

On a wide monitor, pages used to sit in a centred 1,440 px column with empty space on both sides.
They now use the whole area beside the sidebar, with a slim even margin.

{/* truncate */}

- **At least 95 % of the space.** The side margin is 2.5 % of the area beside the sidebar, or the
  density's usual page padding when that is larger, so on a laptop nothing changes.
- **Tables gain room.** Projects, Catalog, Versions, Repositories and Members show wider columns
  on a 1920 or 2560 px screen.
- **Forms keep their reading width.** Form pages such as **Add repository** stay at 920 px, and
  very wide screens stop at 2,400 px so a page never stretches past readable.
