Git, webhook and object-storage delivery destinations can now be saved once per workspace as a
**delivery profile** and used by name — in Export studio, the CLI and the API. See
[Reuse a delivery destination with a profile](/ship/export-delivery-profiles).

{/* truncate */}

- **Reusable, named destinations:** a profile holds a channel, its destination and a reference to the
  credential it delivers with, the repository or a stored bucket credential.
- **Secrets stay sealed:** a webhook's signing secret is sealed on the profile and never shown again.
- **Manage them in Export studio:** a new **Delivery** tab lists, creates, edits and deletes profiles
  and shows each one's deliveries.
- **Deliver on completion:** tick profiles under **Deliver when it completes** on **Review**, pass
  `--deliver` to `apiome export`, or send `delivery_profiles` with a job; the export is delivered as
  soon as it finishes.
- **Deliver by name:** **Deliver now** on a finished export, `apiome export deliver`, or
  `POST …/jobs/{job_id}/deliveries` with a profile id.
- **Every delivery audited:** list a job's deliveries across all channels, or every delivery made with
  a profile. Profile changes and deliveries are in the audit log.
