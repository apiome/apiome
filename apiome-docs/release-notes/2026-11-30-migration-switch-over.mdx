The Migration wizard's **Switch over** step now shows that the move worked, and keeps your old
broker and Apiome in step while pipelines move one by one. See
[Switch over](/ship/pact-broker/migration#switch-over).

{/* truncate */}

- **The agreement check.** After the copy, Apiome asks the old broker and itself the same
  can-i-deploy questions — up to 40: what the old broker has deployed or released and the latest
  version of each pacticipant, against each environment. The step reads *Copied. Apiome and
  acme.pactflow.io give the same answers.*, or *38 of 40 agree.* with each difference, both answers
  and why: a verification result that was not copied (and the reason), a pact or version missing, a
  result only Apiome has, or a question the old broker could not answer.
- **Watch who has moved.** Each pacticipant with where it last published — **Apiome** or **Old
  broker** — and how long ago, stragglers first.
- **Keep copying until everyone has moved.** Switch it on and, every 15 minutes, Apiome copies what
  is new at the old broker — only what is new: what was already copied is not even read again — and
  refreshes the check and the list. The token is kept encrypted while it is on and deleted when it is
  off. If the old broker stops answering for 24 hours, keep-in-sync stops itself and says so.
- **`apiome pact migrate --follow`** does the same from a terminal or a pipeline: it copies again
  every `--interval` minutes from its state file until you press Ctrl-C, or stops itself after 24
  hours without an answer.
- **REST:** `GET` / `PUT /v1/tenants/{t}/pact-migration/runs/{id}/sync`; a finished copy carries
  `facts.agreement` and `facts.moved`, and `options.agreement_questions` sets how many questions are
  asked (OpenAPI 1.709.0).
