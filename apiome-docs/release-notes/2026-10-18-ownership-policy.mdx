Two workspace settings and a report keep [ownership](/workspace/teams#ownership) from quietly
decaying.

{/* truncate */}

- **A catch-all team.** Findings on anything without a confirmed owner still land in the Unowned
  findings report, and now also reach the team you choose — set it in the tenant's
  [Ownership settings](/workspace/tenants#ownership).
- **Require an owner to publish.** Turn it on and a project needs a confirmed owner team before a
  version can be published (reason `no-owner`). The publish dialog always shows the gate — **Off**,
  **Met** or **Blocked** — so nobody is surprised.
- **Ownership drift.** Under **Workspace → Teams**: entities whose owner team was deleted or has no
  members, and suggested owners nobody confirmed in 30 days.
- **Facts for scorecards.** Every entity's owner read carries `owner.present` and
  `owner.contactable`.
