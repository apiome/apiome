OpenAPI 3.2 tag taxonomies now survive import. Each tag keeps its `summary`, `parent`, `kind`
and `externalDocs`, and the `parent` links form a tag tree.
[Tag hierarchy (OpenAPI 3.2)](/bring-in/import-a-spec#tag-hierarchy-openapi-32).

{/* truncate */}

- **Project metadata.** The taxonomy is stored on the project under `x-apiome-tag-taxonomy`.
- **Associations.** Operations keep their tags. Classes list the tags their schema declares
  (`x-tags`, `x-tag` or `tags`) in `x-apiome-tags`.
- **Cycles and unknown parents.** A tag whose parent is undeclared, or that sits on a parent cycle,
  imports as a top-level tag, with an import warning.
- **Analyze.** **Feature compatibility** lists **Tag taxonomy (summary / parent / kind)** with the
  tag, level and top-level counts, and the kinds used.
