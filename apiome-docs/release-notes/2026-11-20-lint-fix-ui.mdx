The deterministic lint fixes are now one click away where the finding is: in a revision's lint
report and in Lint posture.

{/* truncate */}

- **Lint report (Versions):** findings with a deterministic fix carry a **Fix** chip, and **Apply N
  deterministic fixes** applies every ready one. Confirm a reason, and the fixes become a new
  revision that is linted again; the report links it with its new grade.
- **Lint posture:** a finding's drawer has a **Fix** section and an **Apply fix** button, and the
  bulk bar applies the fixes of a selection. A finding moves to **Fixed** only once the new
  revision's lint report no longer lists it.
- **Never a surprise:** breaking and conflicting fixes show their reason instead of an action, a
  published revision's fixes are read-only, and MCP endpoint findings say why they have no document
  to fix.

See [Version dialogs → Lint report](/build/version-dialogs#lint-report) and
[Lint posture → Apply a fix](/govern/lint-posture#apply-a-fix).
