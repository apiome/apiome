Contract testing and breaking-change gates are complete: Apiome sorts every change between two
versions as breaking, non-breaking or docs-only, and uses that one sorting everywhere. The new
[Contract testing and CI gates](/ship/contract-testing-and-ci-gates) page is the map.

{/* truncate */}

- **In pull requests:** `apiome diff openapi.yaml --against payments-api@latest --fail-on breaking`
  fails the build on a breaking change; `apiome/diff-action` adds a pull-request comment, and a
  container image covers GitLab and Bitbucket. A `diff:read` key is all a pipeline needs.
- **At publish:** every publish writes a classified changelog, shown on the **Changes** tab and on
  the browse app, and sent with the `version.published` webhook.
- **Semver kept honest:** the breaking-publish guardrail warns about, or blocks, a breaking change
  published without a major version.
- **Against live deployments:** consumer contracts (with Pact import), provider verification,
  scheduled checks with drift webhooks, and a deploy gate that answers "can I promote this?"
