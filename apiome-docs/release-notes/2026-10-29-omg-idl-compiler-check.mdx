A **CORBA IDL** export is now checked by the IDL compiler its profile targets before you download it.
[How CORBA IDL exports are checked](/ship/export-a-spec#omg-idl-corba-or-dds-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **The right compiler.** A `corba` export is checked by omniORB's `omniidl`, a `dds` export by
  Eclipse Cyclone DDS `idlc`.
- **Compiler detail.** A compile error fails the export and quotes the compiler's own message and line.
- **Without the compiler**, Apiome's CORBA IDL importer reads the export back, as before.
