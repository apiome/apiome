Every card and row on the launcher now opens something. Products that are not available yet are
named once, on a **Coming next** line under the applications, instead of taking a card each.
[The launcher](/getting-started/launcher-and-home#the-launcher).

{/* truncate */}

- **Only what opens.** **Control Panel**, the apps your plan includes and **Browser**. The
  **Control Panel** card is emphasised and says when you last had it open on this device.
- **Coming next.** **Developer Suite**, **Community** and **Marketplace** are listed as text, not
  as cards that do nothing when clicked. **Follow in What’s new** opens the release notes, where
  they are announced when they ship.
- **A quieter hero.** The greeting, the headline and your workspace chips stay. A new **need
  attention** chip counts unpublished versions with blocking lint findings and opens **Lint
  posture**.
- **The roadmap panel is gone.** Its only row could not be opened. **Resources** now spans the
  page.
