People can now sign in to Apiome, create an account and link their Polar account on
[Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) with **Continue with Polar**.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Polar** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It needs
  the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** of an OAuth app created under **Settings → Developers** on
  Polar; there is no issuer to set.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Polar's side. The environment variables are `POLAR_CLIENT_ID` and `POLAR_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/polar`, added as the app's
  **Redirect URI**.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **openid**, **profile** and **email**. It reads the name, email
  address and avatar of the person signing in; it reads no organizations, products, orders,
  subscriptions or customers and keeps no Polar token.
- **Trusted.** Polar says whether the email address is verified. A verified address joins the
  Apiome account with that address, or creates one; an unverified one ends with *Your sign-in
  provider could not confirm that your email address is verified…*.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `polar` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/polar` stores its credentials.
