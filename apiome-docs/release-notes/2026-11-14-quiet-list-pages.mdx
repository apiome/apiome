The quiet pass is complete. Every list page in the Control Panel now puts its first row of data near
the top of the screen at 1440 × 900, without losing a single status, number, note or control. The
new [Reading a list page](/getting-started/reading-a-list-page) page shows where each one lives.

{/* truncate */}

- **One row of status chips.** On **Versions**, *Compatible*, *What’s new* and *deprecated* are chips
  in one row instead of three stacked banners. The most urgent one opens its detail; **click a
  chip** to read another.
- **A summary line instead of stat cards.** **Catalog**, **Repositories** and a repository's page,
  **Primitives & types**, **Members** and **Lint posture** sum up their figures in one line.
  **Show stats** opens the full cards in place and the page remembers your choice. Figures that need
  you are chips that open what they count, such as *1 stale · 1 failed* on Repositories or
  unresolved `$ref` on Primitives & types.
- **Notes show once.** The Catalog's explainer shows on your first visit with **Learn more** and
  **Got it**. Afterwards **How the catalog works** in the page description holds the same text.
  The Sunset timeline's warning banner is now a **Where these warnings come from** link.
- **One toolbar per list.** The Versions list has one toolbar: search, lifecycle, an **Any time**
  date popover, **All authors**, the state chips, sort and **Clear filters (n)**. A repository's
  file filters sit behind **Filters**. Filters are kept in the page address.
- **Fewer header buttons.** The Versions header is **Compare**, **Import**, **More actions** (`⋯`)
  and **New version**, and the project picker is the last step of the breadcrumb (**Jump to
  project**). **Export studio** has the standard header with **Back to Versions**.
- **Coming next instead of dead buttons.** The SSO and SCIM cards on **Members** are one **Coming
  next** line that links to What's new.
- **Smaller fixes.** The **Import specification** wizard is as tall as its step and its forward
  button reads **Continue**; the **Profile** hero is a flat surface.

Each change has its own page: [Versions](/build/versions), [Catalog](/bring-in/catalog),
[Repositories](/bring-in/repositories), [Primitives & types](/build/primitives-and-types),
[Members](/workspace/members), [Lint posture](/govern/lint-posture), [Sunset
timeline](/ship/sunset-timeline), [Export studio](/ship/export-studio) and [the Import
wizard](/bring-in/import-wizard).
