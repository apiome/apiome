Each version on **Versions** now has a **Heatmap**: its classes as tiles, warmer where they score
higher. [How each metric is measured](/build/version-dialogs#heatmap).

{/* truncate */}

- **Four ways to read a schema.** Switch between **Complexity**, **Recent change**, **References**
  and **Documentation**; the warmest classes lead the grid.
- **One place to find hot spots.** Spot the complex class nobody documented, or the one half the
  schema points at, before you change it.
- **Open it from the row.** Choose **Heatmap** from any version's **Actions** menu — published or
  draft.
