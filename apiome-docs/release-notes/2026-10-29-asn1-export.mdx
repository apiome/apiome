Exporting to **ASN.1** now writes one X.680 module that compiles with `asn1tools`, whatever the item
was imported from, and keeps its validation rules as ASN.1 subtype constraints.
[How types and constraints are written](/ship/export-a-spec#asn1-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **Types.** Objects, unions, enums and lists become `SEQUENCE`, `CHOICE`, `ENUMERATED` and
  `SEQUENCE OF`; a sized integer keeps its width (`uint8` is `INTEGER (0..255)`).
- **Constraints.** Lengths become `SIZE (…)`, bounds become value ranges, and patterns become
  `PATTERN`; anything ASN.1 cannot state is listed in the fidelity report.
- **Module options.** Set the **module_name** and the module's **oid**, such as
  `1.3.6.1.4.1.99999.1`.
