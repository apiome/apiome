Ten features that were held back while Catalog and Projects were hardened are now part of the main
build. Every member sees them in the sidebar and the command palette, with no preview flag to set,
and their guide pages are back on this site.

{/* truncate */}

- **Bring in.** [Connectors](/bring-in/connectors) sync the APIs in your gateways and registries.
  The Catalog counts everything by [kind](/bring-in/catalog#kinds), with
  [Agents and models](/bring-in/agents-and-models) and components you
  [migrate from Backstage or Compass](/bring-in/migrate-from-backstage-or-compass).
- **Ship.** The [Schema registry](/ship/schema-registry) gives Avro, JSON Schema and Protobuf
  schemas subjects, ids and versions. [SDK settings](/ship/sdk-settings) is the **SDK & branding**
  tab of the Export studio, with the [Terraform provider](/ship/sdk-infrastructure-targets) and
  [MCP server](/ship/sdk-mcp-server) kits.
- **Govern.** [Style guides](/govern/style-guides) and their
  [revisions](/govern/style-guide-revisions), the [Access audit](/govern/access-audit), the
  [Dependency graph](/govern/dependency-graph), [Shadow APIs](/govern/shadow-apis) and
  [Scorecards](/govern/scorecards).
- **Workspace.** [Teams](/workspace/teams) own projects, catalog items and MCP servers.
- **Projects.** A project's [Consumers](/build/projects#consumers) page registers who depends on
  the API and attributes live traffic to them.
- **Panels that belong to them are back.** The **Owner** column, filter and **Assign owner** on
  Projects, Catalog and Published; the **Dependencies** tab on Versions, catalog items and MCP
  servers; the **Subjects** tab and **Registry** chip in the Catalog; **Recent runs** on
  Repositories; **Compass export** in the import wizard; the **Facts** checklist on a catalog
  item; and the **Ownership** section of the tenant manage drawer.
- **Settings you set over the REST API have their screens again.** The approval gate, the
  compatibility mode and the import & export quality policy are on **Govern → Style guides**.
- **Data browser and Migrations are removed.** The two unfinished tools at `/ade/database` and
  `/ade/migration` are no longer part of Apiome: working with stored records belongs to Api-DB, a
  separate product. Their addresses now answer *page not found*. Nothing else is held back.
