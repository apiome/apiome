Avro schemas are now checked for compatibility the way a Confluent Schema Registry checks them.
[Import an Apache Avro schema](/bring-in/avro-import#compatibility-between-versions).

{/* truncate */}

- **Diffs grade by mode.** Comparing two versions of an Avro catalog item grades a change as
  breaking when new readers cannot read old data (`BACKWARD`), and as dangerous when only old readers
  fail (`FORWARD`). Adding a field without a `default` is breaking, removing one is dangerous, and
  either with a `default` is safe.
- **The registry enforces levels.** Avro subjects now honour `BACKWARD`, `FORWARD` and `FULL` and
  their `TRANSITIVE` forms. An incompatible version is refused with HTTP `409`, and
  `apiome registry compat` lists the reasons.
- **Same answers as Confluent.** Verdicts and messages match a live Confluent Schema Registry on
  more than a hundred recorded cases.
