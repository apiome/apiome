People can now link their TikTok account on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) and sign
in to Apiome with it from then on.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **TikTok** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It needs
  the **Client key** and **Client secret** of a TikTok for Developers app with Login Kit — no
  issuer. TikTok calls its client ID a *client key*; enter it in the **client ID** field.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  TikTok's side. The environment variables are `TIKTOK_CLIENT_KEY` and `TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/tiktok`, added as a Login Kit
  **Redirect URI**.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **user.info.profile** only and uses it to read the display name,
  username and avatar of the person signing in. Apiome reads no videos, followers, likes or
  messages and keeps no TikTok token.
- **Link only.** TikTok never shares an email address, so Apiome can never use TikTok to find or
  create an account. People sign in another way, link TikTok from **Linked accounts**, and can sign
  in with TikTok after that. Before linking, a TikTok sign-in ends with *…did not share an email
  address…*.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `tiktok` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/tiktok` stores its credentials.
