ROS 2 robots talk through typed interfaces. Apiome now imports `.msg`, `.srv` and `.action` files,
or a whole interface package, into the catalog as an RPC API.
[What gets imported](/bring-in/ros2-import).

{/* truncate */}

- **Messages become records.** ROS 2 primitives map to scalars, and constants become an enum on the
  message.
- **Services and actions become operations.** A service is a request/response operation; an action
  is a goal operation plus a subscription to its feedback channel.
- **Bounds become constraints.** `string<=32` sets a maximum length, `T[3]` an exact item count and
  `T[<=10]` a maximum item count.
- **Packages resolve their own references.** Import a package as an archive and fields typed by
  sibling or cross-package messages link to them. A standard package such as `std_msgs` outside the
  upload is listed in the coverage ledger; an unknown one is refused.
