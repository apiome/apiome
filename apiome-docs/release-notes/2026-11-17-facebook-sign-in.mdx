People can now link their Facebook account on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) and sign
in to Apiome with it from then on.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Facebook** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the App ID and App secret of an app in Meta for Developers with the Facebook Login use case
  — no issuer. [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what
  to set on Facebook's side. The environment variables are `FACEBOOK_CLIENT_ID` and
  `FACEBOOK_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/facebook`, added to the app's
  **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs**.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for the **email** and **public_profile** permissions only, and uses them
  to read the name, email address and picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no pages,
  posts or friends and keeps no Facebook token. Before reading the profile, Apiome checks that the
  access token was issued to your app.
- **Link first.** Facebook does not say whether an email address is verified, so Apiome never uses
  a Facebook address to find or create an account. People sign in another way, link Facebook from
  **Linked accounts**, and can sign in with Facebook after that. Signing up with Facebook, or
  signing in with it before linking, ends with *Your sign-in provider could not confirm that your
  email address is verified…* — even when the address matches an existing account.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `facebook` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/facebook` stores its credentials.
