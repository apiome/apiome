The navigation rail is one row shorter: workspaces are managed from the workspace switcher, and
the Data browser and Migrations finally have a menu row of their own.
[Where Tenants lives now](/workspace/tenants).

{/* truncate */}

- **Manage workspaces…** is the last row of the workspace switcher at the top of the rail, next
  to the workspaces it lists. It opens the **Tenants** page, unchanged.
- **Data browser** and **Migrations** sit in your user menu at the foot of the rail, above
  **Admin console**. Until now they were reachable only by typing their address.
- Nothing is gone from the command palette: ⌘K still jumps to **Tenants**, and now to
  **Data browser** and **Migrations** too, listed under **Tools**.
