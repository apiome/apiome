WSDL export is complete: the WSDL + XSD emitter, its fidelity pack, multi-file packaging with
validation, and the **WSDL** target card, CLI and fixtures.
[WSDL and XSD from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#wsdl-and-xsd-from-any-source) now opens with an
overview of all of it.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to export to WSDL:** to hand any REST, RPC, GraphQL or JSON Schema item to a SOAP
  toolchain as a WS-I document/literal service, or to round-trip a WSDL losslessly.
- **At a glance:** one table from the **WSDL** card and its layout, through how the item is written
  and what the fidelity report lists, to the checks before every download and the CLI.
