title: Steps you can click
description: "The shared Stepper can make each step a button that jumps to it."

---

The shared `Stepper` has a new option: pass `onStepSelect` and every step becomes a button that
jumps to that step. See [Stepped dialogs](/reference/design-tokens#stepped-dialogs).

{/* truncate */}

- **Opt-in.** Steppers in the web app look and behave as before; none of them uses the option.
- **Studio.** The class, property and class-property editors use it in **Guided** mode, so a
  click on a step still opens it after they moved to the shared stepper.
- **For contributors.** `onStepSelect` is called with the step's `id` and index. The button is
  `type="button"`, keeps the step's look, and is named by the label and its state ("Step 2 of 3").
