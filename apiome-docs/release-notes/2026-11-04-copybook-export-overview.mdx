The COBOL copybook export is complete: the copybook emitter, its fidelity report, PL/I include
structures, the cb2xml check, and the card, CLI and Browse delivery.
[COBOL copybook from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#cobol-copybook-from-any-source) now opens with an
overview of all of it.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to export to a copybook:** fixed-width records for z/OS Connect, CICS, IMS and batch COBOL from
  any item, a lossless round trip for an imported copybook, and PL/I `DECLARE` structures for PL/I programs.
- **At a glance:** one table from the card and its options through the layout, the fidelity report, PL/I
  output and the checks to the CLI and Browse, each linked to its part of the section.
