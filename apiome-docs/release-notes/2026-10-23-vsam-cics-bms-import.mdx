Apiome now imports two mainframe sources as data schemas: VSAM cluster definitions (IDCAMS
`DEFINE` commands or a `LISTCAT` listing) and CICS BMS maps.
[What gets imported](/bring-in/vsam-cics-bms-import).

{/* truncate */}

- **Clusters become records with their keys.** Each cluster's primary key and every alternate index
  over it become fields at their declared offset and length. Record size, organisation (KSDS, ESDS,
  RRDS, VRRDS) and storage parameters are kept on the record.
- **The missing field layout is declared.** A cluster definition never gives its record's fields, so
  each record says so in the coverage ledger and points you to the copybook. Linear datasets, GDGs
  and utility commands are listed rather than modelled.
- **Maps become records with positioned fields.** Each BMS map is a record. Its named fields keep
  their row, column and length, take their type from `PICIN` / `PICOUT`, and carry their
  attributes (protected, numeric, bright, initial cursor) as declared metadata. Unlabelled literals
  stay on the map.
- **Symbolic maps pair with their mapset.** Upload the mapset with its generated copybook and each
  field names its `L` / `F` / `A` / `I` / `O` entries. A copybook on its own gives the records
  without screen positions.
