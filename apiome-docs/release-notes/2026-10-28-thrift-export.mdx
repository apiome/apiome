Exporting an item to **Apache Thrift** now writes a `.thrift` file the Thrift compiler accepts, from
an OpenAPI, Protobuf, GraphQL or any other typed or service-bearing source.
[How the Thrift is written](/ship/export-a-spec#thrift-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **Valid names.** Type, field, method and service names that are not Thrift identifiers, or that are
  reserved words such as `default` and `class`, are renamed (`com.acme.Order` becomes
  `com_acme_Order`). Each rename is in the fidelity report.
- **Every kind of type.** Unions get a field per member, aliases and named scalars become
  `typedef`s, and an item imported from Thrift keeps its own `typedef`s.
- **Methods that compile.** Parameters are gathered into a `<Method>Request` struct, a method returns
  its success response, and `throws` lists exceptions only.
- **Namespaces per language**, with the new **namespaces** option.
- **Checked.** Each export is compiled with `thrift` when the server has the Thrift compiler, and
  read back by the Thrift importer otherwise.
