Apiome now reads [**SWIFT MT**](/bring-in/swift-mt-import) messages — the legacy half of the ISO 20022
migration — beside the MX messages it already imports.

{/* truncate */}

- **Blocks and fields, natively.** Every header block, sequence and `:nn[a]:` field is on the
  **Format details** tab, with exact source positions.
- **Option letters and qualifiers are structure.** `50K` and `50F` stay different shapes; `/INV/`,
  `/OCMT/` and the other code words keep their qualifier.
- **Statements and covers.** MT940/942 statement lines are grouped with their `:86:`, and an MT202 COV
  keeps its sequence B and links to the payment it covers.
- **MT ↔ MX as a note.** Each message names its documented MX counterpart (MT103 → `pacs.008`,
  MT940 → `camt.053` …) with a clear disclaimer: Apiome does not translate the message and does not
  claim a translation would be valid.
