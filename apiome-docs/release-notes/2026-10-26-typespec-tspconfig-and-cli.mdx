A TypeSpec project's `tspconfig.yaml` now shapes the import, and the CLI imports a project
straight from its folder. [Import a TypeSpec API](/bring-in/typespec-import#tspconfigyaml).

{/* truncate */}

- **`tspconfig.yaml` settings.** The OpenAPI version, `omit-unreachable-types`,
  `include-x-typespec-name`, `safeint-strategy` and `warn-as-error` are applied. Every other
  setting is ignored and listed, with the reason, on the catalog item.
- **One-file projects compile.** A `.tsp` file uploaded with its `tspconfig.yaml` compiles like a
  multi-file project.
- **Import a folder from the CLI.** `apiome import typespec ./shop-api/` packs the project's files,
  leaving out `node_modules` and build output, and starts from `package.json`'s `tspMain` or
  `main.tsp`.
