**Repositories** and a repository's page now open with their table near the top. The rows of
stat cards become one summary line each, and the always-open panels fold away until you ask for
them. [Repositories](/bring-in/repositories).

{/* truncate */}

- **One summary line.** Repositories reads *repositories · files indexed · imports (30d) · last
  scan · needs attention*; a repository reads *files · importable · branches · imports (30d) · last
  scan*. **Show stats** opens the cards they replaced.
- **Refresh activity is a chip.** While a spec imported from a repository is stale or failed to
  refresh, the line ends with a chip such as *1 stale · 1 failed*. Click it to open the full
  Refresh activity panel in a drawer.
- **Filters behind one button.** On a repository's **Files** tab the importable preset stays in
  the table's toolbar; the glob, the regex and the switches open from **Filters**, which counts
  how many are set. The filters are kept in the page address, so a filtered list survives a
  reload and can be shared.
