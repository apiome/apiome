Forms across the Control Panel were spaced a little differently from page to page: a label sat 4,
6 or 8 px above its field, and fields were 12 or 16 px apart, depending on the screen. They now share
one rhythm. [Preferences](/workspace/preferences).

{/* truncate */}

- **Label to field: 6 px.** The same on API keys, agent access, versions, mock scenarios, style
  guides, SDK settings and the other forms that had drifted.
- **Field to field: 16 px.** Sections of a form are 24 px apart.
- **Compact density tightens forms too.** With **Density** set to **Compact** in Preferences, fields
  are 12 px apart and sections 20 px.

Nothing moves to a different place and no field changes size; the gaps between them are now the
same everywhere.
