RC8 is about **Catalog** and **Projects**, the core of Apiome. To keep the release focused, five features
that were opened too early are held back again and will open one at a time, each with a finished guide
and a demo video:

{/* truncate */}

- **Connectors** (Bring in)
- **Products** (Ship)
- **Shadow APIs** (Govern)
- **Dependency graph** (Govern), with the **Dependencies** tabs and **Blast radius**
- **Schema registry** (Workspace), with the catalog **Subjects** tab

They disappear from the sidebar, the command palette and the shortcuts, and their pages say they are not
available yet. Nothing is deleted: the REST API, the CLI and the MCP tools for these features keep working,
and your connectors, products and subjects are untouched. Their guide pages come back on this site when
each feature opens; until then the [REST reference](/reference/rest/connectors) describes what the API does.
