SOAP services imported from WSDL are now checked against the WS-I Basic Profile, the rules SOAP
toolkits follow to interoperate.
[WS-I Basic Profile checks](/bring-in/wsdl-import#ws-i-basic-profile-checks).

{/* truncate */}

- **Document/literal.** `encoded` messages, bindings that mix rpc and document styles, and
  document- or rpc-literal bodies with the wrong parts are errors.
- **Unique operations.** Operations a receiver cannot tell apart are errors; operations that share
  a `soapAction` are warnings.
- **Resolvable imports.** A `wsdl:import`, `xs:import` or `xs:include` that the import cannot read
  is an error, as is a `wsdl:import` of a schema.
- **Imports still succeed.** Every problem is listed on the catalog item with its path.
