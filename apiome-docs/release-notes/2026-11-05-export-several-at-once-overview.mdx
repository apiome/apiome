Exporting no longer means one version, one format, one manual download. Apiome now exports several
formats, items or versions in one job, saves the combination as a preset, and runs it on every publish.
See [Export several at once, and on every publish](/ship/export-several-at-once).

{/* truncate */}

- **Several formats in one job:** one archive with a folder per format, from the Export dialog, Export
  studio or `apiome export <format> --targets`.
- **Several items or every version:** bulk export from the Catalog, **Export all versions…** on a version,
  or `--items` / `"all_versions"` — each item succeeds or fails on its own.
- **Presets:** formats, their options and a delivery profile saved by name, applied in the dialog, the
  studio, the CLI (`--preset`) and the API.
- **Auto-export on publish:** a preset run on every publish of a project and delivered — unless the export
  loses too much, when delivery stops and people are notified.
