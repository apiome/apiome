[Teams](/workspace/teams) are named groups of workspace members — *Payments*, *Platform* — and the
first step towards every catalog entity having an owner who hears about its problems.

{/* truncate */}

- **Owner links.** Each team carries a contact e-mail and links to its chat channel, on-call schedule
  and documentation.
- **Leads and members.** Set the whole membership in one call; everyone must already be in the
  workspace, and anyone who leaves the workspace leaves its teams at the same moment.
- **Safe deletes.** A team that owns entities cannot be deleted until you name the team that takes
  them over.
- **A new permission.** **Roles** has a **Teams** row: Owner and Admin manage teams, Editor and
  Viewer can view them. Every change is in the access audit.

Teams are managed over the [REST API](/reference/rest/teams) for now.
