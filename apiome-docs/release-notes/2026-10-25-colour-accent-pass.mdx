The workspace has more colour and more life, in the places a glance needs an anchor. Nothing
moved and nothing was renamed: every route, dialog, theme and line of copy is where it was.

{/* truncate */}

- **Each area has a hue.** Build is green, Bring in violet, Ship azure, Govern amber and
  Workspace rose. The rail shows a dot of it on every group heading, tints the group's icons
  with it, and draws the current page as a soft tint in that hue with a bar at its edge. The
  page header leads its title with an icon tile in the same hue, dots the area's breadcrumb,
  and underlines itself with a short hairline. Home keeps its white pill: it is the brand
  moment, with the launcher's honeycomb and glows behind the greeting.
- **Selected is azure.** The selected tab's underline, label, count and glyph, the active
  filter chip and the sorted column header all take the azure the design reserves for
  selection, instead of black.
- **Alive on touch.** Clickable cards lift with a deeper shadow and an azure hairline; a row
  tints azure and underlines its link; buttons settle when pressed; the primary button is lit
  from above. All of it is still under *Reduce motion*.
- **Home.** The six counters count up on first paint and carry a tile in their area's hue.
  Quick actions are four tiles in the hue of the area each opens. *Needs attention* rows carry
  a rail in their tone. Done checklist steps turn green.
- **Records.** Project and catalog cards carry a 3 px edge along the top in their format's hue;
  the Versions page leads its title with the project's own hexagon.

Screenshots across the documentation are refreshed for the pages this touches. The proposal
this implements is in [`docs/mockups/PROPOSALS.md` §5](https://github.com/apiome/apiome/blob/main/docs/mockups/PROPOSALS.md).
