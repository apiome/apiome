The released pages are back inside their first-row budgets, with no allowances left to excuse
them. Every unavailable button on those pages now says why it is unavailable.

{/* truncate */}

- **Versions.** The status row starts closed, so the versions come straight after it. Each chip
  still names its version and date, such as *v2.2.0 deprecated · sunset 30 Sep*. **Click a chip**
  to open its detail and its actions. The tab strip stays on one line, and scrolls when the window
  is too narrow for it. See [Versions](/build/versions).
- **Catalog.** The filters fit one row. **Cards** and **Table** moved to the end of the chips row,
  where the mockup draws them. **Domain** and **Tag** appear once an item carries one, and the
  search box narrows to make room for them. See [Catalog](/bring-in/catalog#find-an-item).
- **Catalog item.** **Re-convert to OpenAPI project** sits above **Export** and **View code**, so
  the description and the pills take fewer lines.
- **MCP servers.** The **Owner** filter is now part of the toolbar card, not a row of its own.
- **Unavailable buttons.** Hover a disabled button on Versions and its dialogs, Catalog, Projects,
  the import wizard or Export to see why it is unavailable, and what to do about it. A test now
  keeps these screens at zero buttons without a reason.

For contributors, [Visual parity and first-row budgets](/reference/visual-parity) lists the
allowances that remain. They are all on MCP pages that have not had their quiet pass yet.
