OMG IDL export is complete: the IDL 4.2 emitter with its `corba` and `dds` profiles, its fidelity
pack, validation with omniidl and Cyclone DDS `idlc`, and the **CORBA IDL** target card, CLI and
fixtures. [OMG IDL (CORBA or DDS) from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#omg-idl-corba-or-dds-from-any-source)
now opens with an overview of all of it.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to export to OMG IDL:** to serve a CORBA estate with interfaces and exceptions, to define
  DDS topic types with `@key` fields, or to round-trip a CORBA IDL item losslessly.
- **At a glance:** one table from the **CORBA IDL** card and its options, through how the item is
  written and what the fidelity report lists for each profile, to the compiler check before every
  download and the CLI.
