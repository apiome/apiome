The fidelity report of an **ASN.1** export now says, field by field, which validation rules the
module keeps and how. [What the report lists](/ship/export-a-spec#asn1-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **Constraints carried.** Lengths, bounds, item counts and sets of integers written as ASN.1
  subtype notation are reported as carried, with the notation used.
- **Patterns.** A pattern that only lists the allowed characters (`^[A-Z0-9_]{3,8}$`) becomes a
  permitted alphabet that validators check; any other pattern is kept as a comment and reported as
  dropped.
- **Types-only warning.** Exporting an item with operations or channels raises a critical warning that
  counts what is left out.
