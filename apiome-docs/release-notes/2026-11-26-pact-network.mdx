You can now see a pacticipant's network in the app: every application connected to it through
pacts, and how the newest pact of each integration was verified. Open a pacticipant and click
**Network diagram** in its **Pacts** section. See [Integration network](/ship/pact-broker/network).

{/* truncate */}

- **Dependencies at a glance.** Consumers sit on the left and their providers to the right, so a
  chain of dependencies reads left to right. The whole connected group is drawn, not only direct
  neighbours.
- **Verdict colouring.** Each arrow is coloured and marked by its newest pact's verdict: **Verified**
  ✓, **Failed** ✕, **Failed (pending)** ! or **Not verified yet** ? (dashed). A key counts each one.
- **One click away.** Click an arrow to open the pact, or a pacticipant to see its own network. Each
  one also takes keyboard focus.
- **Nothing hidden.** The **Integrations** table under the diagram lists every integration. A large
  network reads at most 25 pacticipants, and the page names any it did not read or could not read.

The page reads only and needs **Pacts** → **View**.
