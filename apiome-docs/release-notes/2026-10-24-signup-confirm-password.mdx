title: Confirming a new account asks for its password
description: Email sign-up confirmation now requires the password chosen at sign-up.
authors: [apiome]
---

When you confirm a new account from the **Confirm your email for Apiome** message, Apiome now asks
for the password you chose when you signed up. It then makes that password the account's only
password. See [Create an account](/getting-started/create-an-account).

{/* truncate */}

- **Why:** a security review found that someone who signed up first with *your* address, using a
  password of their own, could sign in to the account after you confirmed it and started using it.
  Each confirmation link now belongs to one sign-up, and only that sign-up's password completes it.
- **What changes for you:** after you click **Confirm email**, enter your **Password** and **click
  "Confirm and sign in"**. If the page says *That is not the password chosen for this sign-up*, use
  the link in your newest email, or sign up again with your own password.
- **On confirmation,** every other link and session for the account stops working.
