People can now sign in to Apiome with their Twitch account, and link Twitch beside any other
provider on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Twitch** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the Client ID and Client Secret of a **Confidential** application registered in the Twitch
  Developer Console — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Twitch's side. The environment variables are `TWITCH_CLIENT_ID` and `TWITCH_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/twitch`, added under the
  application's **OAuth Redirect URLs**. Twitch requires an exact match.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **openid** and **user:read:email** only and uses them to read the
  username, email address and picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no streams, follows,
  chat or subscriptions and keeps no Twitch token.
- **Email addresses.** Twitch is a **Trusted** provider: a sign-in joins the account with the same
  address, or starts a new one, only when Twitch reports the email address as verified. An
  unverified address is refused. To use such an account, verify the address in Twitch, or sign in
  another way first and link Twitch from **Linked accounts**.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `twitch` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/twitch` stores its credentials.
