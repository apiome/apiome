**COBOL Copybook** export now works for every item, not only items imported from a copybook: Apiome lays
out its types as `01` record layouts that mainframe programs (z/OS Connect, CICS, IMS) can `COPY`.
[How types become records](/ship/export-a-spec#cobol-copybook-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **Records.** Nested objects become group items; lists become `OCCURS` tables, with a `-COUNT` field
  for lists that vary in size; unions share storage with `REDEFINES`.
- **Pictures.** Text is `PIC X(n)`, integers `S9(n) COMP`, decimals packed `COMP-3`, booleans a
  one-character flag with condition names.
- **Names.** COBOL data names, at most 30 characters and unique in the copybook — the same item always
  gets the same names.
- **Options.** Choose `COMP` or `DISPLAY` numbers, the default text length and table size, the decimal
  scale, and fixed or free format.
