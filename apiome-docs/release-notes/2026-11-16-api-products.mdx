Consumers ask for access to "Payments API", not to four projects and an MCP server. [API
products](/reference/rest/api-products) are that bundle — the first piece of API products and application
access (APX).

{/* truncate */}

- **Ship → Products.** **New product** names the bundle; the editor's **Add member** includes a
  project at latest, a project at a version or range (`2.x`), an MCP server or a catalog item, by
  slug or id, optionally in one environment.
- **Members follow publishes.** A project at latest, or a range, is read at its newest published
  version every time — publish `payments` 1.5.0 and the product moves to it with no edit. The
  members table shows what each member was added with and what it means today.
- **Visibility and state.** **Private**, **Workspace** or **Portal**; **Portal** needs every member
  published, and the editor names the ones that are not. State runs **Draft → Published →
  Deprecated → Retired**.
- **In the dependency graph.** Each product is an `api_product` node with an `includes` edge per
  member, so the blast radius of a breaking change in a project lists the products that bundle it,
  with their owner team.
- **Plans (APX-1.2).** A product's [plans](/reference/rest/api-products) are its tiers: an auth
  strategy (API key, OAuth2 client credentials, mutual TLS or none), declared limits per second,
  minute, hour, day or month (each count at least 1, each window once), manual or automatic
  approval, the gateway environments they are provisioned to, a maximum key age and terms. A plan
  whose environments cannot be provisioned yet is saved as **Declared only** with a notice, a member
  whose security schemes do not fit the auth strategy is flagged, and an OpenAPI export of an included
  version carries the plans as `x-apiome-plans` (other formats drop them, and the fidelity report
  says so).
- **Product pages in Browse (APX-1.3).** A **Portal** product that is published or deprecated gets
  a [page in the browse app](/reference/rest/api-products) at `/tenant/<org>/products/<product>`:
  its summary, the APIs it includes (each linking to the version it resolves to) and a card per plan
  with the authentication, limits, approval, key lifetime, pricing and terms — never the billing
  reference. **Sign in to request access** signs a reader in to the portal. The organization's page
  lists its products under **Products**, and catalog search finds them (`type:product`).
- **Product and plan lifecycle (APX-1.4).** Deprecating a plan blocks new subscriptions and takes a
  **Retire on** date, a **Replacement plan** (published, same auth strategy) and a message for
  subscribers, who are told once. **Migrate to plan…** previews and then moves a deprecated plan's
  subscriptions to the replacement's consumer group without new keys; **Retired** waits for the date
  and revokes the plan's provisioning. A product retires once its plans have. Deprecated products and
  plans appear on the [Sunset timeline](/ship/sunset-timeline#api-products-and-plans) and on the
  [changelog](/ship/api-changelog#plan-and-product-changes) of every API a portal product includes. See
  [Lifecycle](/reference/rest/api-products).

With the API: `GET|POST /v1/tenants/{t}/products`, `GET|PATCH|DELETE …/products/{slug}`,
`GET|POST …/products/{slug}/members`, `DELETE …/members/{id}`, `GET|POST …/products/{slug}/plans` and
`GET|PATCH|DELETE …/plans/{plan}` and `POST …/plans/{plan}/migrate` — see the
[REST reference](/reference/rest/api-products).
