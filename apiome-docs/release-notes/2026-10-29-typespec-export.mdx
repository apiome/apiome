Exporting an item to **TypeSpec** now writes a `.tsp` file that the TypeSpec compiler accepts. You can
export from OpenAPI, Protobuf, GraphQL, Avro or any other typed or service-bearing source.
[How the TypeSpec is written](/ship/export-a-spec#typespec-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **Compiles.** Each export is compiled with the bundled `tsp` before it is offered. The title is
  written in the `@service({ title })` form that compiler accepts.
- **Routes and responses.** Operations keep their verb and full `@route`, and their path, query,
  header and cookie parameters. Responses keep their statuses, headers and error bodies.
- **Types and constraints.** Unions, maps, named lists, scalars and aliases are written, not dropped.
  Constraints become `@minLength`, `@maxValue`, `@pattern` and similar decorators, and defaults are
  kept.
- **Names kept.** A name that is not a TypeSpec identifier is written in backticks rather than
  changed.
- **Exact fidelity report.** Whatever TypeSpec cannot carry is listed on the construct it concerns.
  GraphQL, gRPC and event items are reported as reframed to HTTP, with each query, mutation,
  subscription or stream named.
- **More decorators.** Tags become `@tag`, parameter and header descriptions `@doc`, and lists of
  allowed values unions of literals such as `"name" | "age"`.
- **Fixed:** importing a single `.tsp` file now reads model properties, which were missing. It also
  reads doc comments, operation routes and optional parameters. An interface's operations are no
  longer filed under the previous interface's route.
