People can now sign in to Apiome with their LinkedIn account, and link LinkedIn beside any other
provider on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **LinkedIn** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the **Client ID** and **Primary Client Secret** of a LinkedIn app — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  LinkedIn's side. The environment variables are `LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID` and
  `LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/linkedin`, added to the app's
  **Authorized redirect URLs for your app** on its **Auth** tab.
- **Product.** The LinkedIn app needs the **Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect** product,
  requested on its **Products** tab. It grants the **`openid`**, **`profile`** and **`email`**
  scopes.
- **Keep the same app.** LinkedIn identifies each person per app. Rotate the secret of the app you
  have rather than replacing the app, or people who linked LinkedIn must link it again.
- **Email addresses.** LinkedIn is a **Trusted** provider: a sign-in joins the account with the
  same address, or starts a new one, only when LinkedIn reports the address as verified.
  Otherwise, sign in another way first and link LinkedIn from **Linked accounts**.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `linkedin` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/linkedin` stores its credentials.
