The design system has a split pane. With `SplitPane`, a form sits beside a preview, or a tree,
canvas and inspector sit side by side, and you drag the line between them to resize. See
[Split pane](/reference/design-tokens#split-pane).

{/* truncate */}

- **Opt-in.** Screens in the web app look the same as before; none of them uses it yet. Apiome
  Suite's Slate editors and README generator are the first to use it.
- **Keyboard.** Focus a handle and press **←** or **→** (with **Shift** for larger steps), **Home**
  or **End**. **Enter** collapses or restores a side pane.
- **Remembered widths.** A page can keep its widths in the browser, so they are the same on the
  next visit. Resizing never reloads a preview.
- **For contributors.** `SplitPane` takes `panes` (`id`, `label`, `defaultSize`, `minSize`,
  `maxSize`, `collapsible`), one child per pane, and an optional `storageKey`. The design-system
  gallery shows it under **Split pane**.
