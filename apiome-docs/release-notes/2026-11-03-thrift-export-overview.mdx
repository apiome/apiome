Apache Thrift export is complete: the IDL emitter, stable field-id assignment, its fidelity pack,
validation with the Thrift compiler and thriftpy2, and the **Apache Thrift** target card, CLI and
fixtures. [Thrift from any source](/ship/export-a-spec#thrift-from-any-source) now opens with an
overview of all of it.

{/* truncate */}

- **When to export to Thrift:** to move an OpenAPI, gRPC, GraphQL, WSDL or Avro item to a `.thrift`
  file, to keep clients compatible with field ids that stay the same from one export to the next,
  or to round-trip a Thrift item losslessly.
- **At a glance:** one table from the **Apache Thrift** card and its options, through how the item
  is written and what the fidelity report lists, to the compiler and thriftpy2 checks before every
  download and the CLI.
