A look-and-feel review of the RC6 launch build found the workspace calm but plain: one navy button
carries all the colour, the rail is a grey list, and nothing reacts to the pointer. A set of
before/after mockups now proposes where colour and motion should go — one hue per area in the rail and
page headers, the hexagon on every project and catalog item, azure for everything selected, tone on
the Home numbers, and a lift on everything you can click — without changing any route, theme or copy.

{/* truncate */}

- **What it is:** proposals only. Nothing in the app changes with this note.
- **Where to look:** `docs/mockups/proposals/accent-pass.html` in the repository is the hub, with a
  drag-to-compare pair for Home, Catalog, Projects, Versions and Members and a 1 : 1 rail comparison.
  The rationale and the proposed tickets (HIVE-14) are in
  [`docs/mockups/PROPOSALS.md` §5](https://github.com/apiome/apiome/blob/main/docs/mockups/PROPOSALS.md).
- **When:** planned as the first look-and-feel item for RC7.
