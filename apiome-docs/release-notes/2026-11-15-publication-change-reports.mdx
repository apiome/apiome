Every publish now writes a change report: what changed since the previous published version, as a
header, a body and a footnote you can edit. One new page explains how the parts fit:
[Publication change reports](/ship/publication-change-reports). (#2698)

{/* truncate */}

- **A semantic comparison.** Two resolved OpenAPI documents are compared by schemas, properties,
  references, relationships and documentation, and the result is kept with the report. Compare
  any two documents with [`POST /v1/openapi/change-report`](/reference/rest/openapi-change-report). (#2699)
- **Stored with each publication.** A report belongs to one published version, with your edits kept
  beside the generated text. (#2700)
- **Templates.** Header, body and footnote are Mustache templates, chosen from the request, the
  project's default, the tenant's default or the built-in **1.0.0**
  ([Change report templates](/reference/rest/change-report-templates)). (#2701)
- **Written on publish.** `changeReportBaselineMode` compares with the previous published version
  (`auto`), with nothing (`initial`) or with one you choose (`manual`), and `…/change-report/publish-preview`
  shows the report before you publish. A failed report never blocks the publish. (#2702)
- **Read, edit and regenerate** over [`…/change-report`](/reference/rest/version-change-report), or on the
  **Change report** tab of Versions, which is behind `FEATURE_GITLIKE`. (#2703)
- **Checked by golden fixtures** for the comparison and the rendered text. (#2704)
- **Review, PDF export and an audit log** for enterprise tenants — see
  [Review, approve and export change reports as PDF](/release-notes/change-report-approvals). (#2705)
