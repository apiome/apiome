Publishing a version can now be stopped by your Pact contracts. See
[block a publish on failing contracts](/ship/pact-broker/can-i-deploy#block-a-publish-on-failing-contracts).

{/* truncate */}

- A new `contracts.publishPolicy` in the deploy-gate policy sets what a failing contract does to a
  publish: `off`, `warn` (the default) or `block`.
- Under `block`, a version whose can-i-deploy verdict is "not deployable" is refused. Force publish
  with a reason goes past it, and is recorded in the audit trail.
- The publish dialog gains a **Contracts** card with the failing rows. A verification that is still
  missing shows as pending and never blocks.
- `GET …/versions/{tenant}/{project}/{revision}/contracts-publish-gate` previews the result without
  publishing. OpenAPI 1.694.0.
