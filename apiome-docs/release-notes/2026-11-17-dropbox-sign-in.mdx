People can now sign in to Apiome with their Dropbox account, and link Dropbox beside any other
provider on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Dropbox** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the **App key** and **App secret** of a Dropbox app, entered as the **Client ID** and
  **Client secret** — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Dropbox's side. The environment variables are `DROPBOX_CLIENT_ID` and `DROPBOX_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/dropbox`, added under
  **OAuth 2 → Redirect URIs** on the app's **Settings** tab.
- **Scope.** Apiome asks for **`account_info.read`** only, and uses it to read the name, email
  address and picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no files and keeps no Dropbox
  token, so an **App folder** app is enough.
- **Email addresses.** Dropbox is a **Trusted** provider: a sign-in joins the account with the
  same address, or starts a new one, only when the Dropbox account's email address is verified.
  An unverified address is refused. To use such an account, verify the address in Dropbox, or
  sign in another way first and link Dropbox from **Linked accounts**.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `dropbox` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/dropbox` stores its credentials.
