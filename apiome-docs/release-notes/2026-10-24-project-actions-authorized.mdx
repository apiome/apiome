Editing, deleting, restoring and permanently deleting a project from **Projects** or **Catalog** now
follows the same rule as the REST API: you must be signed in, the project must be in your current
workspace, and your role must grant the action.

{/* truncate */}

- **Role checked.** **Edit** and **Restore** need `projects:edit`; **Delete** and **Delete permanently**
  need `projects:delete`; **New project** needs `projects:create`. A **Viewer** who tries gets
  *You do not have permission to … projects in this workspace* and nothing changes. Tenant
  administrators can do everything, as before. See [Roles](/workspace/roles).
- **Your workspace only.** A project in another workspace is reported as *Project not found*, and a
  new project is always created in the workspace you are signed in to.
- **Fails safe.** If Apiome cannot confirm your permissions, the change is refused rather than
  allowed. Try again in a moment.
