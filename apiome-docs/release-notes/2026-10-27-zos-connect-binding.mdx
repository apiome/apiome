The copybook ↔ REST binding a z/OS Connect descriptor declares is now kept in the catalog.
[Link z/OS Connect to its copybooks](/bring-in/zos-connect-binding).

{/* truncate */}

- **Suggested links.** A descriptor is suggested the copybooks that declare its request and
  response structures, and the OpenAPI document it targets; a copybook is suggested its
  descriptor.
- **Every 01-level record counts.** A copybook that declares several 01-level records, such as a
  request and its response, binds all of them.
- **A binding panel.** The descriptor's catalog item lists each operation with its COBOL
  structures, the copybook items that declare them, and the OpenAPI that defines it, marking
  which are linked.
