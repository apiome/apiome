UBL and Peppol schemas already import; now the mirror exports. The **XSD** export's **Profile** option has
`ubl` and `peppol`. See [UBL and Peppol BIS profiles](/ship/export-a-spec#ubl-and-peppol-bis-profiles).

{/* truncate */}

- **UBL 2.1 document schemas:** every value typed by a UBL data type, names ending with their representation
  term, values that match a UBL basic component written as `cbc:` references, and a document element such as
  `Invoice` headed by the UBL header.
- **Peppol BIS Billing 3.0:** an `Invoice` or `CreditNote` with its mandatory `CustomizationID`, `ProfileID`,
  `ID`, `IssueDate`, type code and currency — the item's own, or added and reported.
- **Validated against the real UBL schemas:** the download ships the official OASIS UBL 2.1 schemas it imports,
  unmodified with their notice, and every export is built against them before you get it.
- **Honest about Peppol:** the export applies the document conventions; Peppol's Schematron business rules are
  not run, and the guide in the download says so.
