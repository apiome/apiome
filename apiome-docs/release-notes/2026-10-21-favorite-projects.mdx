The projects you pin are now your **favorites**, one step away from anywhere: listed on your
[Profile](/workspace/profile#favorite-projects) and at the top of the command palette.

{/* truncate */}

- **Favorite projects on Profile.** A new card under **Account details** lists your pinned projects
  in the current workspace — open one, or unpin it from there.
- **Favorites in ⌘K.** The command palette opens with a **Favorites** group, so any favorite is a
  keystroke and a name away from every page. A favorite is not repeated under **Recent**.
- **One list.** Favorites and **Pinned projects** on Home are the same up-to-8 projects; pin from a
  project's **⋯** menu on **Projects**.
