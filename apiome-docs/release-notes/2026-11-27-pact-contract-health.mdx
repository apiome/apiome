Every consumer ↔ provider pair now has one health state, and its verification has a history you can
read. On **Pacticipants**, click **Contract health**. See
[Contract health and verification analytics](/ship/pact-broker/contract-health).

{/* truncate */}

- **One state per pair.** **Healthy**, **Degraded**, **Failing** or **Unknown**, fused from six
  signals: the newest pact's latest verdict, the window's pass rate and flakiness, whether the pact
  is overdue, an open re-publish request, and failed contract-webhook deliveries. Each signal says
  **OK**, **Warning**, **Failing** or **No evidence**, and why. Pairs are listed worst first.
- **Trends.** A pair's page charts its **pass rate**, **flakiness** (how often the verdict flips
  between one result and the next) and **mean duration** per day, or per week over 90 days, with the
  same numbers in a table. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**.
- **On the Verification page.** Each consumer's health state sits next to its name and opens the
  pair's health.

Health is worked out on every read, so there is nothing to turn on. It needs **Pacts** → **View**.
The REST API has it under `/v1/tenants/{tenant}/pact-health` (REST API 1.687.0).
