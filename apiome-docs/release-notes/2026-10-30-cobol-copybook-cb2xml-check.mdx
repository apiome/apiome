A **COBOL copybook** export is now checked with cb2xml, an independent COBOL copybook parser.
[How copybook exports are checked](/ship/export-a-spec#cobol-copybook-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **It must parse.** An export cb2xml cannot parse fails, with cb2xml's error message (and the line
  and column of a fixed-format copybook).
- **It must parse to the planned layout.** Every record and item cb2xml reads — level, name, `PIC`,
  usage, `OCCURS` and `DEPENDING ON`, `REDEFINES`, `88` values — must match the layout Apiome planned
  with the export's options. Each difference is listed at the item's path.
- **Without cb2xml**, the export is read back by Apiome's copybook reader, as before.
