The Pact Broker is now part of the registry rather than a tool beside it. What consumers publish
and providers verify feeds the same consumer model, gates and pages your specifications already
use. The [Pact Broker overview](/ship/pact-broker/overview#how-the-pieces-fit) shows how it fits
under **Inside the registry**.

{/* truncate */}

**What the epic covers:**

- **One consumer model.** Publishing a pact declares it as the consumer's next contract revision
  in the provider's project, so breaking-change analysis sees consumers that publish pacts —
  [One consumer model](/ship/pact-broker/publish-pacts#one-consumer-model).
- **can-i-deploy in the deploy gate.** The project deploy gate has a `contracts` signal with the
  same verdict as `pact-broker can-i-deploy` —
  [In the deploy gate](/ship/pact-broker/can-i-deploy#in-the-deploy-gate).
- **A quality gate for publishing.** `contracts.publishPolicy` (`off`, `warn` or `block`) decides
  whether failing contracts warn or stop a version publish; the **Publish** dialog shows a
  **Contracts** card, and a force-publish with a reason is audited —
  [Block a publish on failing contracts](/ship/pact-broker/can-i-deploy#block-a-publish-on-failing-contracts).
- **Broker consumers in diff and CI.** `apiome diff --consumers` and the `diff-action` name the
  pacticipant and pact behind each consumer verdict —
  [Broker consumers in the diff and in CI](/ship/contract-testing-and-ci-gates#broker-consumers-in-the-diff-and-in-ci).
- **Contracts on public pages.** A public project's Browse page and the portal show its pact badges
  and the consumers that depend on it —
  [Consumers on Browse and the portal](/ship/pact-broker/badges#consumers-on-browse-and-the-portal).
