People can now link their Railway account on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) and sign
in to Apiome with it from then on.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Railway** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the client ID and client secret of a **Web** OAuth app created under your Railway
  workspace's **Settings → Developer** — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Railway's side. The environment variables are `RAILWAY_CLIENT_ID` and `RAILWAY_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/railway`, added as the app's
  redirect URI.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **openid**, **email** and **profile** only, and uses them to read the
  name, email address and picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no workspaces, projects
  or deployments and keeps no Railway token.
- **Link first.** Railway does not say whether an email address is verified, so Apiome never uses
  a Railway address to find or create an account. People sign in another way, link Railway from
  **Linked accounts**, and can sign in with Railway after that. Signing up with Railway, or signing
  in with it before linking, ends with *Your sign-in provider could not confirm that your email
  address is verified…* — even when the address matches an existing account.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `railway` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/railway` stores its credentials.
