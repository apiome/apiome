tRPC APIs have no spec file to hand a catalog — until now. Apiome imports a tRPC router and catalogs
every procedure as an operation. [What gets imported](/bring-in/trpc-router-import).

{/* truncate */}

- **Queries, mutations and subscriptions** become operations of the same kind, keyed by their
  procedure path.
- **Nested routers** become nested operation groups.
- **Inputs and outputs** are read through Zod import, with their constraints; chained `.input()`
  calls merge, and a schema shared by several procedures is one type.
- **Declared, not guessed.** A hand-written validator keeps its operation without a payload, and a
  lazily loaded router is listed in the coverage ledger.
- **Run in the same sandbox as Zod import**, and procedures are never called.
