A `types.ts` is often the only description a team has of its data. Apiome now imports the exported
`interface`, `type` and `enum` declarations of a TypeScript module as catalog schema types.
[What gets imported](/bring-in/typescript-types-import).

{/* truncate */}

- **Read by the TypeScript compiler.** References resolve across `import type` and re-exports, and
  `Pick`, `Omit`, `Partial`, indexed access and `keyof` become the shape the compiler computes.
- **Modules and sets.** Upload one `.ts` or `.d.ts` file, paste the source, or upload an archive of
  modules that import one another.
- **Generics where they are used.** `type OrderPage = Page<Order>` becomes a concrete record.
- **Nothing becomes `any`.** Conditional, mapped and template-literal types, uninstantiated generics
  and merged interfaces are listed in the coverage ledger instead of being guessed.
