You can now read a pact in the app. A pacticipant's page lists every pact it is in, and each pact
opens on its own page: its interactions, how providers verified it, and what changed since the
previous pact. See [Pact detail](/ship/pact-broker/pact-detail).

{/* truncate */}

- **Every pact of a pacticipant.** The new **Pacts** section on a pacticipant's page lists the pacts
  it is in, as the consumer or the provider, with the latest consumer version and how its last
  verification went.
- **Interactions, as written.** Each interaction opens to show its provider states with their
  parameters, and its request and response, or its message. HTTP, message and synchronous message
  interactions are drawn in any Pact specification; any other type is shown as its JSON, never left
  out.
- **Verification status.** The page says whether the pact is **Verified**, **Failed**, **Failed
  (pending)** or **Not verified yet**, and lists every provider version that verified it, with how.
- **Diff vs previous.** The page compares the pact with the previous *different* pact and lists the
  interactions added, changed and removed. Each changed value shows its path and its old and new
  values.
- **History.** Every consumer version that published a pact for the provider, newest first, with
  its verdict. Click one to open its pact; the address keeps the version so you can share it.

The page reads only and needs **Pacts** → **View**.
