People can now link their Kick account on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) and sign in
to Apiome with it from then on.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Kick** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It needs
  the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** of an app created under **Settings → Developer** on Kick —
  no issuer. [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to
  set on Kick's side. The environment variables are `KICK_CLIENT_ID` and `KICK_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/kick`, added as the app's
  **Redirect URL**.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **user:read** only and uses it to read the username, email address
  and profile picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no channels, chat, streams, followers
  or subscriptions and keeps no Kick token.
- **Link first.** Kick does not say whether the email address it shares is verified, so Apiome never
  uses it to find or create an account. People sign in another way, link Kick from **Linked
  accounts**, and can sign in with Kick after that. Before linking, a Kick sign-in ends with *Your
  sign-in provider could not confirm that your email address is verified…*.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `kick` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/kick` stores its credentials.
