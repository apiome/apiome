The guide pages for the RC6 surface have been checked against the app as it ships, and every
screenshot on them has been captured again.

{/* truncate */}

- **Pages reviewed:** all of **Getting started**; **Catalog**, the **Import wizard**, **Import a
  spec**, **Convert to OpenAPI** and **Format details** under **Bring in**; **Projects**,
  **Versions** and the **version dialogs** under **Build**; and **Cut a version**, **Publish a
  version**, **Export a spec**, **Published** and the **Sunset timeline** under **Ship**.
- **No more pointers to what isn't released.** References to the Export studio, connectors, teams,
  the schema registry, consumer contracts, the dependency graph and the other held-back screens are
  gone. So is a sidebar description that listed them as "coming soon": they are simply not in the
  sidebar.
- **Corrected steps.** These now match the app:
  - **New version** (not "Cut a version").
  - **Continue** at the end of an import.
  - **Manage workspaces…** to reach Tenants.
  - **Download** *file* / **Download .zip** after an export.
  - The Export dialog in place of the Export studio panel.
  - Where the deleted-project buttons live.
- **Roles.** Pages that tell you to click **New project**, **Import**, **New version** or
  **Publish** now say which roles see those buttons.
