People can now sign in to Apiome with their Zoom account, and link Zoom beside any other
provider on [Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts).

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Zoom** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It
  needs the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** of a Zoom **General App** — no issuer.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Zoom's side. The environment variables are `ZOOM_CLIENT_ID` and `ZOOM_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/zoom`, entered as the **OAuth
  Redirect URL** and added to the **OAuth Allow Lists** on the app's **Basic Information** page.
- **Scope.** The Zoom app needs **`user:read:user`** only, added on its **Scopes** page. Apiome
  uses it to read the name, email address and picture of the person signing in. Apiome reads no
  meetings or recordings and keeps no Zoom token.
- **Email addresses.** Zoom is a **Trusted** provider: a sign-in joins the account with the same
  address, or starts a new one, only when the Zoom account's email address is verified. An
  unverified address is refused. To use such an account, verify the address in Zoom, or sign in
  another way first and link Zoom from **Linked accounts**.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `zoom` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/zoom` stores its credentials.
