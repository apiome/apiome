Pin the projects you come back to most, and Home keeps them at the top in a new **Pinned projects**
panel. [How to pin a project](/getting-started/launcher-and-home#pinned-projects).

{/* truncate */}

- **Pin from Projects.** Open a project's **⋯** menu, on a card or a table row, and choose **Pin to
  Home**. The same menu offers **Unpin from Home** afterwards.
- **Unpin from Home.** Each pinned row has its own pin button; the panel counts your pins against
  the limit of eight.
- **Yours, everywhere.** Pins are kept per person on the server, so they follow you across devices,
  and nobody else in the workspace sees them.
- **In the API too.** `GET /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/pinned`, `PUT` and `DELETE
  /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/{project_id}/pin` — see the [Projects reference](/reference/rest/projects).
