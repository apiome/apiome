Apiome now imports Natural DDM listings: an ADABAS file's fields with their formats and byte
lengths, multiple-value fields and periodic groups as arrays, and descriptors as keys.
[What gets imported](/bring-in/natural-ddm-import).

{/* truncate */}

- **Every field keeps its format and length.** Alphanumeric, numeric, packed, binary, floating
  point, logical, Unicode, date and time fields map to their types, with the byte length each takes
  and the assumptions those lengths rest on.
- **Repeating structure becomes arrays.** Multiple-value fields are lists, and periodic groups are
  lists of a nested record. Upload a Natural program's view beside the DDM and its `(1:n)` bounds
  become the arrays' maximum sizes.
- **Descriptors are declared keys.** Descriptors, unique descriptors and super-, sub- and
  hyperdescriptors are listed on the file's record, with the source byte ranges each is built from.
  A descriptor that names a field the DDM never defines is refused.
