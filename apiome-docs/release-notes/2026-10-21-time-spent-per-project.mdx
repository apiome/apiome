Home has a new **Time spent** panel: how long *you* spent on each project over the last 7 or 30
days. [How time is counted](/getting-started/launcher-and-home#time-spent).

{/* truncate */}

- **Your busiest projects at a glance.** A bar for each of your top five projects, longest first,
  with hours and minutes, and your total across every project. Switch between **7 days** and
  **30 days**.
- **Active time only.** Time counts while a project is open on **Versions**, the tab is visible and
  you have used the page in the last five minutes; a forgotten background tab counts nothing, and
  two tabs on one project count once.
- **Private.** Only you see your time — not teammates, not admins.
- **In the API.** `GET /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/time-spent?days=` returns the same figures — see
  the [Projects reference](/reference/rest/projects).
