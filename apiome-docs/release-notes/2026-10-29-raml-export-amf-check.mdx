A **RAML** export is now validated by AMF and then imported again before you download it.
[How RAML exports are checked](/ship/export-a-spec#raml-from-any-source).

{/* truncate */}

- **AMF.** A RAML 1.0 violation fails the export and names its rule, line and column. Warnings do not.
  Without AMF on the server, the RAML importer reads the document instead.
- **Re-imports.** A document that does not import again fails the export with `raml.reimport`.
- **Round trip.** An item imported from RAML exports and re-imports unchanged.
