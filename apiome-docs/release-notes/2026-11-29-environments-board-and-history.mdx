**Contracts** → **Environments** now opens on a **Board** of what runs where: every service against
every environment, a verdict for each running version, and whether the next version can go. A
**History** view says who deployed, released, undeployed or ended support for what, and whether it
was verified at the time. Deployments and releases can now be ended from the app and the CLI. See
[Read the board](/ship/pact-broker/environments-dashboard#read-the-board) and
[Read the history](/ship/pact-broker/environments-dashboard#read-the-history).

{/* truncate */}

- **The board.** Services down, environments across (production last), then **Released to users**.
  Each running version has a dot — verified with everything beside it, unverified or failing — and
  where the environment to the left runs a newer version, the cell says **can go** (click to record
  it) or **blocked** (click to open the deploy simulator with that question). The facts line counts
  the blocked promotions.
- **History.** Every deployment, undeployment, release and end of support, newest first, with who
  recorded it, **Verified at the time** or **Not verified at the time**, and the version a deployment
  replaced.
- **Record an undeployment** from an environment's menu, **End support** from a release on the
  board, or untick a release in **Record a release** to end its support.
- **CLI:** `apiome pact record-undeployment` and `apiome pact record-support-ended`, with
  `pact-broker`'s flags and sentences.
- **REST:** `GET /v1/tenants/{t}/pact-environments/board` and `…/history`; recording a deployment
  or a release stores can-i-deploy's answer at the time, and ending one stores who ended it
  (OpenAPI 1.705.0).
