Arazzo workflows and OpenAPI Overlays now meet the same governance as OpenAPI. A new
[Arazzo and Overlay governance](/govern/arazzo-and-overlay-governance) page shows how the parts fit.

{/* truncate */}

- **Overlay 1.1 and 1.2** apply with their own rules and lint in an `overlay` pack. See
  [Overlay 1.1 and 1.2](/release-notes/openapi-overlay-1-2).
- **Overlays on export and on a version.** Apply them with `apiome export --overlay`, or keep an
  ordered chain on a version. See [Stored overlays](/release-notes/stored-overlays).
- **Twelve Arazzo rules**, with the misspelt rule id kept as an alias. See
  [Arazzo 1.1 validation rules](/release-notes/arazzo-1-1-validation-rules).
- **Arazzo fixes and ladder levels.** See [Auto-fix for Arazzo workflows](/release-notes/arazzo-fixes).
- **REST:** no changes.
