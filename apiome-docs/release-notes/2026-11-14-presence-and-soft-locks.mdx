Two designers can no longer edit the same thing without knowing it. Open a project on **Versions**
and the other people who have it open appear as avatars; an element someone is editing reads
**“Bob Brown is editing this”**.

{/* truncate */}

- **Who’s viewing.** Avatars appear and disappear as members open and close the project.
- **Soft locks.** Editing an element marks it for everyone else. Locks never block you; they ask.
- **Takeover.** **Ask to take over** tells the holder, who can **Hand over**. After 15 seconds
  **Take over** is yours, and the previous holder is told.
- **No stuck locks.** A closed or crashed tab releases its locks on its own.
- **For integrators.** New REST routes and a WebSocket under `…/projects/{project}/presence`; see
  [Presence and soft locks](/build/presence-and-soft-locks).
