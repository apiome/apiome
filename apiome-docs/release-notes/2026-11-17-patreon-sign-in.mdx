People can now sign in to Apiome, create an account and link their Patreon account on
[Linked accounts](/workspace/linked-accounts) with **Continue with Patreon**.

{/* truncate */}

- **For administrators.** **Patreon** is a new entry in **System settings → Add Provider**. It needs
  the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** of a client created under **Clients & API Keys** on the
  Patreon developer portal; there is no issuer to set.
  [Sign-in providers](/admin/auth-providers#settings-on-the-providers-side) lists what to set on
  Patreon's side. The environment variables are `PATREON_CLIENT_ID` and `PATREON_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- **Callback URL.** `<BETTER_AUTH_URL>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/patreon`, added under the client's
  **Redirect URIs**.
- **Scopes.** Apiome asks for **identity[email]** only. It reads the name, email address and
  profile image of the person signing in; it reads no campaigns, memberships, pledges or posts and
  keeps no Patreon token.
- **Trusted.** Patreon says whether the email address is verified. A verified address joins the
  Apiome account with that address, or creates one; an unverified one ends with *Your sign-in
  provider could not confirm that your email address is verified…*.
  [How a provider's email is trusted](/admin/auth-providers#how-a-providers-email-is-trusted)
  explains the classes.
- **REST API.** `GET /v1/admin/auth-providers` lists `patreon` as an available provider, and
  `PUT /v1/admin/auth-providers/patreon` stores its credentials.
