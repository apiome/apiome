Bring in covers everything that starts outside Apiome: importing a specification, the **catalog**
of supported formats and connected **repositories**.

The catalog home counts everything Apiome knows about by **kind** — Projects, catalog items, MCP
servers, consumers, connectors, agents, models and teams — and lists any of them, with their owner
teams, in one place; see [Kinds](./catalog.mdx#kinds) and [Agents and models](./agents-and-models.mdx).

Start with the [Catalog](./catalog.mdx) and the [Import wizard](./import-wizard.mdx);
[Repositories](./repositories.mdx) bring in whole repositories, [Connectors](./connectors.mdx) sync
the APIs in gateways and registries, and [Supported formats](./supported-formats.md) lists every format and which importer handles it.
