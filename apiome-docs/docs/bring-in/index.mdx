Bring in covers everything that starts outside Apiome: importing a specification, the **catalog**
of supported formats, connected **repositories**, **connectors** and **MCP servers**.

[Import and catalog any format](./import-any-format.mdx) is the map of every format Apiome brings in and where each part is described. [How importing works](./how-importing-works.mdx) gives the overview: the sources, the preview-then-commit flow, bulk imports and import history. Then start with the [Catalog](./catalog.mdx) and the [Import wizard](./import-wizard.mdx);
[Repositories](./repositories.mdx) and [MCP servers](./mcp-servers.mdx) bring in whole repositories and MCP servers, [Connectors](/reference/rest/connectors) sync the APIs in gateways and registries, [Migrate from Backstage or Compass](./migrate-from-backstage-or-compass.mdx) brings over another catalog as reviewed suggestions, and [MCP Registry integration](./mcp-registry-integration.mdx) maps importing, syncing and searching the official MCP Registry, and [MCP notifications and monitoring](./mcp-notifications-and-monitoring.mdx) maps change webhooks and uptime checks; and [Supported formats](./supported-formats.md) lists every format and which importer handles it. [Find APIs across formats](./find-apis-across-formats.md) shows how to filter, categorize and search them by protocol and format. [Keep imports up to date](./keep-imports-fresh.mdx) explains how each source stays current. The catalog home counts everything Apiome knows about by **kind** — Projects, catalog items, MCP servers, consumers, connectors, agents, models, components and teams — and lists any of them, with their owner teams, in one place; see [Kinds](./catalog.mdx#kinds) and [Agents and models](./agents-and-models.mdx).
