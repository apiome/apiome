Govern keeps a portfolio of APIs consistent and accountable: the **lint posture** across projects,
**reviews**, and the **shadow APIs** your gateways serve but the catalog does not describe.

Work through findings on [Lint posture](./lint-posture.mdx), require approvals with
[Reviews](./reviews.mdx), and find undescribed APIs with [Shadow APIs](./shadow-apis.mdx). The Style guides and Access audit screens are not yet available.

The rules themselves are documented under [Build](/build) —
[Built-in lint rules](../build/lint-rules.md) and [Custom lint rules](../build/custom-rules.md); this
group also covers the rule catalogs for MCP servers.
