Govern keeps a portfolio of APIs consistent and accountable: **style guides** and their rules, the
**lint posture** across projects, the **access audit** and **reviews**.

Start with [Style guides](./style-guides.mdx) — the rules every spec is scored against and the
policies that gate imports, exports and publishing. Work through findings on
[Lint posture](./lint-posture.mdx), read who changed access in the [Access audit](./access-audit.mdx),
and require approvals with [Reviews](./reviews.mdx).

The rules themselves are documented under [Build](/build) —
[Built-in lint rules](../build/lint-rules.md) and [Custom lint rules](../build/custom-rules.md); this
group also covers [style-guide history](./style-guide-revisions.md) and the rule catalogs for MCP
servers.
