A **license** is a plan: a type (free, paid or sponsor), a set of **seat limits** such as how many
tenants a person may own, and the **feature flags** it switches on. Every person holds at most one.
**Sign in to the admin console and choose Licenses** in the sidebar.

The screen has four tabs — **Licenses**, **Feature Flags**, **Flag packages** and **Assignments** —
each with its count. Feature flags and flag packages are covered on [Feature flags](./feature-flags.mdx).

<Screenshot id="admin-licenses" alt="The Licenses tab: Free, Paid, Sponsor and a disabled Partner pilot plan, with Paid expanded to show its five feature flags and four seat limits" />

## The plans a new installation has

| Plan | Type | Seat limits | Feature flags |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | free | `max_tenants` 1, `max_users_per_tenant` 5 | Schema Designer |
| **Paid** | paid | `max_tenants` 5, `max_users_per_tenant` 25 | Schema Designer, API Paths, AI Assistant, Repositories, Primitives Type Registry |
| **Sponsor** | sponsor | `max_tenants` 20, `max_users_per_tenant` 100 | the same as Paid |

Each row shows the plan's name, type badge, description and seat limits; *disabled* marks a plan
that cannot be assigned. **Click the chevron** to list its **Included Feature Flags** and **Seat
limits**.

## Seat limits

Seat limits are numeric key–value pairs stored on the plan. The form offers these presets, and
accepts any custom key:

| Key | Limits |
| --- | --- |
| `max_tenants` | Tenant workspaces a person may own — checked when they create a workspace |
| `max_users_per_tenant` | Members of a tenant on this plan — checked when someone is invited or a suspended member is reinstated; pending invitations hold a seat, suspended members do not |
| `max_projects` | Catalog projects |
| `max_versions` | Versions per project |
| `max_ai_requests` | AI assistant requests; `0` is none and `-1` unlimited |

A negative value means unlimited. A key a plan leaves out falls back to Apiome's built-in default.
Operators can switch member-seat enforcement off without a redeploy by setting
`APIOME_LICENSE_ENFORCEMENT_ENABLED=false` on the REST API; the tenant cap is always enforced.

## Create or edit a plan

1. **Click “New License”** (or a plan's **pencil**).
2. Enter a **Name** and choose the **Type** — **Free**, **Paid** or **Sponsor**.
3. Add a **Description**.
4. Under **Seat limits (quotas)**, **click “Add limit”**, pick a key from **Select a limit…** (or
   **Custom key…**, for example `max_branches`) and enter a whole number. The **trash** icon removes a
   row. Each key may appear once.
5. Under **Commercial products & features**, tick the flags the plan turns on —
   **Commercial applications** (the apps shown on the home page and in the top navigation) and **Additional
   platform features**.
6. **Click “Create License”** (or **“Save Changes”**).

<Screenshot id="admin-license-form" alt="Editing the Paid plan: name, type, description and its seat limits with preset keys and values" />

Deleting a plan (the **trash** icon) asks you to confirm. People who held it lose the features it
granted the next time they load the app.

## Assign a plan to a person

1. **Open the Assignments tab**. Every account is listed with its **Assigned License** and **Seats**;
   **Search users…** narrows the list.
2. **Click “Assign”** on a person's row.
3. **Click a plan** in **Assign license** — only enabled plans are offered, and a tick marks the
   current one.

<Screenshot id="admin-license-assignments" alt="The Assignments tab: six people with their Sponsor, Paid or Free plan and seat limits, two with no license" />

The same dialog has **Override feature flags…**, to grant flags to one person on top of their plan
(see [Feature flags](./feature-flags.mdx#grant-flags-to-one-person)), and **Remove license**, which
drops them to the unlicensed feature set the next time they load the app.

A workspace's own plan and seat use are shown to its administrators under **License & plan** in
[Tenants & the manage drawer](../workspace/tenants.mdx).

## With the API

Plans are edited only here. Workspace members read their tenant's plan, seat use and effective
features with `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant_slug}/license` — see the
[API reference](../reference/api-reference.md). An invitation refused for lack of seats answers
`403` with the code `license-seats-exhausted`.
