**Single sign-on** lets the people of one company sign in to Apiome with their work account, through
that company's own identity provider (IdP) — Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Keycloak,
Auth0 or any other that speaks **SAML 2.0** or **OpenID Connect (OIDC)**. You connect the IdP once,
give it the email domains the company owns, and from then on anyone with an address at those domains
can choose **“Sign in with SSO”** on the [sign-in page](../getting-started/sign-in.mdx).

It is set up by an installation administrator: **sign in to the admin console, choose System
settings** in the sidebar and find the **Single sign-on** card.

## SAML or OIDC?

Use whichever your IdP offers — most offer both.

| | OIDC | SAML 2.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You give Apiome** | The issuer URL, a client ID and a client secret | The IdP's metadata URL, or its metadata XML pasted in |
| **You give the IdP** | The **Redirect URI** Apiome shows | The **ACS URL** and the **SP entity ID** Apiome shows |
| **Where the email comes from** | The ID token's `email` — the IdP must mark it verified | The NameID, or an attribute you name |

The client secret, and a SAML signing key if you add one, are stored encrypted and never shown
again.

## Connect an identity provider

1. **Click “Add identity provider”** on the **Single sign-on** card.
2. Choose **OIDC** or **SAML 2.0**, and give it a **Name** (for example `Northwind Okta`). The **ID**
   fills in from the name; it appears in sign-in URLs and in the audit trail as `sso:<id>`.
3. Optionally enter the **Workspace ID** the IdP's people belong to.
4. For **OIDC**, enter the **Issuer URL** (for example `https://northwind.okta.com`), the **Client
   ID** and the **Client secret** from the app you registered at the IdP. Apiome reads the issuer's
   discovery document and checks it before saving.

   For **SAML 2.0**, enter the **IdP metadata URL** or paste the **IdP metadata XML**, then choose
   where the email address comes from: **The NameID** (when the IdP sends the email as NameID) or
   **An attribute** and its name (for example `email`).
5. **Click “Add provider”.** The provider is added **disabled**.
6. Copy the values the card now shows into your IdP's app settings: the **Redirect URI** for OIDC, or
   the **ACS URL (reply URL)** and **SP entity ID (audience)** for SAML.

Apiome refuses an IdP it could never sign anyone in through, and says why — for example
*“The IdP offers single sign-on only over HTTP-Artifact”* (Apiome sends sign-in requests over
HTTP-Redirect), or a discovery document that names a different issuer.

## Add the company's email domains

1. In the provider's row, type a domain in the domain box — for example `northwind.io` — and **click
   “Add domain”**.
2. **Click “Mark verified”** once you have confirmed the company owns the domain.

Only verified domains are used for sign-in, and a domain can belong to only one identity provider
on the installation. Automatic verification by DNS record is coming with the workspace owner's
single sign-on page.

:::caution Verify only domains the company owns
An identity provider signs in *anyone* at its verified domains. Marking a domain you have not
confirmed would let that IdP sign in people at a company it does not represent.
:::

## Turn it on

**Click “Enable”.** The provider shows **Live** once it is enabled and has at least one verified
domain — from the next sign-in, people at those domains can use **“Sign in with SSO”**.
**Needs a verified domain** means it is enabled but nothing routes to it yet. **“Disable”** stops
new sign-ins through it at once; **“Remove…”** deletes it, with its secrets.

## What happens when someone signs in

Apiome applies the same rules to a single sign-on as to any other sign-in:

- **Only verified domains.** An IdP can sign in only addresses at its own verified domains. An
  address anywhere else is refused, whatever the IdP says.
- **A verified email.** For OIDC, the IdP must mark the email verified in the ID token; an
  unverified email is refused, never linked to an account.
- **One email, one account.** If an Apiome account with that email already exists, single sign-on is
  added to it, and the person keeps everything they had. A first-time person is sent to finish
  setting up their account, under your [sign-up policy](./auth-providers.mdx#sign-up-policy).
- **Disabled and suspended accounts stay out**, and an account with two-factor authentication still
  asks for its code.
- **Every attempt is recorded** in the sign-in audit trail as `sso:<id>`, and so is every change on
  this card.

Each sign-in must answer a request Apiome sent, within five minutes; a SAML response can be used
once, must be signed, and must carry valid timestamps. A sign-in started from the IdP's own
dashboard (IdP-initiated) is refused — start from Apiome's sign-in page.

## When sign-in fails

The sign-in page explains each refusal. The codes appear in the address bar and the audit trail:

| Code | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sso-provider-not-found` | No live provider covers the email's domain | Check the domain is added, verified and the provider enabled |
| `sso-email-domain-mismatch` | The IdP returned an email outside the provider's domains | Check the IdP sends the work email, or add the domain |
| `unverified-email` | The OIDC IdP did not mark the email verified | Verify the email in the IdP |
| `sso-token-invalid` | Apiome could not verify the OIDC ID token | Check the client ID and the issuer |
| `sso-assertion-invalid` | Apiome could not verify the SAML response | Check the ACS URL, SP entity ID and the IdP's signing certificate |
| `sso-assertion-replayed` | The SAML response was used before | Start again from the sign-in page |
| `sso-state-invalid` | The sign-in took too long or was started elsewhere | Start again from the sign-in page |
| `sso-binding-unsupported` | The IdP answered over HTTP-Artifact | Have the IdP send responses over HTTP-POST |
| `sso-discovery-failed` | Apiome could not read the OIDC discovery document | Check the issuer is reachable |
| `sso-account-not-linked` | An account with the email exists but could not be linked | Ask the person to sign in another way |

## For operators

- The card needs `AUTH_CONFIG_ENC_KEY` set (the same key that seals sign-in provider secrets) and
  `INTERNAL_SERVICE_TOKEN` shared between apiome-ui and apiome-rest; without the token, no single
  sign-on is offered.
- Apiome fetches discovery documents and metadata only from public `https://` addresses. To test
  against an IdP on your own network, set `APIOME_SSRF_ALLOW_PRIVATE=true` on apiome-rest — never in
  production.
- The same operations are available over the REST API:
  [`/v1/admin/sso/providers`](../reference/rest/sso.md).
