**Users** holds every account on the installation, across all workspaces, and the queue of people
who have signed up and are waiting for an account. **Sign in to the [admin console](./admin-console.mdx)
and choose Users** in the sidebar.

This is the installation-wide account list. To manage who belongs to *one* workspace and with which
role, use [Members & seats](../workspace/members.mdx) in the app, or [Tenants](./admin-tenants.mdx) here.

<Screenshot id="admin-users-signups" alt="Users on the Pending Signups tab: three people waiting, with source and date" />

The four cards at the top are live:

| Card | Shows | Underneath |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Total Users** | Every account | How many were created in the last 7 days |
| **Verified Users** | Accounts with a verified email | How many are enabled |
| **Pending Signups** | Requests in the queue | How many arrived today |
| **Recent Signups** | Requests from the last 7 days | *Last 7 days* |

**Refresh** reloads the cards and both tabs. The result of every action shows in a banner above
the cards for five seconds.

## Approve a signup

The page opens on **Pending Signups**, whose tab carries the queue's count. Each row shows the
person's **Name**, **Email**, **Source** (*Direct* when the signup did not record one) and **Date**.

1. **Click the ⋮ button** at the end of the row.
2. **Click “Create User”.**
3. **Click “Create account”** in *Create an account for &lt;name&gt;?*

The person gets a verified, enabled account with the password they chose when they signed up, can
sign in straight away, and leaves the queue. If an account with that email already exists, the
banner reads *User with this email already exists* and nothing changes.

To turn a request down instead, **click “Delete Signup”** and confirm with **“Delete request”**. The
request leaves the queue; nobody is emailed, and the address can sign up again.

## Find and manage an account

**Click “Active Users”** for every account, with its **License**, **Status** and **Created** date.
**Status** shows two badges: **Verified** or **Unverified**, and **Enabled** or **Disabled**.

<Screenshot id="admin-users" alt="The Active Users tab: five accounts with license, verified and enabled badges, and creation date" />

**Click the ⋮ button** on a row for its actions:

<Screenshot id="admin-users-menu" alt="A user's actions: Mark Unverified, Disable User, the license list, Manage Feature Flags and Delete User" />

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark Verified** / **Mark Unverified** | Sets whether the email counts as verified. An unverified account cannot sign in. |
| **Disable User** / **Enable User** | A disabled account cannot sign in. Nothing is deleted. |
| **Assign License…** | Opens the list of enabled licenses; **click one** to assign it, replacing any license the user had. **Remove License** appears when the user has one. See [Licenses](./licenses.mdx). |
| **Manage Feature Flags…** | Opens **Feature Flags** for this user — the flags their license grants, and per-user overrides. **Click “Done”** to close it. See [Feature flags](./feature-flags.mdx). |
| **Delete User** | Deletes the account — see below. |

## Create an account

To add someone who has not signed up:

1. **Click “New User”** at the top right.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Email** and **Password** (at least 8 characters; it is stored as a bcrypt
   hash). Give the person this temporary password by a channel you trust.
3. Leave **Mark email as verified** and **Enable account immediately** ticked so they can sign in
   at once, or untick either to hold the account back.
4. **Click “Create User”.**

<Screenshot id="admin-users-create-dialog" alt="Create New User with a name, email and temporary password, both boxes ticked" />

The email is stored in lower case. The page switches to **Active Users**, where the new account
appears.

## Delete an account

1. **Click the ⋮ button** on the row, then **“Delete User”**.
2. **Type the user's email** in the confirmation field. **Delete user** stays off until it matches.
3. **Click “Delete user”.**

<Screenshot id="admin-users-delete-dialog" alt="The delete confirmation: the user's email typed to confirm, and the Delete user button" />

The account, every tenant membership it carried and any license assigned to it are gone; this cannot
be undone. To stop someone signing in but keep their account, **disable** it instead.

## With the API

There is no REST endpoint or CLI command for these screens: apiome-ui reads and writes the
`apiome.users` and `apiome.signup` tables through its own server. Inside one workspace, membership and
roles are managed from [Members & seats](../workspace/members.mdx) instead.
