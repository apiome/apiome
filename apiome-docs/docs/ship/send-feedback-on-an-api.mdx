Someone reading a published API in Browse can tell the team that owns it what is wrong or unclear.
For example: "this response shape is wrong", or "this description doesn't say what happens on a
retry". Feedback lands in the team's [Discussion](../build/versions.mdx#consumer-feedback) tab with a
**Consumer** tag. The team's replies come back to the reader in Browse.

Only a signed-in [reader](./portal-readers.mdx) can send feedback. A visitor who isn't signed in sees
an invitation to sign in instead.

---

## Send feedback

1. **Open a published version in Browse** and **click the "Feedback" tab**.
2. If you aren't signed in, **click "Sign in to send feedback"**, sign in with the emailed link, and
   you return to the **Feedback** tab.
3. Under **About**, pick **Whole API** or one operation, for example `GET /pets/{petId}`.
4. **Write your message** and **click "Send feedback"**.

<Screenshot id="browse-feedback-tab" alt="The Feedback tab of a published version for a visitor who is not signed in: an invitation and the Sign in to send feedback button" />

Your feedback appears under **Your feedback**, with the operation it is about and its status
(**Open** or **Resolved**).

## Follow up

- The team's replies appear in the thread, marked **Team**, with the name of the person who wrote them.
- **Type in the "Reply" box** and **click "Reply"** to answer.
- **Click "Resolve"** when your question is settled, or **"Reopen"** to bring it back. The team can
  resolve and reopen it too.

Only you and the owning team see your feedback. Other readers never see it, and you never see the
team's internal discussion.

## Limits

- Feedback can only be sent on a **published, public** version.
- A message can be up to 20,000 characters. Sending and replying share a per-person budget, so
  many messages in a minute are refused for a short while.
- `@name` in your message is plain text. It does not notify anyone on the team.

## With the REST API

The `portal-feedback` endpoints are in the [REST reference](../reference/rest/portal-feedback.md).
Every one needs a reader session for the organization, sent as `Authorization: Reader <token>`.
The team lists the same threads with `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant}/projects/{project}/comment-threads?origin=consumer`
([comments](../reference/rest/comments.md)).
