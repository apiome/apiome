Ship is what happens after a version is ready: **publishing** it, planning its **sunset**, exporting
it in another format, generating an **SDK** and trying it against a **mock**.

The usual order is [cut a version](./cut-a-version.md), [publish it](./publish-a-version.md), then
manage it on [Published](./published.mdx) and plan its end of life on the
[Sunset timeline](./sunset-timeline.mdx). Convert it with the [Export studio](./export-studio.mdx)
(what survives is in [Understand export fidelity](./export-fidelity.md)), brand its SDK in
[SDK settings](./sdk-settings.mdx), and try its mock with [Mock try-out](./mock-try-out.mdx). The
guides behind the mock engine are under [Mock runtime](/ship/mocks).
