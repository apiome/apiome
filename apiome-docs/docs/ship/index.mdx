Ship is what happens after a version is ready: **publishing** it, planning its **sunset**, exporting
it in another format and trying it against a **mock**.

The usual order is [cut a version](./cut-a-version.md), [publish it](./publish-a-version.md), then
manage it on [Published](./published.mdx) and plan its end of life on the
[Sunset timeline](./sunset-timeline.mdx). [Export to any format](./export-to-any-format.mdx) is the map
of everything export: where to start one, what survives, and where each part is described. [Export it](./export-a-spec.md) in another format, in place with [the Export dialog](./export-dialog.mdx) ([How an export runs](./export-jobs.mdx) explains
the job behind every export, and [How an export is checked](./check-an-export.mdx) what makes it
safe to download), or
convert it step by step in the [Export studio](./export-studio.mdx) (what survives is in
[Understand export fidelity](./export-fidelity.md)), [visualize it](./visualize-an-export.mdx) and [test drive the result](./test-drive-an-export.mdx) — and, for a team that exports every day, [run exports at scale](./run-exports-at-scale.mdx); what an export gives you and how to [get it out](./get-an-export-out.mdx) is summed up in one place; [Export catalog items](./export-catalog-items.mdx)
covers catalog items one at a time or in bulk. An Avro, JSON Schema or Protobuf export can be [pushed to a schema
registry](./push-avro-to-a-schema-registry.md). [Contract testing and CI gates](./contract-testing-and-ci-gates.mdx) is the map of breaking-change
gates: in CI, at publish and against live deployments. [Compare two published
versions](./compare-published-versions.md) and try its mock with
[Mock try-out](./mock-try-out.mdx). The
guides behind the mock engine are under [Mock runtime](/reference/mock-runtime). Hosts and gateways
can discover your imported MCP servers from the [MCP registry endpoint](./mcp-registry.mdx).
xRegistry and CloudEvents tools can read your published schemas and events from the
[xRegistry API](./serve-an-xregistry.md).
The schema registry screens are not yet available.
