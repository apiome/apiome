The **Network** page draws one pacticipant's network: every pacticipant connected to it through
pacts, near or far, and the newest pact of each integration with how its latest verification went.
Use it to see what an application depends on, what depends on it, and where a broken pact sits.
For the terms, see [Pacticipants and versions](./concepts.mdx).

The page only reads. It answers what the Pact Broker's network diagram answers.

## Before you start

- You hold **Pacts** → **View**. Without it, the page shows **Permission denied**.
- The pacticipant is in at least one pact. A consumer [publishes its pacts](./publish-pacts.mdx).

## Open the network

1. In the rail, under **Ship**, click **Contracts**.
2. On the **Pacticipants** page, click a pacticipant.
3. In the **Pacts** section, click **Network diagram**.

The page is at `/ade/dashboard/pacts/pacticipants/<handle>/network`.

## Read the diagram

Each box is a pacticipant. Each arrow is an integration. It points from the consumer to its provider.
Consumers sit on the left and their providers to the right, so a chain of dependencies reads left to
right. The pacticipant you opened is highlighted.

Each arrow is coloured and marked by the verdict of the integration's newest pact:

| Mark | Verdict | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ✓ | **Verified** | The newest result passed. |
| ✕ | **Failed** | The newest result failed. |
| ! | **Failed (pending)** | The newest result failed, but the pact was pending, so the provider's build did not fail. See [Pending and work-in-progress pacts](./pending-and-wip.mdx). |
| ? | **Not verified yet** | No provider has verified the pact. The arrow is dashed. |

The key above the diagram counts the integrations with each verdict.

- **Click an arrow** to open that integration's newest pact. See [Pact detail](./pact-detail.mdx).
- **Click a pacticipant** to open its own network.

You can also move through the diagram with **Tab**: each pacticipant and each arrow can take focus,
and its name says what it is. When two pacticipants consume each other, or a chain loops back, the
arrow that closes the loop is drawn as an arc under the boxes.

## The integrations table

Under the diagram, **Integrations** lists every integration in the network. Each row shows:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Pact** | The consumer and the provider. Click it to open the newest pact. |
| **Latest consumer version** | The consumer version that published the newest pact. A commit SHA is shortened to 12 characters. |
| **Published** | When that pact was published, in UTC. |
| **Verification** | The verdict of its newest result. |

The table is the full list. If a column of the diagram has more than 12 pacticipants, the diagram
draws the first 12, says how many more there are, and leaves their arrows out. The table still lists
those integrations.

## Large networks and reads that fail

The page reads the network outward from the pacticipant you opened, nearest pacticipants first. It
reads the pacts of at most 25 pacticipants. When the network is larger, the pacticipants it did not read are still drawn, with a
dashed outline, and a notice above the diagram names them. Their own integrations further out are
not shown. Open one of them to see its network.

If one pacticipant's pacts cannot be read, the notice names it and gives the reason. It is drawn with
a dashed outline, and the rest of the network still shows. If the pacticipant you opened cannot be
read, the page shows the reason and **Try again**.

If no pact names the pacticipant yet, the page shows the `apiome pact publish` command.

## Who can do what

| Action | Permission |
| --- | --- |
| Open a pacticipant's network | **Pacts** → **View** |

Every read is scoped to your current workspace.
