A **tenant** is a workspace: its own projects, catalog, members, keys and policies. **Open the
workspace switcher at the top of the rail and choose “Manage workspaces…”** (its last row), or press
<Kbd keys="Mod+K"/> and type *Tenants*, to see every tenant you belong to, switch between them,
create a new one, and — for the ones you administer — open the **manage drawer**. The page needs no
current workspace, so it is also where you go when you have none.

<Screenshot id="workspace-menu" alt="The workspace switcher open: the filter, your workspaces, and Manage workspaces… as the last row" />

<Screenshot id="tenants" alt="Tenants: four workspaces with slug, description, your role and status; Northwind is current" />

## The tenants list

Each row shows the tenant's name and slug, its **Description**, **Your role** (**Admin** or
**Member**) and **Status** (**Enabled** or **Disabled**). The current tenant carries a **Current**
badge.

- **Filter by name or slug…** narrows the list; the chips **All**, **You administer** and
  **Enabled** carry their counts.
- **Click a tenant's name** to make it your current tenant (or choose **Select** from the row's
  **⋯** menu). The workspace switcher in the rail does the same from any page.
- **Manage** opens the drawer, and **⋯ → Edit** opens **Edit tenant**. Both appear only for tenants
  you administer; for the others the menu reads *Manage — admins only*.

## Create a tenant

1. **Click “New tenant”** (or press <Kbd keys="N"/>; the switcher's **Create workspace** opens the
   same dialog).
2. In **Create a tenant**, enter the **Organization name** — the **URL slug** is suggested from it;
   edit it if you like (lower-case letters, numbers and dashes).
3. **Click “Continue”.**

You become the new tenant's administrator and it becomes your current tenant.

## Edit a tenant

**Edit tenant** (from the row menu or the drawer's header) changes the **Tenant name**, **Tenant
slug** and **Description**. **Click “Save changes”.** The slug appears in published OpenAPI URLs, so
changing it asks you to confirm the before → after values first.

## The manage drawer

**Click “Manage”** on a tenant you administer. **Manage *tenant*** opens with its sections down
the side; the header shows the slug, the member count and whether it is your current tenant.

<Screenshot id="tenants-drawer-members" alt="Manage Northwind, Members section: five members with their Admin and Member roles, and Add member" />

**Security**, **License & plan**, **MCP settings**, **Per-key capabilities** and **Policy history**
read the *current* tenant only. For any other tenant they say *Select … as your current tenant to view …* —
select it first. **Members** works for every tenant you administer. A section keeps its state while
you look at another, so an unsaved MCP draft survives a glance at the history (the tab shows a dot
while changes are unsaved).

**Done** closes the drawer; **Open full page** goes to [Members](./members.mdx).

### Members

The tenant's people, administrators first, with **Filter by name or email…**.

- **Click “Add member”**, enter the **Email address**, tick **Administrator** to grant that role
  too, and **click “Add member”**.
- **Edit member roles** grants or revokes the administrator role; **click “Save changes”**.
- **Remove** asks to confirm, names the tenant they lose, and warns when the person is also an
  administrator.

You cannot edit or remove your own row. For invitations, seats and custom roles use the
[Members](./members.mdx) and [Roles](./roles.mdx) pages.

### Security

Require every member of the tenant to use two-factor authentication (2FA).

<Screenshot id="tenants-drawer-security" alt="Security: Require two-factor on with a 7-day grace period; 4 members with two-factor, 1 in grace period, 0 overdue" />

1. Turn on **Require two-factor**.
2. Set the **Grace period (days)** — how long a member without 2FA may keep working, from `0` to
   `90` (default `7`). It counts from when you turn the requirement on, or from when a member joins
   if that is later.
3. **Click “Save changes”.** Turning the requirement on starts everyone's grace period now.

While the requirement is on, a line counts the active members **with two-factor**, **in grace
period** and **overdue**; **See who on the Members page** opens [Members](./members.mdx#two-factor-compliance),
where each one is marked.

What members see:

- **During the grace period**, a **Set up two-factor in *N* days** reminder on every page, with a
  link to [their profile](./profile.mdx#turn-it-on). **Dismiss** hides it until they next sign in.
- **After it**, the app opens on **Set up two-factor to continue** instead of any page. **Set up
  two-factor** runs the usual enrolment (Authy, Google Authenticator or any TOTP app); **Sign out**
  is the only other way out. Once enrolled they carry on where they were.

Two-factor protects the whole account, so a person in several workspaces enrols once, by the
earliest deadline. Members who sign in with GitHub, GitLab, Microsoft or Google can comply too. API
keys are not affected. Turning the requirement off and on again restarts every grace period; changing
only the grace period does not. Every change is recorded in the
[access audit](../reference/rest/access.md#list-audit-v1-access-tenant-slug-audit-get) as `two_factor_policy.update`. Only tenant administrators can change it.

### License & plan

<Screenshot id="tenants-drawer-license" alt="License & plan: the Team plan, 9 of 10 member seats used, plan limits and the features with their source" />

- **Current plan** — the plan's name and type. **Upgrade plan** is marked **Coming soon**; contact
  your operator to change plans today.
- **Member seats** — *9 of 10 used*, with a meter that turns to a warning tint from 80 %. At 100 %,
  inviting is blocked until a seat is freed or the plan grows.
- **Plan limits** — **Projects**, **Published versions per project** and **AI assistant requests**;
  *Unlimited* means no cap.
- **Features** — what the plan includes, each **Enabled** or **Disabled**, marked **Included in
  plan** or **Tenant override** (and **Preview** for preview features).

Plans, licences and overrides are set by an operator in the admin console.

### MCP settings

The tenant's MCP policy decides which tools agents may *call*. `tools/list` always returns the full
catalog; the ceiling, defaults and anonymous flags only gate `tools/call`.

<Screenshot id="tenants-drawer-mcp" alt="MCP settings: default mode, capability profile, the anonymous-calls switch and five toolsets with their ceiling badges" />

1. Choose a **Default mode** — **All registry tools**, **Inherit registry defaults** or **Explicit
   per-tool flags**.
2. Optionally pick a **Capability profile** — **Catalog only**, **Search + catalog** or **Full
   read** set the toolset ceilings in one click; **Custom** stays editable afterwards.
3. Turn **Allow anonymous MCP calls** on or off. When on, unauthenticated agents may call the tools
   flagged **Anonymous**.
4. Under **Toolsets**, switch whole toolsets into or out of the ceiling. A card's badge reads **All
   in ceiling**, **Mixed** or **Off**. Tick **Advanced: individual tools** to set **In ceiling**,
   **Default** and **Anonymous** per tool.
5. **Click “Save changes”** in the **Unsaved MCP settings changes** bar (or **Discard**).

Disabling a toolset that active MCP keys use asks first — those agents lose access on their next
call. Only tenant administrators can change MCP options; others see them read-only.

### Per-key capabilities

What one **MCP API key** may call. Each key either **Inherit tenant defaults** or has a **Custom
enable-set**, capped by the tenant ceiling.

<Screenshot id="tenants-drawer-keys" alt="Per-key capabilities: the Support copilot key on a custom enable-set, with its toolsets and tools switched on" />

1. **Choose the key** under **MCP API key**.
2. Choose a **Capability mode**. With **Custom enable-set**, switch toolsets and tools on or off; a
   tool outside the tenant ceiling is locked.
3. Check **Effective summary** — how many tools the key can call, and which are denied.
4. **Click “Save capabilities”** (or **Discard**).

To issue a key, **click “Create MCP key”**, enter a **Label** (for example `Prod agent`) and create
it; **MCP API key created** shows the secret once — copy it and **click “I've saved my key”**. A new
key inherits the tenant policy. Revoked keys are listed but cannot be edited.

### Policy history

Every saved change to MCP settings and key capabilities, newest first: **When**, **Actor** and
**Change**. **Expand a row** to see its **Settings changes** and **Tool-flag changes** as before →
after values. **Refresh** reloads the list.

<Screenshot id="tenants-drawer-history" alt="Policy history: two changes, the newest expanded to show the default mode changed from all to inherit_registry" />

## With the API and the CLI

| Section | Endpoints |
| --- | --- |
| Security | `GET` / `PUT /v1/tenants/{tenant}/two-factor-policy` — see the [reference](../reference/rest/access.md#get-two-factor-policy-v1-tenants-tenant-slug-two-factor-policy-get) |
| License & plan | `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant}/license` |
| MCP settings | `GET` / `PUT /v1/tenants/{tenant}/mcp-policy` |
| Policy history | `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant}/mcp-policy/history` |
| Per-key capabilities | `GET` / `POST /v1/tenants/{tenant}/mcp-keys`, `GET` / `PATCH` / `DELETE …/mcp-keys/{key}`, `PUT …/mcp-keys/{key}/capabilities`, `POST …/capabilities/preview` |

See the [API reference](../reference/api-reference.md). `apiome auth tenants` lists the tenants you
can reach (`GET /v1/tenants/me`) — see the [CLI quick-start](../reference/cli-quickstart.md).
