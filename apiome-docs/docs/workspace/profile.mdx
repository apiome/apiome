**Profile** is your own account: the name teammates see, your password, two-factor authentication,
the ways you can sign in and your current session. **Open the user menu at the foot of the rail and
choose Profile.** The tabs under the title switch between **Profile**, **Linked accounts** and
**Preferences** (<Kbd keys="Mod+," />).

<Screenshot id="profile" alt="Profile: Priya Raman's identity card with 2FA on, account details, sign-in methods and session" />

Every signed-in user has this page; nothing on it needs a role.

## Your identity

The card at the top shows your name, email, the workspace you are in with your role, and **2FA on** or
**2FA off**. **Account details** below it lists:

| Tile | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Full name** | The name shown to teammates and in activity |
| **Email** | Your sign-in address, marked **Verified** once confirmed |
| **User ID** | Your account id, with a copy button |
| **Current tenant** | The workspace you are working in and its id, with a copy button |
| **Last login** | When you last signed in |

### Change your name

1. **Click “Edit name”** at the top of the page (or the pencil on the **Full name** tile).
2. Type your **Full name**.
3. **Click “Save”**. The rail's user menu changes at once.

Your email cannot be changed here.

## Change your password

1. **Click “Change password”** at the foot of the **Security** card.
2. Enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter a **New password**. It needs at least 8 characters, one uppercase and one lowercase letter,
   and one number or special character; the meter under the field reads *Weak*, *Fair* or *Strong*.
4. Type it again in **Confirm new password**.
5. **Click “Change password”**. *Password changed successfully.* appears above the page.

<Screenshot id="profile-change-password" alt="The Change password dialog with its requirements, current password and new password filled in" />

If you signed up with an identity provider and never set a password, sign in with that provider — see
[Linked accounts](./linked-accounts.mdx).

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from an authenticator app — Authy, Google
Authenticator or any TOTP app — after your password. It is set up in the **Security** card.

<Screenshot id="profile-security" alt="The Security card with two-factor enabled: sign-in methods, email code, backup codes, trusted device and recovery note" />

### Turn it on

1. **Click “Enable 2FA”** in the **Security** card.
2. Enter your **Current password** and **click “Continue”**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you cannot scan it, copy the `otpauth://` link
   under **Or enter this URI** into the app instead.
4. Type the six-digit **Authentication code** the app shows and **click “Confirm and enable”**.
5. Save your backup codes: **click “Copy codes”** or **“Download .txt”**, keep them somewhere safe,
   then **click “Done”**.

<Screenshot id="profile-2fa-scan" alt="Enable two-factor authentication, step 2: the QR code, the otpauth URI and a six-digit code entered" />

<Screenshot id="profile-2fa-backup-codes" alt="Enable two-factor authentication, step 3: ten one-time backup codes with Copy codes and Download .txt" />

The codes are shown once. Each one signs you in a single time when you do not have your
authenticator. The next time you sign in, Apiome asks for a code — see
[Pass the two-factor check](../getting-started/sign-in.mdx#pass-the-two-factor-check).

### Once it is on

The **Security** card then shows:

- **Sign-in methods** — **Authenticator app (TOTP)**, and **Email OTP** when your deployment can send
  email. An emailed code needs no set-up: you can ask for one at sign-in instead of using the app.
- **Backup codes** — how many are left (*8 remaining*).
- **Trusted device** — whether this browser is trusted, which skips 2FA when you sign in here for
  about 30 days. **Click “Forget this device”** to be asked for a code in this browser again.
- **Recovery** — if you lose your authenticator, use a backup code at sign-in or ask an admin, then
  make a new set of codes.

### Make new backup codes

1. **Click “Regenerate backup codes”**.
2. Enter your **Current password** and **click “Regenerate”**. Your old codes stop working at once.
3. Copy or download the new codes and **click “Done”**.

### Turn it off

1. **Click “Disable 2FA”**.
2. Enter your **Current password** and **click “Disable 2FA”**.

Your backup codes and this browser's trusted-device mark are cleared too. You can turn 2FA on again
at any time.

## Sign-in methods

The **Sign-in methods** card lists every way you can sign in: **Password** (when you have one) and each
linked provider, such as GitHub. **Click “Manage linked accounts”** to add or remove providers on
[Linked accounts](./linked-accounts.mdx).

## Your session

The **Session** card shows when your current sign-in expires, how much of its 30 days is left, and
the browser and system you are using.

**Click “Sign out everywhere”** to end every session on every device, including this one; you land
on the sign-in page. Use it if you signed in on a shared computer or think someone else has your
password.

## With the API

Profile, password and two-factor settings are only in the app; there is no REST endpoint or CLI
command for them. To call Apiome from scripts, create an [API key](./api-keys.mdx).
