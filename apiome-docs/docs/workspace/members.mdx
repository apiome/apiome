**Members** lists everyone who can sign in to the workspace — and the invitations that have not
been accepted yet — with their role, status and when they were last active. It is also where you
see how many of the plan's **member seats** are taken. **Go to Workspace → Members**, or choose
**Invite a teammate** on Home.

<Screenshot id="members" alt="Members: one summary line — 9 members, active, pending, suspended, 9 of 10 seats used, Team plan — with a thin seat bar, then a roster of nine people with role, status, last active and joined columns" />

Every member can read the page. Inviting needs `members:create`, changing a role or suspending
needs `members:edit`, and offboarding needs `members:delete` — workspace administrators hold all
three. Without them the page is the same, with fewer controls: roles show as plain badges and the
row buttons are gone. What each role grants is set on [Roles & permissions](./roles.mdx).

## The summary line and member seats

One line under the header counts the workspace: how many **members**, how many are **active**,
**pending** and **suspended**, then the plan's **seats used** — *9 of 10 seats used* — and the plan
(for example **Team plan**). A pending invitation holds its seat until it is accepted or cancelled,
and a suspended member keeps theirs. On a plan with unlimited seats the line reads *unlimited seats*.

Once 80 % of the seats are taken, a thin bar under the line shows how full the plan is.

When every seat is taken the page warns **All member seats are in use** and **Invite member** is
not shown. Free a seat by cancelling an invitation or offboarding someone, or change the plan (see
[Tenants](./tenants.mdx)).

## Find people

- **Filter by name or email…** narrows the roster as you type.
- The chips — **All**, **Active**, **Pending**, **Suspended** and **Admins** — each show their count.
- **User**, **Status**, **Last active** and **Joined** sort; click the header again to reverse.

The header reads *9 members · 2 pending*, and the table foot counts every state — *9 people · 6
active · 2 pending · 1 suspended*. Your own row is marked **(you)**, and none of its controls work:
you cannot change your own role, suspend or offboard yourself.

## Invite a member

1. **Click “Invite member”** (or press <Kbd keys="N" /> on the page).
2. Type their **Email address**. They must already have an Apiome account — invitations are not
   emailed.
3. Pick a **Role**, or leave **Default role** for the workspace's default.
4. Check **Seats after this invite**, then **click “Send invite”**.

<Screenshot id="members-invite-dialog" alt="The Invite member dialog: an email address, the Editor role, and seats after this invite at 10 of 10" />

The person appears as a tinted **Pending** row with an envelope and *Invited Jun 13, 2026*. Their
membership becomes active the next time they sign in. To nudge someone, **click “Resend”** on
their row — Apiome renews the invitation and records it in the access ledger; it still sends no
email. To withdraw it, **click the bin** and **“Cancel invite”** (or **“Keep invitation”** to back
out); the seat is returned to the licence.

## Change a role

**Choose a role from the row's Role menu.** The change is saved at once and recorded in the
workspace's [access audit](../reference/rest/access.md#list-audit-v1-access-tenant-slug-audit-get). If it fails, a banner above the roster says why.

## Read a member

**Click a row** to open the member's drawer:

- status, role and an **Admin** badge for workspace administrators;
- **Membership** — role (changeable here too), status and since when, last active, joined,
  **Two-factor** (**Enabled** or **Not enabled**) and the user id;
- **Effective permissions** — the `resource:action` grants their role gives, with a link to that
  role;
- **Recent access activity** — the latest access-ledger entries by or about them.

The footer has **Offboard** (or **Cancel invite**), **Resend** for an invitation, **Suspend** or
**Reinstate**, and **Audit trail**, which opens the full ledger. Owners also see **Reset
two-factor** for a member who has two-factor on.

<Screenshot id="members-drawer" alt="Sam Okafor's drawer: active Release manager with two-factor enabled, their effective permissions and recent access activity" />

## Reset a member's two-factor

A member who has lost their authenticator and their backup codes cannot sign in. As an **owner** of
the workspace you can reset their two-factor so they can.

Confirm who you are dealing with before you do this.

1. **Click the member's row** to open their drawer, then **click “Reset two-factor”**. The button
   appears only for owners, and only for an active member who has two-factor on.
2. Enter a **Reason** of at least 10 characters. It is kept in the audit trail; the member does not
   see it.
3. **Type the member's email address** to confirm, and **click “Reset two-factor”**.

The member's authenticator and backup codes stop working, their trusted devices are forgotten and
they are signed out everywhere. The dialog says whether they were emailed. The reset is recorded in the
[access audit](../reference/rest/access.md#list-audit-v1-access-tenant-slug-audit-get) with you, the member and your reason.

**A member who also belongs to another workspace cannot be reset here.** Two-factor protects their
whole account, not only their place in your workspace, so the dialog answers *This member also
belongs to another workspace…* and the reset has to be done by an administrator of your Apiome
installation. You also cannot reset your own two-factor here — use your
[profile](./profile.mdx#turn-it-off).

## Suspend and reinstate

Suspending stops someone signing in without removing them — for leave, or while an incident is
looked into.

1. **Click the power button** on their row (or **“Suspend”** in the drawer).
2. Read the confirmation — *They keep their seat but cannot sign in to this workspace until
   reinstated.* — and **click “Suspend”**.

<Screenshot id="members-suspend-dialog" alt="The confirmation: Suspend Elena Vasquez? with Cancel and Suspend" />

A suspended row is dimmed and offers **Reinstate**; confirming lets them sign in again. If the
licence has no seat left, the dialog stays open and says so.

## Offboard a member

Offboarding removes someone's membership, role and any administrator rights at once.

1. **Click the bin** on their row (or **“Offboard”** in the drawer).
2. **Click “Offboard”** to confirm.

They lose all access immediately and their seat is returned to the licence. Their projects,
versions and audit entries stay in the workspace. Offboarding an administrator adds a warning that
counts the administrators who would remain — and if none would, it tells you to promote someone
else first.

## With the API

The page calls the access API, scoped to the workspace — see the
[API reference](../reference/api-reference.md):

| Call | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/access/{tenant}/members` | The roster |
| `POST /v1/access/{tenant}/members` | Invite (`email`, optional `role_id`) |
| `PATCH /v1/access/{tenant}/members/{user_id}` | Change `role_id` or `status` (`active`, `suspended`) |
| `POST /v1/access/{tenant}/members/{user_id}/resend-invite` | Renew a pending invitation |
| `DELETE /v1/access/{tenant}/members/{user_id}` | Offboard, or cancel an invitation |
| `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant}/license` | Plan and seat usage |

There is no CLI command for members.
