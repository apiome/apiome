Apiome tells you when something concerns you: somebody **mentions** you in a comment, asks for your
**review**, answers a review you requested, **resolves** a thread you took part in, or **publishes**
a version you collaborated on. Notifications belong to you and to the current workspace — switching
workspace shows that workspace's inbox.

| Type | You get one when… | Reads like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mentions** | somebody names you in a comment | *Marcus Lee mentioned you in a comment*, with the comment's first words |
| **Review requests** | you are asked to review a version | *Priya Raman requested your review* |
| **Review decisions** | a reviewer answers a review you requested | *Sam Okafor approved your review* or *… requested changes on your review* |
| **Resolved threads** | a thread you took part in is resolved | *Elena Vasquez resolved a thread you took part in* |
| **Publishes** | a version you collaborated on is published | *Priya Raman published 1.8.2* |

## The bell

**Notifications** sits at the foot of the sidebar, above **Help & docs**. A dot on the bell and a
count beside it show how many are unread (over 99 reads *99+*); with the sidebar collapsed, the dot
stays and the tooltip says the count. The count refreshes every minute while the tab is visible.

**Click the bell** (or focus it and press <Kbd keys="↓"/>) to open the latest twenty, grouped under
**Today**, **Yesterday**, **Earlier this week** and **Older**. Unread rows carry a dot.

- **Click a notification** to go to it and mark it read: a mention or resolved thread opens the
  thread on the project's **Discussion** tab, a review request or decision opens the review, and a
  publish opens the project's **Versions**.
- **“Mark all read”** clears the count without closing the menu.
- **“See all notifications”** opens the notifications page.

## The notifications page

**Click “See all notifications”**, or go to `/ade/dashboard/notifications`. The page lists your whole
inbox, newest first, under the same time headings, fifty at a time — **“Load more”** fetches the
next fifty.

<Screenshot id="notifications" alt="The Notifications page: a review request, two mentions, an approval, a publish and a resolved thread" />

### Filter

- **Unread only** shows only what you have not read; its count is your unread total.
- **All types** or one type — **Mentions**, **Review requests**, **Review decisions**, **Resolved
  threads**, **Publishes** — each with its unread count. Click a selected type again to clear it.

The two combine: *Unread only* with *Mentions* lists the mentions you still have to read.

<Screenshot id="notifications-filtered" alt="The page filtered to Unread only and Mentions, leaving two unread mentions" />

### Mark as read

- **Click a notification** to open it; it is marked read on the way.
- **Click the double tick** at the end of an unread row to mark just that one.
- **“Mark all read”** marks the whole inbox.

**Refresh** reads the inbox again. A notification whose project or version was deleted keeps its
sentence but is no longer a link.

## Choose what you see

**Click “Change what you see”** on the notifications page, or open
[Preferences](./preferences.mdx#notifications) (<Kbd keys="Mod+,"/>) on its **Notifications** tab,
and switch types off. A switched-off type disappears from the bell's count, the menu, the page and
its filters, and the page says so — *One notification type is switched off on this device.* Nothing
is deleted: turn the type back on and its history returns. With every type off, the page reads
*Every notification type is switched off* and offers **“Open notification preferences”**.

<Screenshot id="preferences-notifications" alt="The Preferences pane's Notifications tab with Publishes switched off, over the notifications page" />

The switches are kept in this browser, so they apply on this device only. Notifications are in-app:
email digests and chat delivery are not available yet.

## With the API

Your inbox is also available over REST, for the caller's own notifications in a workspace:

| Endpoint | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant_slug}/notifications` | A page of the inbox, newest first — `unread`, `type`, `limit`, `offset` |
| `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant_slug}/notifications/unread-count` | The unread total and the count per type |
| `POST /v1/tenants/{tenant_slug}/notifications/read` | Mark `{"ids": [...]}` or `{"all": true}` read |

See the [API reference](../reference/api-reference.md). There is no CLI command for notifications.
