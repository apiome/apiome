**Preferences** change how Apiome looks and behaves for you. They are personal and stored in this
browser: they apply to every workspace you open on this device, and nobody else sees them.
[Keyboard and preferences](../getting-started/keyboard-and-preferences.mdx) is the two-minute
version; this page covers every setting.

## Open Preferences

- **Press <Kbd keys="Mod+,"/>** anywhere, or
- **click Preferences** at the foot of the sidebar, or
- **open your user menu and choose “Preferences”**.

The pane opens on the right with four tabs — **Appearance**, **Account**, **Notifications** and
**Shortcuts**. Every change applies and saves the moment you make it (*Saved automatically*);
**click “Done”**, the close button or press <Kbd keys="Esc"/> to close it.

## Appearance

### Theme

Pick one of nine cards. A theme is a token swap — surfaces, ink and accent change; layout and type
never do — so every screen works the same in all of them. **Follow system** is selected until you
choose another, and its card says which theme it is using right now (*Currently: Light*).

### Font size

The slider scales the whole interface, not just body text, in six steps. The chip beside the title
reads the current step (*Default · 16px*), and the **Preview** card under the slider is drawn at
that size.

| Step | Base size |
| --- | --- |
| **Small** | 14 px |
| **Compact** | 15 px |
| **Default** | 16 px |
| **Large** | 17 px |
| **Larger** | 18 px |
| **Largest** | 20 px |

### Density

**Comfortable** (the default) gives rows, controls and page padding more room. **Compact** tightens
them for large screens, so more rows fit. Arrow keys move between the two.

### Switches

| Switch | Off (default) | On |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reduce motion** | Menus, dialogs and the rail animate — unless your operating system asks for less | Transitions and animated progress are off |
| **Collapse sidebar by default** | The sidebar opens with labels | The sidebar starts as an icon rail; hover to peek at labels |

| Switch | On (default) | Off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monospace for identifiers** | Ids, hashes and versions are set in JetBrains Mono | They use the body font |
| **Show keyboard hints** | Shortcut chips appear on buttons and menus | The chips are hidden; the shortcuts still work |

**Reduce motion** follows your operating system's *reduce motion* setting until you turn it on
here. <Kbd keys="Mod+\"/> (or the handle on the sidebar's edge) flips **Collapse sidebar by
default** too, so the sidebar stays the way you left it.

## Account

Account details live on their own pages, so there is one place to change each of them. The tab
links to:

- **Profile** — name, email, password and two-factor authentication ([Profile & security](./profile.mdx));
- **Linked accounts** — the identity providers connected to your account ([Linked accounts](./linked-accounts.mdx)).

## Notifications

**In-app notifications** has one switch per kind of notification — **Mentions**, **Review
requests**, **Review decisions**, **Resolved threads** and **Publishes**. Turning a type off hides
it from the bell's count and list and from the notifications page; nothing is discarded, so turning
it back on brings its history back. The switches apply to this device only. Email digests and chat
delivery are not available yet.

<Screenshot id="preferences-notifications" alt="The Notifications tab with Publishes switched off and the other four types on" />

See [Notifications](./notifications.mdx) for the bell and the notifications page.

## Shortcuts

**Everywhere** lists the shortcuts that work on every screen, and **“All keyboard shortcuts”**
(<Kbd keys="?"/>) closes the pane and opens the full sheet for the screen behind it. See
[Help & shortcuts](./help.mdx#keyboard-shortcuts).

## Where preferences are kept

Preferences are saved in this browser's local storage (`hive.theme`, `hive.fontScale`,
`hive.density`, `hive.motion`, `hive.rail`, `hive.monoIds`, `hive.kbdHints` and the
`hive.notify.*` switches), so another browser or device starts from the defaults. Clearing site
data resets them. There is no API for them.

[Accessibility](./accessibility.md) covers what each theme and setting does for screen readers,
low vision and motion sensitivity.
