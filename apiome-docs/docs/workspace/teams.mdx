A **team** is a named group of workspace members — *Payments*, *Platform*, *Identity* — and the
links people use to reach it: a contact e-mail, a chat channel, an on-call schedule and its
documentation. Teams are the [owners](#ownership) of projects, catalog items, MCP endpoints,
consumers and connectors. A team is the only kind of group in a workspace.

Teams are managed over the [REST API](../reference/rest/teams.md) today; the Teams screen and the
`apiome teams` command come later.

## What a team holds

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `slug` | The team's handle, for example `payments`: lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. Made from the name when you leave it out. **It cannot be changed** — gateway tags such as `owner:payments` and the `x-apiome-owner` extension refer to it |
| `name` | What people read, for example *Payments* |
| `description` | What the team does (optional) |
| `contact_email` | A shared mailbox (optional) |
| `chat_url`, `oncall_url`, `docs_url` | Links to the team's chat channel, on-call schedule and documentation (optional; each must start with `http://` or `https://`) |

Each slug is unique among the workspace's teams. Once a team is deleted, its slug is free to use
again.

## Members

Each member is a **lead** or a **member**. Everyone on a team must already be a member of the
workspace — invite them on [Members & seats](./members.mdx) first.

Setting the members always sends the **whole list**: anyone left out is removed, and an empty list
empties the team. If someone on the list is not in the workspace, nothing changes and the reply
names them.

When someone leaves the workspace, they leave every team they were on at the same moment.

## Delete a team

Deleting a team removes it from every list, and its slug is free again. Its history stays in the
access audit.

A team that is the confirmed [owner](#ownership) of anything cannot simply be deleted: the request is refused with how many
entities it owns. Name another team with `reassign_to` and those entities move to it as the team is
deleted.

## Ownership

Every project, catalog item, MCP endpoint, consumer and connector can have an **owner**: a team.
An owner is either:

- **Confirmed** — the team responsible for the entity. Findings about the entity are routed to it.
  There is at most one.
- **Suggested** — a team an importer found evidence for. Each suggestion shows where it came from,
  and nothing is ever sent to a suggested owner. There can be several.

A suggestion only becomes the owner when someone confirms it, and the access audit records who did.

### Where suggestions come from

| Source | Evidence | Becomes a suggestion for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `gateway_tag` | A Kong tag `owner:payments`, or an AWS API Gateway tag `apiome:owner` = `payments` (see [Connectors](../bring-in/connectors.mdx)) | The catalog item the connector synced |
| `codeowners` | A CODEOWNERS rule naming `@your-org/payments` for the spec's path — the file in `.github/`, the repository root or `docs/`, last matching rule wins | The project or catalog item imported from that path |
| `spec_extension` | `x-apiome-owner: payments` at the root of the document, or `info.contact.name: payments` | The project or catalog item the import wrote |

Only team slugs count. A suggestion naming a team that does not exist is not stored. A connector
says so in its run's findings — *unknown team `ghost` in tag* — so you can create the team or fix the
tag. CODEOWNERS owners that are people (`@alice`) or e-mail addresses are not suggestions.
Suggestions refresh on every sync, scan and import: when the tag, rule or extension goes away, so
does the suggestion.

### Confirm or assign an owner

- **Confirm a suggestion:** `POST /v1/tenants/{tenant}/owners/{kind}/{id}/confirm`, with
  `{"team": "payments"}` — or `{}` when only one team is suggested. The owner keeps its source and
  records who confirmed it.
- **Assign any team:** `PUT /v1/tenants/{tenant}/owners/{kind}/{id}` with `{"team": "platform"}`.
- **Assign one team to many entities:** `POST /v1/tenants/{tenant}/owners:assign` with
  `{"team": "platform", "entities": [{"kind": "catalog_item", "id": "…"}]}` (up to 200; all or
  nothing).
- **Remove the owner:** `DELETE /v1/tenants/{tenant}/owners/{kind}/{id}`. Suggestions stay, and an
  owner that came from a suggestion becomes a suggestion again.
- **See an entity's owner and suggestions:** `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant}/owners/{kind}/{id}`.

`kind` is `project`, `catalog_item`, `mcp_endpoint`, `consumer` or `connector`. Who may change an
owner is whoever may edit the entity: `projects:edit` for projects, catalog items and MCP endpoints,
`consumer_contracts:edit` for consumers and `imports:edit` for connectors. Reading needs the matching
`view`. Every change is in the access audit as `owner.assign`, `owner.confirm`, `owner.remove`,
`owner.bulk_assign`, or — when an importer changes the suggestions — `owner.suggestions`.

### What an owner team hears about

Lint regressions, breaking changes on refresh, verification drift, failing deploy gates, MCP trust
drift, shadow APIs and failed connector syncs reach the **confirmed** owner team: a notification for
each member and one message to the team's `chat_url`. See
[Notifications → Owner findings](./notifications.mdx#owner-findings). Without a confirmed owner the
finding goes to the **Unowned findings** report instead.

The catalog, project, MCP endpoint and consumer lists show each entity's owner as
`owner: {team, status, source}`: the confirmed owner, or else its first suggestion with
`status: "suggested"`. See the [Owners API reference](../reference/rest/owners.md).

## Permissions

Teams have their own row in the [permission matrix](./roles.mdx#the-permission-matrix), **Teams**
(`teams`):

| Role | Teams permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner**, **Admin** | View, create, edit (including members) and delete |
| **Editor**, **Viewer** | View |

A team decides who is told about — and paged for — what it owns, so changing teams is kept with
the people who run the workspace. Give a custom role `teams:edit` to let it manage membership.

Every change is recorded in the workspace's
[access audit](../reference/rest/access.md#list-audit-v1-access-tenant-slug-audit-get) as
`team.create`, `team.update` (each changed field, old and new), `team.members.replace` (who was
added, removed or changed role) or `team.delete`.

## With the API

See the [Teams API reference](../reference/rest/teams.md):

| Call | Does | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant}/teams` | Every team, with its links and member count | `teams:view` |
| `POST /v1/tenants/{tenant}/teams` | Create a team | `teams:create` |
| `GET /v1/tenants/{tenant}/teams/{slug}` | One team and its members, leads first | `teams:view` |
| `PATCH /v1/tenants/{tenant}/teams/{slug}` | Change the name, description or links; `null` clears one | `teams:edit` |
| `PUT /v1/tenants/{tenant}/teams/{slug}/members` | Replace the members (`[{"user_id", "role"}]`) | `teams:edit` |
| `DELETE /v1/tenants/{tenant}/teams/{slug}` | Delete; add `?reassign_to=<slug>` when it owns entities | `teams:delete` |

For example, to create a team and make yourself its lead:

```bash
curl -X POST "$APIOME/v1/tenants/acme/teams" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name": "Payments", "chat_url": "https://chat.example.com/c/payments"}'

curl -X PUT "$APIOME/v1/tenants/acme/teams/payments/members" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"members": [{"user_id": "<your user id>", "role": "lead"}]}'
```

What a refusal means:

| Status | `detail.code` | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `team-invalid` | A malformed slug, a blank name, a link that is not http(s), a bad e-mail, or a user listed twice |
| 400 | `team-reassign-invalid` | `reassign_to` names the team being deleted, or no team |
| 404 | `team-not-found` | No team has that slug |
| 409 | `team-slug-taken` | Another team already has that slug |
| 409 | `team-owns-entities` | The team owns entities; `detail.owned_count` says how many |
| 422 | `team-member-not-in-tenant` | Someone on the list is not a workspace member |
