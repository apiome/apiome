Workspace & account covers who can do what in a workspace and how Apiome works for you.

**Running the workspace** — invite people and manage seats on [Members & seats](./members.mdx),
decide what each role may do on [Roles & permissions](./roles.mdx), issue scoped keys for scripts,
CI and agents on [API keys & agent keys](./api-keys.mdx), and set a workspace's plan, limits and MCP
policy in [Tenants & the manage drawer](./tenants.mdx).

**Your account** — your password, two-factor sign-in and sessions on
[Profile & security](./profile.mdx), the sign-in providers you have linked on
[Linked accounts](./linked-accounts.mdx), theme, text size, density and motion in
[Preferences](./preferences.mdx), and what reaches you in [Notifications](./notifications.mdx).

**Finding your way** — [Help & shortcuts](./help.mdx) searches the guide and lists every keyboard
shortcut; [Keyboard](./keyboard.md) gives the keyboard path for every task and
[Accessibility](./accessibility.md) the accessibility contract.

Every change made on these screens is recorded in the [Access audit](../govern/access-audit.mdx).
