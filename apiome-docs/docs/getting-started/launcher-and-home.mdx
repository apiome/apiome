## The launcher

<Route path="/ade" />

After you sign in, Apiome opens the **launcher**. It greets you, names your workspace and your role
in it, and counts its projects and published versions.

<Screenshot id="launcher" alt="The launcher: Your API specification workspace, with Control Panel, Designer Suite, Developer Suite and Browser cards" />

- **Applications** — **Control Panel** is where everything in this guide happens. **Browser**
  opens the public catalog of published specs in a new tab. Other cards appear when your plan
  includes them.
- **Resources** — **Help & docs** opens the in-app help, with a search over these pages.
- The top bar holds **Preferences**, your profile and **Sign out**.

**Click “Control Panel”** to open Home.

## Home

<Route path="/ade/dashboard" />

Home is the Control Panel's first page: what is moving in your workspace, and what needs you.

<Screenshot id="home" alt="Home: a getting-started checklist, six workspace statistics, Pinned projects, Pick up where you left off, Quick actions and Needs attention" />

From top to bottom:

1. **The header** greets you and sums up the workspace. **“Import a spec”** and **“New project”**
   start the two ways to add an API.
2. **Get to your first published spec** — a checklist that follows the rest of this guide: **Cut a
   version**, **Publish it**, **View it in Browse** (with two Designer steps first when your plan
   includes the Designer). The button on the next step takes you there. Dismiss it with **×**.
3. **Statistics** — tenants, projects, versions, published versions, classes and properties.
4. **Pinned projects** — the projects you pinned, newest first, up to eight. Click one to open its
   versions, or click its pin button to unpin it. See [Pinned projects](#pinned-projects).
5. **Pick up where you left off** — a card per project someone in your workspaces recently changed,
   with its latest version and quality score. Click one to open its versions.
6. **Recently viewed** — the projects *you* last opened in this workspace, most recent first, up to
   five. Each row shows the latest version, when you last viewed it and, after the first visit, how
   many times. Click one to open its versions. See [Recently viewed](#recently-viewed).
7. **Recent activity** — your latest changes.
8. **Quick actions** — **Import a spec**, **Browse the catalog**, **Create an API key** and **Invite
   a teammate**.
9. **Needs attention** — blocking lint findings, expiring API keys, versions near their sunset and
   reviews waiting on you. It is hidden when nothing needs you.
10. **Time spent** — how long *you* spent on each project in the last 7 or 30 days. See
    [Time spent](#time-spent).

The sidebar groups the rest of the Control Panel by job: **Build**, **Bring in**, **Ship**,
**Govern**, and your **Workspace** settings. **Primitives & types**, **MCP servers**, **Export studio**,
**Style guides** and **Access audit** are marked as coming soon: they are not yet available and cannot
be opened.

**Click a group's heading** to fold it away, and click it again to open it. A folded heading shows how
many pages it holds, and the sidebar remembers your choice on this device. **Workspace** starts folded,
so the sidebar fits a laptop screen without scrolling. Click **Workspace** to reach **Members**,
**Roles**, **Teams**, **API keys** and **Schema registry**.

## Pinned projects

Pin the projects you come back to most, and they stay at the top of Home.

1. **Browse to Projects**.
2. On a project's card or row, **open its menu (⋯)** and **choose “Pin to Home”**.
3. Back on Home, the project is listed under **Pinned projects**.

To unpin, click the pin button at the end of its row on Home, or **choose “Unpin from Home”** in its
menu on **Projects**.

Pinned projects are also your **favorites**: they are listed under **Favorite projects** on your
[Profile](../workspace/profile.mdx#favorite-projects) and lead the command palette
(<Kbd keys="Mod+K"/>) under **Favorites**.

You can pin up to **8** projects in each workspace; the panel's footer counts them. Pinning a ninth
says *You can pin up to 8 projects. Unpin one on Home first.* Pins are yours alone and follow you
across browsers and devices. A project moved to the trash leaves the list, and comes back if it is
restored.

The REST API reads and changes the same pins:
[`GET /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/pinned`](../reference/rest/projects.md#list-pinned-projects-v1-projects-tenant-slug-pinned-get)
lists them, [`PUT /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/{project_id}/pin`](../reference/rest/projects.md#pin-project-v1-projects-tenant-slug-project-id-pin-put)
pins a project (409 `pin_limit_reached` past eight) and
[`DELETE /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/{project_id}/pin`](../reference/rest/projects.md#unpin-project-v1-projects-tenant-slug-project-id-pin-delete)
unpins it.

## Time spent

**Time spent** shows where your own time in this workspace went: a bar for each of your five busiest
projects, longest first, with the hours and minutes beside it, and your total across every project
underneath. **Click “7 days” or “30 days”** to switch the window; days are counted in UTC and
include today. **Click a project** to open its versions.

<Screenshot id="home-time-spent" alt="The Time spent panel on Home: 7 days selected, a bar each for Payments API and Orders Service, and 3h 00m in total" />

Time is counted while a project is open on **Versions**, the browser tab is visible and you have
used the page — clicked, typed, scrolled — in the last five minutes. A tab left open in the
background, or a page you walked away from, stops counting until you come back. Two tabs on the
same project count once. It is a measure of attention, not a timesheet.

Your time is yours alone: nobody else in the workspace, including admins, can see it. Scripts can
read the same figures with
[`GET /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/time-spent?days=7`](../reference/rest/projects.md#get-time-spent-v1-projects-tenant-slug-time-spent-get)
(1–90 days).

## Recently viewed

A project is added to **Recently viewed** when you open it: when you **click a project** on
**Projects**, a card or row on Home, a catalog item's **“Open version history”**, or **pick it in the
project selector** on **Versions**. Arriving on **Versions** without choosing a project — when it
shows the first project for you — does not count.

The list is yours alone: other members of the workspace never see what you opened. It follows you
across browsers and devices, and lists only the workspace you are in. A project moved to the trash
leaves the list, and comes back if it is restored.

Scripts and integrations can read and clear the same history through the REST API:
[`GET /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/recently-viewed`](../reference/rest/projects.md#list-recently-viewed-projects-v1-projects-tenant-slug-recently-viewed-get)
lists it, [`POST /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/{project_id}/views`](../reference/rest/projects.md#record-project-view-v1-projects-tenant-slug-project-id-views-post)
records a view, and [`DELETE /v1/projects/{tenant_slug}/recently-viewed`](../reference/rest/projects.md#clear-recently-viewed-projects-v1-projects-tenant-slug-recently-viewed-delete)
clears it.
