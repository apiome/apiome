Two screens get you everywhere in Apiome. The **launcher** (`/ade`) is where you start, and it shows
only what you can open. The **sidebar** of the Control Panel lists every page, grouped by job. It
fits a 1440 × 900 window without scrolling, and nothing is hidden. Epic 12 (#5595) gave both their
current shape. This page shows where each destination lives.

## The launcher

<Route path="/ade" />

<Screenshot id="launcher" alt="The launcher: Control Panel, Designer Suite and Browser cards, a Coming next line, Resources and Recently shipped" />

- **Applications** shows only cards that open. **Control Panel** is emphasised, **Browser** opens
  the public catalog, and your plan can add more cards.
- **Coming next** is one line of text. It names products that are not available yet. They are not
  buttons. **Click “Follow in What’s new”** to read the release notes.
- **Recently shipped** shows the three newest entries in **What's new**.
- The build you are running, for example `v0.661.0 RC8`, sits in the footer. **Click it** to open
  **What's new**.

[The launcher and Home](./launcher-and-home.mdx) covers every card.

## The sidebar

<Route path="/ade/dashboard" />

From top to bottom:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| *(no heading)* | **Home** |
| **Build** | **Projects**, **Primitives & types** |
| **Bring in** | **Catalog**, **Repositories**, **Connectors**, **MCP servers** |
| **Ship** | **Published**, **Sunset timeline**, **Export studio** |
| **Govern** | **Style guides**, **Lint posture**, **Shadow APIs**, **Dependency graph**, **Access audit** |
| **Workspace** | **Members**, **Roles**, **Teams**, **API keys**, **Schema registry** |

- **Click a group's heading** to fold it. Click it again to open it. A folded heading shows how many
  pages it holds. The sidebar remembers your choice on this device.
- **Workspace** starts folded, because you visit it less often than the other groups. That is what
  lets the sidebar fit a 900 px tall window.
- A small honey dot after a page's name means the page is in **Preview**. **Lint posture** has one.
  Hover the dot to read *Preview*. A screen reader announces the link as “Lint posture, preview”.
- Press <Kbd keys="Mod+\"/> to collapse the sidebar to icons, and press it again to expand it. In
  the icon rail every label moves to a tooltip. See [Preferences](../workspace/preferences.mdx).

### The footer

Four rows sit at the foot of the sidebar, in this order:

1. **Notifications** shows a count of your unread notifications. Over 99 it reads *99+*. In the icon
   rail the count becomes a dot on the bell. See [Notifications](../workspace/notifications.mdx).
2. **Help & docs** opens the in-app help. See [Help](../workspace/help.mdx).
3. **Preferences** (<Kbd keys="Mod+,"/>) opens theme, density and font size.
4. **Your account**, with your name and email, opens the user menu.

## Pages that are not in the sidebar

These pages have no row in the sidebar. Each one opens from the menu or tab next to what it
configures:

| Page | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| **Tenants** | **Open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar** and **choose “Manage workspaces…”**, its last row. See [Tenants](../workspace/tenants.mdx). |
| **SDK settings** | **Open Export studio** and **choose the “SDK & branding” tab**. The old `/ade/dashboard/sdk-settings` address redirects there. See [Export studio](../ship/export-studio.mdx). |
| **Profile** and **Linked accounts** | **Open your user menu**. See [Profile](../workspace/profile.mdx). |

<Screenshot id="workspace-menu" alt="The workspace switcher open: the filter, your workspaces, and Manage workspaces… as the last row" />

The command palette (<Kbd keys="Mod+K"/>) still finds every one of these pages under **Jump to**.
Old links and **G** shortcuts keep working.
