A **version** freezes your project's schemas and paths under a number. Publishing a version makes
it read-only and available to browse, export and MCP.

## Open a project's versions

**Go to Build → Projects and click your project.** Its **Timeline** lists every version, newest
first; **All**, **Drafts** and **Published** filter it.

<Screenshot id="versions" alt="A project's Versions page: the Timeline tab with compatibility and deprecation banners, project facts and the version list" />

## Cut a version

1. **Click “New version”.**
2. Under **Copy source**, keep **Copy from version** to start from the latest version, or choose
   **Create blank version**.
3. Under **Version**, keep **Auto-generate version** and pick a **Bump strategy** — minor is the
   default — or choose **Manual entry** and type a **Version ID** such as `1.1.0`.
4. Under **Describe the release**, write a **Message** (for example `First public release`).
   **External reference** and **Changelog** are optional.
5. **Click “Create version”.** The new draft appears at the top of the timeline.

## Publish it

1. On the draft's row, **click the “Publish” lock button**, or open the row's **Actions** menu and
   **choose “Publish”**.
2. Choose the **Visibility**:
   - **Private** (the default) — readers need an API key;
   - **Public** — anyone can read the spec without a key.
3. Enter a **Revision note** — one line that stays with this publish — and, if you like, a
   **Changelog**.
4. Check **Publish gates** on the right. **Review**, **Style guide**, **Breaking changes** and
   **Verification policy** each say whether they pass. While one blocks, the footer says why and
   **“Publish”** stays disabled: fix the finding (see [Lint and check
   quality](../build/lint-and-quality.md)), or tick **Force publish (ignore validation errors)**
   and give a reason.
5. **Click “Publish”.**

<Screenshot id="publish-dialog" alt="The Publish dialog: Private and Public visibility, revision note and changelog, and the publish gates panel" />

The version is now read-only. To change it, cut a new version — or choose **Unpublish** from its
**Actions** menu.

## With the CLI or the API

- REST: `POST /v1/versions/{tenant}/{project}` cuts a version; `POST
  /v1/versions/{tenant}/{project}/{version}/publish` publishes it. See [Cut a
  version](../ship/cut-a-version.md), [Publish a version](../ship/publish-a-version.md) and the [API
  reference](../reference/api-reference.md).
