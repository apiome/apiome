When you sign in and do not belong to any organization (a **tenant**) yet, Apiome shows a short
setup instead of the app. Progress is saved as you go, so you can leave and come back.

<Screenshot id="onboarding-welcome" alt="The onboarding welcome step: Let's set up your first tenant, with Check again and Set up your organization buttons" />

1. **Welcome.** If a teammate is about to invite you, wait for the invitation and **click “Check
   again”** — you go straight in once you are added. Otherwise **click “Set up your
   organization”**.
2. **Organization.** Enter an **Organization name** (for example `Acme, Inc.`). The **URL slug**
   is suggested from it (`acme-inc`); use lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. **Click
   “Continue”.**
3. **Review.** Check the name and slug. The Free plan card lists what you get: one tenant, one
   project and three versions per project, plus a sample project so your workspace is not empty.
   **Click “Create organization”.**
4. **Security.** Your organization now exists, and the last step, **Protect your account**, offers
   two-factor authentication (2FA): a six-digit code from Authy, Google Authenticator or any TOTP app
   each time you sign in.
   - To turn it on, **click “Set up two-factor”** and follow the same three steps as on your profile:
     confirm it's you, scan the QR code and **click “Confirm and enable”**, then save your backup codes
     and **click “Done”**. See [Turn it on](../workspace/profile.mdx#turn-it-on) for each step.
   - To leave it for later, **click “Skip for now”**. You can turn it on any time from
     **Profile → Security**, which reminds you while it is off.
5. **Done.** You are the organization's administrator. **Click “Go to your dashboard”** to open
   the launcher.

<Screenshot id="onboarding-two-factor" alt="The Protect your account step: Acme Corp is ready, the three steps of setting up two-factor, and Skip for now and Set up two-factor buttons" />

Progress is saved up to the **Review** step. Once the organization is created, leaving the setup
takes you to your dashboard; two-factor stays where you left it on your profile.

You can change the name and slug later in the tenant's settings.
