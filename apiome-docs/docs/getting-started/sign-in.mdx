administrator has enabled has its own button, and the email form sits underneath.

## Sign in

1. **Open Apiome** in your browser. On the local stack that is the apiome-ui address you started
   (see [Run Apiome locally](./run-locally.md)).
2. **Choose your provider** — for example **“Continue with GitHub”** — and approve the request on
   the provider's page. You come back to Apiome signed in.
3. Or sign in with email: if the form is folded away, **click “or use your email”**. Fill in
   **Email Address** and **Password**, then **click “Sign In”**.

   On the local stack, use `ada@example.com` with the password `apiome-dev`.

A wrong password shows **“Incorrect email or password.”** under the field — check both and try
again.

## Pass the two-factor check

If your account has two-factor authentication, Apiome asks for a code before it lets you in.

1. With an authenticator app, enter the six-digit code from the app in **Authentication code**.
2. With email, **click “Send email code”**, then enter the code from your inbox in **Email code**.
   The **Authenticator** and **Email code** tabs switch between the two when both are set up.
3. **Click “Verify and continue”.**

**“Back to sign in”** returns to the first page without signing you in.

## Create an account

**With a provider.** Click your provider's button on the sign-in page. The first time, Apiome asks
you to **finish setting up your account**:

1. Enter **Your name**, **Organization name** and **Organization URL slug** — the slug is
   suggested from the name.
2. **Click “Create account”.** Your organization is created on the Free plan and you land on the
   launcher.

**With email.** Under the form, **click “Create one”**, then fill in **Full Name**, **Email
Address** and **Password** and **click “Create Account”**. This sends a sign-up request; the page
says what happens next. Once you can sign in, Apiome takes you through
[first-tenant onboarding](./onboarding.mdx) if you are not part of an organization yet.
