Every list page in the Control Panel is built the same way, so the first row of data sits near the
top of the screen. Nothing was taken away to make room: each status, number, note and control is
still on the page, one click away at most. This page shows where each one lives, so you know where
to look on any list.

<Screenshot id="catalog" alt="The Catalog in Table view: the facts line under the header, then one card with the filters, view chips and items" />

From top to bottom, a list page has:

1. **The header** — the page title, its description, and a few buttons for the whole page.
2. **One status row or one summary line** — what is worth knowing about the page right now.
3. **A first-visit note**, only until you dismiss it.
4. **The list** — one toolbar, then the rows.

## The header

The header holds at most one main button (drawn last, for example **“New version”**) and two other
buttons. Anything else is under **More actions** (`⋯`), between them. An action that you cannot use
yet stays in its menu, dimmed, and says why when you hover over it or move to it with the keyboard.

Pickers live next to what they pick. On [Versions](../build/versions.mdx), for example, the last
step of the breadcrumb is the project's name: **click it** and type in **Jump to project** to move to
another project.

Longer explanations are links in the page description, such as **“How the catalog works”** on the
[Catalog](../bring-in/catalog.mdx#how-the-catalog-works), **“How resolution works”** on [Primitives &
types](../build/primitives-and-types.mdx) and **“Where these warnings come from”** on the [Sunset
timeline](../ship/sunset-timeline.mdx). **Click one** to open the explanation over the page.

## Status: one row of chips

When a page has several things to say about the same object, they are chips in one row instead of
a stack of banners. On [Versions](../build/versions.mdx#timeline) the chips are **Compatible** (or
**Breaking changes**), **What’s new** and any **deprecated** version.

<Screenshot id="versions" alt="The Versions page, Timeline tab: the project name as the last breadcrumb, Compare, Import, More actions and New version, the Status strip, the Project facts card, and the versions as a timeline under its one filter toolbar" caption="Captured from a development build, which also draws git-like controls marked gitlike; production builds hide them." />

- The most urgent chip opens its detail on its own: errors first, then warnings, then news, then
  good news.
- **Click a chip** to open its detail underneath. Only one detail is open at a time, and it keeps
  the actions the old banner had, such as **“View report”** or **“Sunset timeline”**.
- **Click the open chip**, **click “Collapse”** or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to hide the detail.
- A chip with nothing to say is not drawn. With nothing to report, the row is not drawn at all.

## Numbers: one summary line

A page's figures are one line under the header, for example *3 items · 3 formats · avg quality
B · 83*. A figure that needs your attention is a coloured chip, and **clicking it** takes you to what
it counts.

- **Click “Show stats”** at the end of the line to open the full figures as cards, in place. The
  button then reads **“Hide stats”**. The page remembers your choice on this device.
- A figure about something that is not there yet is a **Coming next** line instead of a card with a
  button that does nothing. **Click “Follow in What’s new”** to read the release notes, where it is
  announced when it ships.

| Page | The line counts | Chips that open something |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Catalog](../bring-in/catalog.mdx) | Items, formats, average quality, converted to OpenAPI, active and disabled | — |
| [Repositories](../bring-in/repositories.mdx) | Repositories, files indexed, imports (30d), last scan, needing attention | *1 stale · 1 failed* opens **Refresh activity** |
| A repository's page | Files, importable, branches, imports (30d), last scan | — |
| [Primitives & types](../build/primitives-and-types.mdx) | Core system types, tenant types, imported schemas, properties bound, unresolved `$ref` | Unresolved `$ref` opens the **Resolver** tab |
| [Members](../workspace/members.mdx#the-summary-line-and-member-seats) | Members, active, pending, suspended, seats used and the plan | — (a seat bar appears from 80 %) |
| [Lint posture](../govern/lint-posture.mdx#the-summary) | Unwaived security errors, missing coverage, new findings, active waivers, then a chip per grade | Each of the four figures filters the queue |

## Notes: shown once

A page that needs explaining shows a note on your first visit. **Click “Learn more”** to read the
full explanation, or **“Got it”** to hide the note on this device.

Hiding a note never hides what it says. The same text stays one click away, from a link in the page
description — on the Catalog, **“How the catalog works”** and, in the [Import
wizard](../bring-in/import-wizard.mdx), **“Which formats can I import?”**.

## The list: one toolbar

Each list has exactly one toolbar, on top of its rows. Everything that narrows or orders the list is
there: search, the filter menus, the state chips with their counts, the sort, and the view switch
(such as **Cards** or **Table**). On [Versions](../build/versions.mdx#filter-the-list):

- dates are one **“Any time”** button that opens **Created between**, with **Last 7 days**, **Last
  30 days** and **This quarter**;
- people are one **“All authors”** list;
- **“Clear filters (n)”** appears while any filter is on, and **clicking it** shows every row again.

Filters that do not fit the row sit behind one **“Filters”** button, which counts how many are set,
for example **Filters (2)**. Filters are kept in the page address, so you can **copy the link** to
share a filtered list, and a reload keeps them.

## What a page remembers

| Choice | Remembered | Until |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which status chip is open | Per project, in this tab | You close the tab |
| **Show stats** / **Hide stats** | Per page, on this device | You change it, or clear the site's data |
| **Got it** on a note | Per note, on this device | You clear the site's data; the note then shows again |
| Filters and sort | In the page address | You change them or open the page without them |
| **Cards** / **Table**, **Timeline** / **Table** | Per page, on this device | You change it |
