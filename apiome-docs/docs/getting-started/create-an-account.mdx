You can create an Apiome account with a sign-in provider (GitHub, GitLab, Microsoft or Google, when
your installation offers them) or with an email address and a password. Either way, you then name
your first organization and land in the app.

**Go to `/signup`**, or **click “Create one”** under the sign-in form. The page lists every sign-in
provider your installation offers, then the email form.

<Screenshot id="signup" alt="Create your account: Sign up with GitHub and Sign up with Google, a note that sign-up is open to addresses at northwind.io, then the email form and Already have an account? Sign in" />

If your installation limits sign-up to some email domains, the page says which — for example *Sign-up
is open to addresses at northwind.io.*

## With a provider

On **Create your account**, **click “Sign up with …”** for your provider. The first time, Apiome
asks you to **finish setting up your account**:

1. Enter **Your name**, **Organization name** and **Organization URL slug** — the slug is
   suggested from the name.
2. **Click “Create account”.** Your organization is created on the Free plan and you land on the
   launcher.

## With an email address

1. On **Create your account**, under **or use your email**, fill in **Full name**, **Email
   address** and **Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters, an uppercase and a
   lowercase letter, and a number or special character — the hint under the field lists them.
   Use a permanent address: addresses from disposable email services are refused.
2. If your installation shows a **security check** above the button, complete it — **Create
   account** stays switched off until it passes.
3. **Click “Create account”.**

<Screenshot id="signup-form" alt="Create your account: the Full name, Email address and Password fields filled in for Ada Lovelace, with the password rules under the password field and the Create account button" />

Apiome shows **Check your email** and sends a message to the address you entered.

<Screenshot id="signup-check-email" alt="Check your email: we sent a link to ada@example.com, with Resend email and Use a different email buttons" />

4. Open the email **Confirm your email for Apiome** and **click “Confirm email”**.
5. On **Confirm your email**, enter the **Password** you chose in step 2 and **click “Confirm and
   sign in”**.

<Screenshot id="signup-verify-confirm" alt="Confirm your email: a Password field and the Confirm and sign in button" />

The page says **Email confirmed** and signs you in.

<Screenshot id="signup-verified" alt="Email confirmed: your account is ready, signing you in" />

6. Apiome then walks you through [setting up your first organization](./onboarding.mdx).

You cannot sign in with the new account until you confirm the email address.

### If the email does not arrive

- Check your spam folder.
- On **Check your email**, **click “Resend email”** for a new link. Each new email replaces the
  link in the previous one, so use the newest. You can ask for a few emails an hour; after that,
  wait and try again.
- **Click “Use a different email”** if you mistyped the address.

### If the link does not work

A confirmation link works **once** and expires after **24 hours**. Opening it again, or after it
expires, shows why instead of signing you in:

| Page title | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **This link has expired** | More than 24 hours passed. Ask for a new link |
| **This link was already used** | You already confirmed your address. **Click “Sign in”**, or ask for a new link if you did not |
| **This link does not work** | The link is incomplete, or a newer email replaced it. Use the newest email, or ask for a new link |

If **Confirm your email** says *That is not the password chosen for this sign-up*, the link belongs
to a different sign-up for your address — someone may have signed up with it before you, or you
signed up twice with different passwords. Use the link in your newest email, or sign up again with
your own password; that sends a link only your password can confirm. Whoever confirms an address
sets its password, and every other link and session for the account stops working.

To get a new link, enter your **Email address** and **click “Resend email”**.

<Screenshot id="signup-verify-expired" alt="This link has expired: an Email address field, the Resend email button and a Sign in link" />

### If the address already has an account

Apiome shows **Check your email** as usual — the page never says whether an address is already
registered. The email that arrives says **You already have an Apiome account** and links to the
sign-in page. Sign in with your password or the provider you used before.

### If email sign-up is not offered

If **Create your account** shows *Sign-up with an email address is not available on this server*,
your installation has no email sender set up. Create your account with a provider, or ask your
administrator. Administrators turn email sign-up on in
[Authentication providers](../admin/auth-providers.mdx#email-sign-up).

## When you cannot create an account

Your administrator decides who may sign up. When sign-up is **closed** or **by invitation**, the page
says so instead of offering buttons and a form, and links you to sign in:

<Screenshot id="signup-closed" alt="Create your account with Sign-up is closed: new account sign-ups are currently disabled on this server, and Already have an account? Sign in" />

- **Sign-up is closed** — ask your administrator for an account.
- **Sign-up is by invitation** — ask a workspace administrator to set up your account and invite
  you. Invitations go to existing accounts, so there is no invitation link to enter here.

If sign-up is open but the installation does not accept yours, the page says why instead of
creating an account:

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *New account sign-ups are currently disabled on this server.* | Sign-up is closed | Ask your administrator for an account |
| *New accounts on this server are by invitation only.* | Only invited people get in | Ask a workspace administrator to set up your account and invite you |
| *New accounts on this server are limited to approved email domains…* | Your email domain is not on the list | Sign up with your work email address |
| *Too many sign-up attempts.* | Too many tries from your network or for your address in the last hour | Wait an hour, then try again |
| *Too many sign-up attempts from your network.* | Too many provider sign-ups started from your network in the last minute | Wait a minute, then try again |
| *Addresses from disposable email services cannot be used to sign up.* | Your address is at a throwaway email service | Sign up with a permanent address, such as your work email |
| *We could not confirm the security check.* | The security check above **Create account** did not pass or expired | Complete the check again, then **click “Create account”** |

None of this affects an account you already have — sign in as usual. Administrators set this on
[System settings](../admin/auth-providers.mdx#sign-up-policy), and see refused attempts there under
[**Recent blocked sign-ups**](../admin/auth-providers.mdx#abuse-protection).
