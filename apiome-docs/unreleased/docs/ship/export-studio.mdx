The **Export studio** converts one version — or one catalog item — into another format, and shows
you what the conversion keeps and loses **before** you generate it. Five steps run across the top:
**Source**, **Target**, **Options**, **Verify** and **Review & Generate**. **Back** and the step's
main button at the foot move between them.

<Screenshot id="export-studio" alt="Export studio on the Target step: target cards grouped by family, each with its fidelity tier" />

Two tabs sit under the header: **Export**, the steps below, and **SDK & branding**, where the
[SDK settings](./sdk-settings.mdx) for generated packages live. Switching tabs keeps your source,
target, options and step, so **Export** brings you back to where you were.

## Open the studio

The studio always exports a specific source, so open it from one:

- **A version** — in a version's **Export** panel (*Convert to any format*), **click “Export this
  version”**, a target chip, or **Re-run in Studio** on a recent export. In the **Export** dialog,
  **“Open in Export Studio”** carries your target and options across.
- **A project** — on **Versions**, **click “Export studio”** on the project's facts card, or choose
  **Export studio** in the header's **⋯** menu. Both open the studio on the project's head version.
- **A catalog item** — choose **Export to another format…** in the item's menu on the
  [Catalog](../bring-in/catalog.mdx), or in the **⋯** menu on the item's page. (The menu's **Export** row
  and the item page's **“Export”** button open the in-place **Export** dialog.) Exporting a catalog item produces a file; it
  never turns the item into a project.

Opened without a source — from **Export studio** in the rail or the command palette — the
**Source** step lets you choose one:

1. **Click “Catalog items”** and type in the search box to filter by name, slug, format or
   protocol. **Click an item** to export it.
2. Or **click “Projects”**, **click a project**, then **click a version** — or **“Latest version”**.
   Catalog items are not listed here: they stay catalog items until you convert them.

The studio then opens on that source at **2. Target**, and the address is a link you can share.
The **SDK & branding** tab is still one click away.

**“Copy link”** in the header copies a link that reopens the same source, target, options and step.
Credentials are never included in it; a link with a value that cannot be used opens with a yellow
notice saying what was ignored.

## 1. Source

The source's name, its version and how many export targets are available. A catalog item also shows
its format and counts of services, operations, types and channels. **Click “Choose target”.**

<Screenshot id="export-studio-source" alt="Export studio on the Source step: Pet Store API, version 1.2.0, ten export targets" />

## 2. Target

**Choose a target format.** Cards are grouped — **REST & HTTP**, **API clients & collections**,
**RPC**, **Events**, **Data schema & graph**, **Agents & tools**, **Other targets** — and each carries
a **fidelity tier** computed for *this* source. **API clients & collections** holds the targets that
export saved requests rather than a contract: Postman, Hoppscotch, Thunder Client and the `.http`
request file (see [Export to an API client](./export-to-an-api-client.md)).

| Tier | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `lossless` | Carries the source cleanly. |
| `lossy` | Drops or approximates some constructs. |
| `types-only` | Only the schemas survive; operations and channels are dropped. |

A readiness badge (**ready**, **check first**, **blocked**, **unavailable**) appears when the
pre-flight check has run; blocked and unavailable cards cannot be chosen and say why. Toggle
**Sorted by readiness** / **Sorted by name** above the grid. How tiers and readiness are worked out
is in [Understand export fidelity](./export-fidelity.md).

When the source was imported in another format, the studio hides the same-format target and offers
**Original source** instead: **“Download original”** gives you the file unchanged.

Select a card — the foot reads *Exporting to gRPC / Protobuf* with its tier and **% preserved** — and
**click “Continue”**.

### Several targets

To export the source to more than one format at once, **tick “Several targets”** above the cards.
The cards become a checklist, each with its tier for this source.

1. **Tick two or more targets.** **“Continue”** stays disabled until you have.
2. **Click “Continue”.** The studio skips **3. Options**: every target exports with its default
   options.
3. On **4. Verify**, **click “Verify all targets”**. One dry run measures every target; a table lists
   each target's tier and **% preserved**, and the line above it names the lowest fidelity. Nothing
   is generated yet. **Click “Continue to review”.**

   <Screenshot id="export-studio-multi-verify" alt="Verify with several targets: OpenAPI 3.1 lossless, GraphQL SDL and Protocol Buffers lossy, lowest fidelity 64% preserved" />

4. On **5. Review & Generate**, **click “Generate”**, then **“Download .zip”**. The archive has a
   folder per target and a `manifest.json`.

Ticking or unticking a target clears the verification, so run it again. Untick **Several targets**
to go back to choosing one card.

### Gateway targets

**Kong Declarative Config** and **Gateway API** write a gateway's own format. A gateway config's
**Push back** ([Environments](../bring-in/connectors.mdx#push-a-catalog-item-back-to-the-gateway)) opens the
studio on the gateway's target at **4. Verify**, so the fidelity report is the first thing you see;
the target is offered even when the catalog item was imported from that same gateway. A gateway
configuration carries no request/response schemas, so schemas are reported as dropped. AWS API
Gateway, Azure API Management and Apigee targets are still to come (FMT-7.7).

<Screenshot id="export-studio-gateway" alt="Pushing a catalog item back to Kong: the fidelity report for Kong Declarative Config before export" />

## 3. Options

The target's options: checkboxes, a row of choices, or text fields. Errors appear under the field,
for example *… is required.* A target with no options says so. **Click “Continue”.**

## 4. Verify

**Click “Run verification”** (or turn on **Verify automatically**). Fidelity, validation and lint run
in one pass; nothing is generated or stored yet.

<Screenshot id="export-verify" alt="Verify the gRPC / Protobuf conversion: Lossy — acknowledge to continue, 64% preserved, and the acknowledgement checkbox" />

The banner gives the verdict:

| Verdict | To continue |
| --- | --- |
| **Clean** | Nothing — **“Continue to review”** is enabled. |
| **Lossy — acknowledge to continue** | Tick *I understand this conversion is lossy and want to export anyway.* |
| **Severe — acknowledge to continue** | Type `export produces a types-only artifact` in the box. |
| **Invalid — export blocked** | Fix the source or choose another target; this export cannot be generated. |

The **Fidelity**, **Validation** and **Lint** tabs carry counts. **Fidelity** shows the % preserved
and what was dropped, approximated or synthesized; **Show per-construct report** opens the
projection map, where each construct links to its reason. **Lint** lints the *emitted* file, not the
source. Changing the target or an option clears the verdict and the acknowledgement — run it again.

## 5. Review & Generate

The summary lists the **Source**, the **Target** (tier and % preserved) and the **Options** you
changed. **Click “Generate”.**

<Screenshot id="export-studio-job" alt="Generating Protocol Buffers: running at 62%, the Emit stage active, a warning and Cancel" />

The job moves through **Load source**, **Analyze fidelity**, **Emit**, **Validate** and **Package**,
and is **QUEUED**, **RUNNING**, **COMPLETED**, **FAILED** or **CANCELED**. **“Cancel”** stops it while
it runs; **“Generate again”** restarts a canceled one.

When it completes:

- **One file** — read it in the viewer, then **“Download *file-name*”** or **“Download .zip”**.
- **A bundle** — browse the files in the tree, then **“Download .zip”**.
- **Artifact entities**, **Problems**, the mapping graph and **What … loses, worst first** explain
  the result; **“Run round-trip check”** reads the file back and compares it with the source.
- For an OpenAPI target, **Mock server test drive** serves the file as a live API — see
  [Mock try-out](./mock-try-out.mdx#test-drive-an-export).

### When generation fails

A failure card names the stage and offers the next step — for example *The emitted artifact failed
validation* with **“Review in Verify”**, or *Target not available* with **“Choose a different
target”**. **“Retry export”** is offered whenever the job can be retried.

<Screenshot id="export-studio-failure" alt="A failed job: the emitted artifact failed validation, with the validator's message and Review in Verify" />

If a workspace quality policy blocks delivery, *Delivery blocked by quality policy* lists the reasons
— **Artifact validation**, **Source quality**, **Conversion fidelity** or **Tenant policy** — and how
to request a waiver.

<Screenshot id="export-studio-gate" alt="Delivery blocked by quality policy: conversion fidelity and source quality reasons, and the waiver request" />

## Export several catalog items at once

On the [Catalog](../bring-in/catalog.mdx), tick the items you want, then **click “Export *n*…”** in
the bulk bar.

1. **Choose a target.** The list shows the targets the first ticked item can export to.
2. **Click “Generate”.** One job exports every item, each at its latest version.
3. A table lists each item with its outcome: the fidelity tier, or why it failed. An item that fails
   does not stop the others.
4. **Click “Download .zip”.** Each item's files sit in a folder named after the item.

<Screenshot id="catalog-bulk-export" alt="Export 3 catalog items to OpenAPI 3.1: two completed with their tiers, one failed with its reason" />

One export takes at most 100 items. The studio is not involved, and no item becomes a project.

**Recent export jobs**, under every step, lists earlier jobs with their state, target and progress.

## With the CLI or the API

- CLI: `apiome export targets`, `apiome export preflight`, `apiome export evidence --target <format>`
  and the per-format commands (`apiome export openapi`, `asyncapi`, `grpc`, `graphql`, `avro`) — see
  [Export a spec](./export-a-spec.md) and the [CLI quick-start](../reference/cli-quickstart.md).
  `apiome export <format> --targets …` and `--items …` run the several-targets and several-items
  jobs — see [From the CLI: several formats or several items](./export-a-spec.md#from-the-cli-several-formats-or-several-items).
- REST: `GET /v1/export/{tenant}/targets`, `POST /v1/export/{tenant}/preview` and
  `POST /v1/export/{tenant}/jobs` — see
  [Understand export fidelity](./export-fidelity.md#rest-endpoints) and the
  [API reference](../reference/api-reference.md).
