## Consumers

<Route path="/ade/dashboard/projects/[projectId]/consumers" />

**Consumers** lists the teams and services that depend on the API, and which operations and fields
each one uses. **“Register consumer”** adds one by hand; **“Import Pact file”** adds one from a
consumer contract.

### Bindings and traffic

A consumer's **contract** says what it declares it uses. Its **bindings** let the
[usage index](../govern/runtime-usage.mdx#attribute-traffic-to-consumers) recognise its live calls,
so you can see what it really calls next to what it declares.

1. On **Consumers**, open a consumer's row menu (**⋯**) and choose **Bindings and traffic**.
2. Under **Bindings**, pick the **Kind**: **Header**, **API key**, **JWT claim** or **User agent**.
3. Enter the name: the header (`x-client-id`), the header carrying the key (`x-api-key`), the claim
   (`azp`), or a user-agent pattern such as `billing-service/*`.
4. For every kind except **User agent**, enter the **Value** this consumer sends. It is hashed
   before it is stored and is never shown again.
5. **Click “Add binding”.** Calls ingested from now on are attributed to the consumer.

<Screenshot id="project-consumers-bindings" alt="The Bindings and traffic drawer for Billing Service: an API key and a user-agent binding with when each last matched, the add-binding form, and the declared-vs-observed lists with an Add to contract button" />

Each binding shows its name and when it last matched, never its value. The trash button deletes
one: it stops matching from the next batch, and calls it already attributed stay with the consumer.
Until a consumer has a binding that matches, its calls are counted as **anonymous**.

**Declared vs observed** covers the last 30 days:

- **Used, undeclared** lists operations the consumer calls but does not declare. **Click “Add to
  contract”** to store a new contract revision with that operation added to what is already
  declared.
- **Declared and used** lists declared operations with calls, with the count and the last day seen.
- **Declared, unused** lists declared operations with no calls in the window.

Adding and deleting bindings needs the same permissions as registering and retiring consumers.

A client that calls the API but isn't registered yet is listed as a **suggested consumer** on the
project's **Dependencies** tab. **“Register consumer”** there registers it together with its
binding. See [Suggested consumers](../govern/dependency-graph.mdx#suggested-consumers).

## Owner

A project can have an owner team. CODEOWNERS rules for the spec's path and the document's own
`x-apiome-owner` extension *suggest* one, and a suggestion becomes the owner only when someone
confirms it.

**In a list**, the **Owner** column (on cards, a line under the summary) names the team. A
*suggested* owner carries a **Suggested** badge saying where it came from — *Gateway tag*,
*CODEOWNERS*, *Spec* — and a **“Confirm”** button: **click “Confirm”** to make it the owner; the
row updates in place. The team's name opens its [team page](../workspace/teams.mdx#team-pages).

**Owner** in the toolbar shows one team's projects (kept in the address as `?team=`). In **Table**
view, tick projects and use **Assign owner** in the bar at the bottom of the page to give them all
the same owner.

On a project's **Consumers**, the **Owner** column is the consumer's owner team and **Contact** is
the free-text owner it was registered with; **Owner** in the toolbar filters by team there too. See
[Teams → Ownership](../workspace/teams.mdx#ownership).
