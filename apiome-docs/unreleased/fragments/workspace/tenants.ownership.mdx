### Ownership

Two settings for entities that have no confirmed [owner team](./teams.mdx#ownership):

<Screenshot id="tenant-ownership-policy" alt="The Ownership section: Platform as the catch-all team and Require an owner to publish switched on" />

- **Catch-all team** — findings on anything without a confirmed owner also go to this team's
  members and its chat channel (they are still listed in the **Unowned findings** report). Choose
  **None — the Unowned findings report only** to turn it off. If the chosen team is later deleted,
  the section warns that unowned findings reach nobody.
- **Require an owner to publish** — a project with no confirmed owner team cannot publish a version
  (reason `no-owner`) unless it is force-published with a reason. The publish dialog shows this gate
  whether it is on or off.

1. Choose the **Catch-all team** and switch **Require an owner to publish** on or off.
2. **Click “Save changes”.** The change is recorded in the access audit as
   `ownership_policy.update`.

Changing these needs `teams:edit` (Owner and Admin); anyone with `teams:view` sees them read-only,
and the [Teams](./teams.mdx) page shows them too.
