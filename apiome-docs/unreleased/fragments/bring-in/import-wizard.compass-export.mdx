### Compass export

Moving off Atlassian Compass? Upload its export, and its components and owners arrive as
suggestions to review. See [Migrate from Backstage or Compass](./migrate-from-backstage-or-compass.mdx)
for how each field maps.

1. **Click “Import to catalog”**, then **click “Compass export”**.
2. **Click “Choose the Compass export (.json)”** and pick the JSON file (up to 5 MB).
3. **Click “Import export”.** The wizard jumps to **Import** and shows the import report: **Teams**,
   **Components** and **Unmapped fields**.
4. **Click “Create team”**, **“Register component”** or **“Apply to …”** on each suggestion you want,
   or **“Dismiss”**. You can also come back later: **Import reports** on the Catalog lists the report.
