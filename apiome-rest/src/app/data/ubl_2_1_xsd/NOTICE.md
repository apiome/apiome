# OASIS Universal Business Language (UBL) 2.1 schemas

The files in this folder are the XML Schemas of **OASIS Universal Business Language (UBL) 2.1**, OASIS
Standard, 04 November 2013, copied **unmodified** from the OASIS distribution:

    http://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.1/UBL-2.1.zip   (xsd/common/UBL-CommonBasicComponents-2.1.xsd,
                                                              UBL-QualifiedDataTypes-2.1.xsd,
                                                              UBL-UnqualifiedDataTypes-2.1.xsd,
                                                              CCTS_CCT_SchemaModule-2.1.xsd)

These are the schemas a UBL document schema written by Apiome imports (the basic components and the data types
they use); the document, aggregate, extension and signature schemas of the distribution are not needed and are not
included.

One of them, `common/CCTS_CCT_SchemaModule-2.1.xsd` (the Core Component Type Schema Module), is UN/CEFACT's and
ships in the OASIS distribution under UN/CEFACT's notice, which is in the file itself: "Copyright (C) UN/CEFACT
(2006). All Rights Reserved." — it may be copied and distributed unmodified with that notice.

Apiome uses them, offline, to validate the UBL and Peppol profiles of the XSD export (MFX-24.6, #4346:
``app.ubl_xsd_profile``). The OASIS notice below applies to them and is reproduced here as it requires.
