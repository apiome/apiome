First visit

The explainer shows once per device, then lives behind “How the catalog works”
Catalog items are non-publishable. They come from formats that don’t map 1:1 to OpenAPI (gRPC, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, OData, WSDL…). They are cataloged, versioned, diffed & linted like Projects — the path to a publishable spec is Convert to OpenAPI. OpenAPI/Swagger imports land in Projects.

How the catalog works
Everything the standing banner and the formats row used to say.
Catalog items are stored as-is
gRPC, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, OData, WSDL, Avro, RAML, Smithy, TypeSpec, API Blueprint and 30+ more are kept in their original format, versioned, diffed and linted like Projects.
They are not publishable until converted
The one path to a publishable spec is Convert to OpenAPI, with a fidelity preview before you commit.
OpenAPI and Swagger go to Projects
Importing one of those never creates a catalog item — it lands in Projects directly.
