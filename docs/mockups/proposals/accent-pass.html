Colour & accent pass — before and after

The shipped redesign is calm, consistent and grey. This round spends colour where the eye needs an anchor — one hue per area in the rail and the page header, the hexagon on every record, azure for everything selected, tone on the numbers, and a lift on everything you can click — without changing a route, a token value, a theme or a line of copy. Drag the handle on each pair; press B on any proposal page to see the shipped page it replaces.
