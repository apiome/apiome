The same, from anywhere

apiome pact retention set \
  --main-branch-latest 5 \
  --other-branch-latest 1 \
  --other-branch-days 90 \
  --keep-tag keep \
  --nightly \
  --restore-days 30
  • Reads PACT_BROKER_BASE_URL and PACT_BROKER_TOKEN, or the workspace you signed in to with apiome login.

Replace {tenant} with your workspace’s handle in the REST path. apiome pact retention preview takes the same flags.
