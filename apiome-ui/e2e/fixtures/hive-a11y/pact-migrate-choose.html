The same, from anywhere

apiome pact migrate \
  --from https://acme.pactflow.io \
  --token "$PACT_SOURCE_BROKER_TOKEN"
  • Reads PACT_BROKER_BASE_URL and PACT_BROKER_TOKEN, or the workspace you signed in to with apiome login.

Not carried by the command

  • Only the versions clean-up would keep is not carried by the command (REST carries it): apiome pact migrate copies everything the source returns; this choice is the wizard’s and the REST request’s.

The command keeps its progress in a state file (--state) on your machine; the wizard keeps it on the server, under Past migrations. Replace {tenant} with your workspace’s handle in the REST path.
