The same, from anywhere

apiome pact create-environment \
  --name production-eu \
  --display-name 'Production EU' \
  --production \
  --contract-required \
  --contact-name 'Platform team' \
  --contact-email-address platform@acme.dev
  • Reads PACT_BROKER_BASE_URL and PACT_BROKER_TOKEN, or the workspace you signed in to with apiome login.

Replace {tenant} with your workspace’s handle in the REST path.
